# Accessibility QA

Apply this checklist to future raw studies before handoff:

- Semantic structure and heading order are valid.
- Interactive controls are keyboard operable.
- Focus states are visible and ordered logically.
- Controls have accessible names and state communication.
- Images and media slots define appropriate alternative-text behavior.
- Text and meaningful UI states meet applicable contrast requirements.
- Content remains usable with zoom and increased text size.
- Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Validation results and unresolved exceptions are recorded in the batch document.

No accessibility review is performed during structure-only setup because no studies exist.
