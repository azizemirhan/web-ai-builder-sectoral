A clearer brief for your solar project

A useful project brief makes room for both ambition and uncertainty. Start by describing what you want to understand, then separate the information you have from the questions still open.

Start with the question

Describe the decision the project should help you make. A focused question gives the first conversation a direction, while leaving space to discuss different approaches. Keep desired outcomes distinct from promises about what a system will achieve.

Describe the site

Bring together the location, available background information and the people who can clarify it. Label documents by their source and date. Where information is missing, record the gap rather than replacing it with an assumption.

Keep unknowns visible

A brief can evolve. Note which questions need further discussion, who will follow them up and what would help resolve them. Revisit those questions as the project scope becomes clearer.
