## Research Metadata and Scope

Sources: user continuation request, section README, sector theme contract,
preceding studies, authoring standard and media policy. Research date: 2026-09-15.
External research: NONE. Document-metaphor justification: NONE.

Each study presents one project in depth: initial brief, decisions and approach,
delivery responsibilities, and outcomes with their evidential limits. Project name,
location, discipline, period, narrative and results are explicit placeholders.
No clients, ownership, capacities, performance figures or testimonials are invented.
Partner contributions must be distinguished from the organisation's work.

## Interaction and Responsive Decisions

One labelled section with H2; factual metadata uses a definition list. One article
contains four H3 narrative groups. Native details reveals evidence and permission
notes without JavaScript. The service exploration link opens the existing S23 study
with the same variant number. It offers further exploration, without claiming that
the example planning service was involved in an unspecified project. Production
route and service attribution mapping belong to ingestion.

Desktop compositions vary media scale, arrangement and narrative placement. Mobile
reflow preserves all chapters and photographs in normal flow, with no horizontal
gallery or hidden content. Scoped CSS provides readable text measures, border-box
descendants, visible focus, 44px interaction targets and reduced-motion treatment.
No raw scripts or remote dependencies. S23 and S24 both use shape B.
