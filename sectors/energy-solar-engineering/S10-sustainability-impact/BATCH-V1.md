## Research Metadata and Scope

Sources: user continuation request, section README, sector brief,
../ENERGY-THEME-CONTRACT.md, preceding S08 and S09 studies, authoring standard
and media policy. Research date: 2026-09-15. External research: NONE.
Document-metaphor justification: NONE.

Copy frames project boundaries, resource choices, equipment life and evidence as
discussion topics. It does not claim environmental performance for a provider.
No emissions reductions, savings, circularity rates, carbon neutrality, lifecycle
results or project ownership are invented. The disclosure reserves an approved
impact statement with boundary, baseline, period, method, source and limitations.
Supply verified evidence before publication. Quantitative impact statistics remain
the role of S11; this section introduces context and the sustainability approach.

Explore the evidence opens native details content locally. No fake report download,
dead links, pretend submissions, global headers or footers, raw scripts or remote
runtime dependencies.

## Composition and Responsive Decisions

The five established themes remain consistent while narrative placement, photograph
proportions and disclosure emphasis vary. All variants use two anchored media areas,
shape B, breaking the A sequence from S08 and S09. No gauges, dashboards or technical
document metaphors are used.

Below 850px, the site image, narrative and lifecycle image stack in DOM order.
Phone media heights are 300px and 250px. Expanded content stays in normal flow.
A labelled H2 introduces a labelled article with an H3. Scoped CSS includes
border-box descendants, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion treatment and
control targets at least 44px tall.

## Media

Two reserved photographs per study, ten total. The first depicts the actual site
context; the second depicts relevant equipment maintenance or lifecycle activity.
Neither image is evidence of an environmental outcome by itself. Visible subject
captions and accessible reserved-image labels identify each slot. Licensing,
provenance and final descriptive alt text are required before ingestion. Solid
theme-colour fields preserve the intended composition without fabricated imagery.
