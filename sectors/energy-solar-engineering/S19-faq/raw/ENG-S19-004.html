Frequently asked questions

A little more clarity. Before your next step.

Explore the questions that help frame an energy project, from the first enquiry to handover. Use these answers to prepare a conversation about your site, then confirm the details that apply to your project.

Planning your project

What information should I prepare for a project enquiry?

Describe the site location, project goals and preferred timing. Include the type of support you need and any known site constraints.

Can you assess whether my site is suitable?

[Confirmed assessment scope, required site information, access arrangements and any charges. Explain how suitability is reviewed before work is agreed.]

Which locations and project types do you support?

[Verified service areas and eligible project types. Identify any location restrictions and the contact route for confirming coverage.]

What determines the proposed project scope and quotation?

[Approved quotation process, information requirements, inclusions, exclusions and validity period. Clarify when a site review or further information is needed.]

How are installation dates and project milestones agreed?

[Confirmed scheduling process and dependencies. Explain how access, approvals, equipment availability and project scope affect dates without promising a fixed timeline.]

What happens after installation and project handover are complete?

[Verified handover documentation, support arrangements, maintenance options and warranty terms. Identify the responsible contact and applicable service limitations.]
