## Research Metadata and Scope

Sources: user continuation request; section README; sector brief;
../ENERGY-THEME-CONTRACT.md; preceding S01 studies; authoring standard and media
policy. Research date: 2026-09-14. External research: NONE.
Document-metaphor justification: NONE.

Solar planning, energy storage and engineering support are exploratory content
categories. Descriptions frame questions a visitor could discuss, not verified
offerings of an invented provider. The introduction explicitly asks visitors to
confirm availability and scope. Each disclosure reserves the provider-specific
services, deliverables, eligibility, compatibility or professional responsibilities.
These must be verified before publication. No prices, capacity, savings, ROI,
certification, project evidence or performance guarantees are invented.

Each View service scope control opens its own native details content. Accessible
names include the service title to distinguish the repeated visible labels.
There are no dead links, pretend submissions or references to unauthored detail
pages. The interaction is complete locally without JavaScript.

## Composition and Responsive Decisions

001 offers three equal cards. 002 uses open horizontal editorial rows. 003 gives
solar a full-width featured module above two supporting cards. 004 pairs visitor
questions with prominent blue disclosure controls. 005 offsets three differently
weighted cards and gives solar a curved lime field. These differences remain
visible with the same descriptions; no technical document metaphor is used.

Below 850px, all layouts become one column in solar, storage and engineering order.
Expanded content stays in normal flow. Each service remains a labelled article
with an H3 under the single section H2. No media is needed to explain these three
areas; type and colour carry the service index. No global header, footer or forms.

S01: B / B / B / C / B. S02: A / A / A / A / A. There is no consecutive grid run
beyond this first A section. Subsequent sections should continue the theme contract
and review page rhythm. Scoped CSS includes explicit border-box sizing, visible
focus, reduced-motion treatment and control targets of at least 44px.
