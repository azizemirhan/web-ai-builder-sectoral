# Section Role

What clients said — and what the reader may do about it.

# The Governing Idea

Testimonials are the least trusted element on any professional-services site, and the reason is
structural. The reader cannot verify a sentence in quotation marks, and knows the firm chose which
sentences to print.

The usual fix is an attribution — a name, a title, a company, a headshot. That fix is **not
available here and would not have worked anyway.** Not available, because
`../CONSULTING-THEME-CONTRACT.md` forbids naming clients in every section and forbids inventing a
third party. Would not have worked, because a name the reader cannot ring is decoration.

> **A quote you cannot check is an advertisement.**

So the section attaches to every quote the thing a reader can act on: **what they are permitted to do
to check it.**

# The Check States

| State | What it means | How many |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| **Will take a reference call** | That client agreed to speak to a prospective one | 2 |
| **Named privately** | The name is given in the room, never on a website | 2 |
| **Words only** | Permission covers the sentence and nothing else | 1 |

**A ladder where every rung is the top rung is not a ladder.** No firm holds reference-call
permission across its whole book, so a page claiming otherwise is one nobody believes. Every study
says in words that the bottom rung is **a stricter legal team, not a lesser client.**

| 05 | not renewing | The market moved and the plan did not | Stage 05 · Delivery | Words only |

**Every quote opens on a reservation.** A testimonial that opens on praise is indistinguishable from
copy the firm wrote about itself. Quote 05 is the strongest thing on the page precisely because the
client did not come back.

Situations come from `CONS-S02`, stages from `CONS-S05`. All five studies carry the same five, so the
batch varies by composition rather than by copy.

# What Is Attached To A Quote, And What Is Not

**Not attached, anywhere:** a name, job title, role, company, sector, industry, headshot or logo —
not even placeheld. The contract forbids inventing a third party, and a placeholder job title is the
same claim in smaller type.

**Attached instead:** the situation, the stage, and the check state — the firm's own vocabulary and
the firm's own disclosure policy.

The quote sentences are placeholder demo text marked `data-placeholder="true"`. Unattributed, they
make no representation about any person or company.

# The Media Problem

Ask this section's photograph what it is **of**. Not the client's face — they are anonymous, and
portrait areas stay reserved regardless. Not their premises or product. A handshake photograph is
decoration pretending to be evidence.

What remains honest is **the room a reference call is taken in** — the firm's own place, and where
the section's offer actually leads. Two studies carry one; three carry none.

Shape A does not appear in this batch and could not: A requires media in every item, and five items
would mean five photographs of a person who may not be shown.

# What This Section Will Not Do

- **No names, titles, roles, companies, sectors, headshots or logos on a quote.** Placeheld or
  otherwise. See *Client quotes* in `../CONSULTING-THEME-CONTRACT.md`.
- **No quote that opens on praise.** It reads as copy the firm wrote about itself.
- **No page where every client will take a call.** No firm has that permission across its book.
- **No satisfaction score, NPS, star rating or review count.** Each asserts a survey system.
- **No status board.** Three states are not three grades, and the middle one carries no fill so the
  set cannot read as a scale of good to bad.
- **No global header or navigation.** The section-shell rule.
