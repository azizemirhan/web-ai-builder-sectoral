# Section Role

What the firm is in a position to count.

# The Governing Idea

Every rule this catalog has written forbids the ordinary contents of an outcomes section. Value
delivered, ROI multiples, savings, retention and satisfaction scores are all excluded in
`../CONSULTING-THEME-CONTRACT.md` as measurements nobody ran.

That is not a section with nothing to say. It is a section with an argument.

> **Consulting cannot measure its own effect, because the counterfactual does not exist. Nobody runs
> the company twice.**

A firm publishing *"£2.4bn of value delivered"* is claiming to know what would have happened
otherwise. It did not run the company twice. Doubting the figure is not conservatism, it is
arithmetic.

So the section **counts the work, not the effect.**

All five studies carry the same five, so the batch varies by composition rather than by copy. Every

counter in `CONS-S01`.

# Why `47` Stays

`CONS-S05` sells the client the right to stop at every stage. **A methodology with exits and an
outcomes section with no early exits contradict each other.** The early-exit count is what makes the
methodology credible, and it is the most testable claim on the page — which is why `004` builds its
call to action on it: *"Ask about the 47."*

# The Missing Number

Every study closes on a refusal naming the four metrics the firm will not publish, and why.

> `Value delivered` · `ROI` · `Savings` · `Satisfaction`
>
> Each would require knowing what would have happened otherwise.

**Not the same device as the withheld line in `S07`.** That one says *we cannot tell you* — a
confidentiality limit, someone else's secret. This one says *it cannot be known* — an epistemic
limit, nobody's secret.

Each study names what to do instead: **ask the references.** A refusal with no alternative is a dead
end.

# The Media Problem

A number cannot be photographed, and a chart of a number is not a photograph of anything. What
remains honest is the work the figures came from — the room, the model on a screen, the interviews.
Every slot label says so, and `001` and `005` carry no slot at all.

Three reserved areas across five studies. The most media-light batch in the sector.

# Figures Are Placeholder Data

Every counted figure is marked `data-placeholder="true"`, as is the `1998` in the counting-period
line. The ghost in `005` is **not** — it carries no value and never will. It is not a field awaiting
content, it is the argument.

# What This Section Will Not Do

- **No value delivered, ROI, savings, retention or satisfaction figures.** Each would require a
  counterfactual nobody ran. They appear only in the list of what is refused.
- **No chart, gauge, delta, arrow or sparkline.** Each implies a target and a trend, which implies a
  measurement system that does not exist here — and the technical-document idiom is out regardless.
- **No client names, logos or testimonial attributions.** Confidentiality, sector-wide.
- **No awards, rankings or certifications.**
- **No unsourced count.** Four figures are the firm's own record, one is the client's, and every one
  says which.
- **No global header or navigation.** The section-shell rule.
