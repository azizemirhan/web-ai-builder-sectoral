What we have published

A piece that could not have been wrong was not worth writing.

So each one carries the claim it makes, stated plainly enough to disagree with, and whether we still believe it.

  • The reorganisation will not move the decision rights

    Authority follows budget, not reporting lines. Moving boxes moves nothing.

    Still holds

  • Cost programmes rebuild the cost they removed

    Removing cost without removing the work that generates it buys a delay, not a saving.

    Still holds

  • Outsourcing a capability you do not understand transfers the confusion

    You cannot specify what you have never run.

    Still holds

  • Data quality is a governance problem, not a tooling one

    A tool cannot decide who owns a definition.

    Partly wrong — we underrated how much the tool shapes the governance
