How to get in

  1. One

    The door. The one beside the sandwich shop, not the glass one on the corner.

  2. Two

    The bell. There is no reception. Ring the one marked four.

  3. Three

    The lift. It needs a fob, so somebody comes down for you.

  4. Four

    Who to ask for. The person you are meeting, by name. We have no front desk.

  • Getting here. Six minutes from Farringdon, badly signposted.

  • Who is based here. Fourteen of eighteen desks. A. Whitfield is in three days a week.

  • What is not here. No parking. No visitor wifi worth using. No room bigger than twelve.
