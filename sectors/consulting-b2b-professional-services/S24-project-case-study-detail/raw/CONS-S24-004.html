How it ran

  1. One

    We stopped interviewing after nine

    The tenth said what the ninth had. Sampling, not census.

  2. Two

    We drew no chart until week ten

    Everyone expected one in week two. It would have been wrong.

  3. Three

    We tested it on decisions already taken

    Four from the previous quarter, re-run. Two came out differently.

What we got wrong

  • We started with the wrong sponsor

    Six weeks in we moved to the COO. It should have been week one.

  • We under-scoped the interviews

    We asked for eight and needed fourteen. That cost them, not us.

What was delivered. A decision map, a redesigned ownership model, and ninety days of us in the room.
