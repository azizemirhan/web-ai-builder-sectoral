The strategy was agreed and nothing had changed

Ordered by what we did not know yet, rather than by what we achieved. The two things we got wrong are in the column where they happened.

  • DisciplineOperating model
  • LengthFourteen weeks
  • TeamThree, one full-time
  1. Week 0

    Which decision was stuck

    Signed off twice, and eleven months later nothing downstream had moved. Nobody in the building could name it.

  2. Week 4

    Whether nine interviews were enough

    The tenth said what the ninth had. We stopped: sampling, not census.

  3. Week 6

    Whose call it actually was

    We had started with the wrong sponsor. We moved to the COO. It should have been week one.

  4. Week 8

    How many interviews it really needed

    We asked for eight and needed fourteen. That cost them, not us.

  5. Week 10

    Whether a chart would help yet

    Everyone had expected one in week two. Drawn then, it would have been wrong.

  6. Week 12

    Whether the design held

    Four decisions from the previous quarter, re-run. Two came out differently.

What was delivered. A decision map, a redesigned ownership model, and ninety days of us in the room.
