## The Section Role

One body of work in depth, as evidence of how the organisation actually works.

## The Trap

Every case study describes a smooth arc, because the firm chooses the frame, the scope and the
ending. The reader is asked to treat a self-portrait as evidence, and a reader who has run a
programme knows it — which is why they discount every case study they have ever read.

## The Governing Idea

> **A case study is the only account of an engagement written by the people who were paid for it.**

So every study carries **what we got wrong**, at the same weight as what we did:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **We started with the wrong sponsor** | Six weeks in we moved to the COO. It should have been week one |
| **We under-scoped the interviews** | We asked for eight and needed fourteen. That cost them, not us |

Both cost the client something, which is what makes them worth printing. **A case study that contains
no error is not a record, it is a brochure with dates in it.**

## The Confidentiality Constraint, Carried From S07

The sector brief scopes this role to anonymised engagement context. `S07` established the answer and
every study here applies it: **anonymity removes the client, not the problem.** The situation is
stated in full because it is the reader's situation, and the redaction is named rather than hidden —
*the sector, because two firms operate in it and naming one identifies the other.*

## The Media Set — purpose, type and fallback

The README requires this record. **Ask this section's photograph what it is *of*.** It cannot be the
client's premises, their people or their product — that is the whole constraint. What remains honest
is the firm's own artefact.

| Slot | Purpose | Expected type | Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The decision map** | The first artefact the engagement produced; proves the method existed before the chart | Photograph or screen capture of the firm's own working document | Area collapses; `001` and `004` read complete without it |
| **The interview wall** | Shows sampling rather than census — the decision behind stage two | Photograph of the firm's own workspace mid-engagement | Area collapses; the caption carries the fact |
| **The room it was argued in** | The redesign was contested; this is where | Interior photograph, the firm's own or a neutral meeting space | Area collapses |
| **The ownership model on screen** | The only chart in the engagement, at week ten | Screen capture of the firm's own model | Area collapses |
| **The re-run session** | The design tested against decisions already taken | Photograph of the firm's own working session | Area collapses |

**No slot may hold a client logo, a client interior, a named individual from the client, or a stock
boardroom.** Every label states what the photograph is and never who is in it, per `S01`'s rule that
the pose is the failure, not the subject.

`003` carries the full five — the largest documented set in this sector's catalogue, which is what
the README asks this role for. `005` carries none, and says why.

## The Five Compositions

- **`001`** — the safe complete page: situation and facts, one lead strip, the three decisions, a
  pair of artefacts, what was delivered, the errors, the withheld line last.
- **`002`** — a gallery of one firm's own artefacts is a mood board; it documents that work happened
  without documenting what happened. So this study carries **one** area, placed at the point where
  the account turns — **the last thing before *what we got wrong***. The reader arrives at the error
  having just looked at the object that was supposed to prevent it, which is the only argument a
  single photograph on a case page can make, and it can only make it from that position.
- **`003`** — **the documented set is the account.** Each area carries the decision it belongs to and
  the week it was made, and there is no separate narrative of how it ran, because this is it. An
  uncaptioned grid of five images proves work happened and nothing else; a dated one is a record, and
  a record is the only thing a case study under confidentiality still has to offer. **The dates are
  the evidence the client's name would have been.**
- **`004`** — a case study is offered as evidence and almost never says **what it is evidence about.**
  The closing panel names one condition under which it transfers and one under which it does not,
  with the second pointing at different work by name. **A firm that tells you when its own best work
  is irrelevant to you is the only kind whose relevant work you can believe** — and it forfeits every
  enquiry that came from a reader assuming a case generalised.
- **`005`** — **the page is ordered by what we did not know yet.** Every other case study is ordered
  by achievement, which is only available in hindsight and is therefore the one ordering nobody had at
  the time. The consequence is the argument: **the two errors are no longer quarantined in a section
  at the bottom — they are dated events at `week 6` and `week 8`**, met in order along with everything
  else. A section for mistakes is still a way of containing them.

`005` carries no media because every honest artefact here is a picture of something already resolved.
**An artefact photographed in week four cannot show what was still unknown in week four**, so a
documented set would quietly contradict the ordering.

### Not the `S07-004` move

`S07-004` inverted the hierarchy and made the **withheld** line the header of every card, selling the
discretion itself. `S24-004` leaves the withheld line where the rest of the batch puts it and sells
the boundary of the evidence instead. Different arguments, non-overlapping compositions.

### A tooling fix, and what it revealed

Last batch recorded that `check-composition-collisions.js` returned **empty sequences for every `S23`
study**. This batch found the cause and fixed it: the checker matched `<section class="shell">`, but
page-level studies use `<article class="shell">`, so the shell regex never matched and the sequence
came back empty. Worse than silence — **the near-match scorer then read two empty sequences as a 100%
collision**, and this batch was initially reported as colliding with `S23` at three variants. The
regex now accepts either element via a backreference, and `S23`–`S27` are covered for the first time.

With real fingerprints there are **no exact collisions**. One residual 100% near match between
`S23-002` and `S24-002` is a remaining limitation, not a defect: both wrap their content in a single
`div class="col"`, and the block regex only reads the first indentation level, so the checker sees
`COL` and nothing else in both. The two were compared by eye — a service page with its costs at
display size and no media, against a case page with one artefact at the hinge — and they do not share
a skeleton. Left as a recorded limitation rather than patched, to avoid re-fingerprinting the whole
sector mid-batch.
