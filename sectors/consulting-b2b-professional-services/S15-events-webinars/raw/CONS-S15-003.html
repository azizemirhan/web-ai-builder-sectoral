Events

How much of the room would be yours.

What makes an hour worth it is who else is in it.

Every entry leads with your share of the room, which is the field no events section prints, because three of the four figures are unflattering.

  • 1 of 12 Room of twelve

    The stage-03 session

    For people whose programme is late and whose reporting says it is fine.

    When it fills we run it again, within the quarter.

  • 1 of many Broadcast

    The counterfactual hour

    For anyone. There is no room, so there is no cap.

    We will not call it a workshop. Recorded either way.

  • 1 of a few hundred Someone else’s stage

    A conference talk

    For whoever bought a ticket. We do not control the room.

    Useful for deciding whether to talk to us, not for getting work done.

  • Not you Closed

    A client briefing

    For one client’s team. Not open and not on sale.

    Listed because leaving it off would make this calendar look busier.

The cap is real. The urgency is not.

No countdown, no seats-remaining counter, no waiting list that is really a mailing list. Twelve exists so the session works; when it fills, we run it again.
