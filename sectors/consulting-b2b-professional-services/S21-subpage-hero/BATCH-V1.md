## The Section Role

Top-of-page contextual hero for an internal page. It states where the visitor has landed and what the
page is for, then hands off to the page body.

## The Trap

**The subpage hero is a shrunken homepage hero.** Same claim, same stock image, same *book a call*,
with the page title dropped into the middle of it. It tells the visitor nothing they did not already
know and delays the page they clicked to reach.

The second failure follows from the first: the visitor **came from somewhere** — a search result, a
link in a proposal, a piece somebody forwarded — and a header that re-introduces the firm has thrown
that away and started the conversation over.

## The Governing Idea

> **A subpage hero is the only block on the page the visitor did not come for.**

They are not here to meet the firm. They are checking that this is the page they wanted. So the
section's job is **to be finished** — confirm the page in one pass and hand off.

Which settles the question of the one permitted action. It is not *book a call*: that asks for the
decision before the page justifying it has been read, and every section from `S18` to `S20` asks for
it better. The honest single action is **the way out** — the one condition under which the reader is
on the wrong page, and the name of the right one.

## The README Test

> *`S01` is the homepage or primary sector hero. `S21` is the internal page contextual hero. A study
> that would work unchanged as `S01` has not answered this role.*

`S01` in this sector is a proposition — *hired when the decision is too expensive to get wrong*.
**Nothing in this batch makes a claim about the firm at all.** Every line is about this page: who it
is for, how long the work runs, where it stops, and when to go elsewhere. Dropped onto a homepage,
any of the five says nothing.

Three further decisions come from the same test:

- **No stages.** No study sets a viewport height, a full-bleed ground or type over an image. Every one
  is a *block* at the top of a page.
- **Media never behind the title, and never at stage scale.** `001` is a contained portrait beside a
  column; `003` is a contained strip beneath the head. Both are internal-page scale.
- **Three of five carry no image at all.** The scaffold asks for at least one variant that works
  without one; the honest reading of internal pages is that most have none.

## The Page All Five Render

One page, five compositions, so the composition is the variable and not the copy.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Parent** | `Services` |
| **Title** | `Operating model redesign` |
| **What the page is** | For when the strategy is agreed and the organisation underneath it is why it has not happened |
| **Who it is for** | A COO with an agreed strategy and a structure that predates it |
| **How long** | `Nine to sixteen` weeks, named in the first hour |
| **Where it stops** | Design, and the first `ninety` days. Not the rest |
| **The way out** | Wrong page? If the strategy itself is the question, read `Strategy under uncertainty` |

The three facts are the ones that let a visitor confirm the page without reading it. *Where it stops*
is there because a service page that does not say where the work ends is describing a relationship,
not a service — the boundary `S19`'s section `05` makes in the proposal, made at the top of the page.

## The Five Compositions

- **`001`** — the parent pill, title, framing line and the three facts all sit in one column, and a
  contained 4:5 portrait anchors the full height of it. The way out closes on a rule beneath.
- **`002`** — no cards, no columns, no metadata strip. The facts are **three ordinary lines with the
  label inline**, because a header that arranges three short facts into a grid has spent structure on
  something the eye takes in either way. What the editorial reading buys is scale: the title is the
  largest thing and the framing sentence the second largest.
- **`003`** — the dense reading is not *more words* (it sits mid-band) but **more of the page answered
  before the body starts**. The two facts that decide whether to read on sit beside the title; the
  length, which is scanned rather than read, is a **badge on the media** instead of a line in a
  column. Not a fuller header — a finished one.
- **`004`** — **converting by ejecting.** The panel exists to get the wrong visitor out in the first
  two seconds, because the readers who remain are then reading a page that is about their problem.
  The trade is real: this header forfeits every enquiry from a visitor who would have filled in a form
  on the wrong page. **A conversion rate measured on the wrong page is not a conversion rate.**
- **`005`** — **the sentence that would make you leave is set at the same size as the title.** Every
  other subpage hero sets the page name large and the caveat in grey six-point type at the bottom.
  Here they are the same size and the second is only dimmer, so the reader takes in the page and its
  own limit in one movement of the eye.

`005` is the sector-native reading because this catalog has answered *is this mine?* by disqualifying
rather than persuading — `S15` names who the room is not for, `S17` refuses to call a registered
address an office, `S20` agrees with three of the four reasons to leave. **A page header that puts its
own disqualifier in display type is the same move at the top of the page**, and a hero is the only
block a reader has not yet committed to.

## The Boundary With S22

A list of sibling pages would render *where you are* spatially and was drafted for `005`. It was cut:
a sibling set is the page's **position in the site**, which is `S22`'s subject. What stayed is a
single named alternative tied to one stated condition, which is about **this page's limits** — the
same line the `ARC` batch drew when it kept an in-page contents list in `S21` and the site locator in
`S22`. No study carries a breadcrumb trail, a `<nav>`, or more than one link.

## What This Section Will Not Do

- **No restatement of the homepage hero**, no sector positioning, no brand manifesto.
- **No *book a call*, no form, no second action.** One contextual link per study.
- **No stage.** No viewport height, no full-bleed ground, no type over media.
- **No breadcrumb trail and no navigation.** Those are `S22`.
- **No body content.** That is `S23`–`S27`.
- **No global header or footer.** The section-shell rule.

### Composition collisions — one real, one accepted

**`S21-004` was rebuilt.** Its first draft was a split head followed by one saturated panel with the
list inside it, at zero media — which is `CONS-S01-004`'s skeleton exactly. That is the sharpest
possible failure of this section's own README test, and the checker caught it at 80%. A saturated band
*after* the head also reads as a conclusion, which a page header is not. The exit now sits in the
**second column of the head**, simultaneous with the page identity rather than consequent on it, and
the facts run across the ground beneath both.

**`S21-004` ↔ `S02-004` (80%) is accepted.** `S02-004` is a featured card with five compact cards and
**six reserved image areas** — shape A. The 80% comes from the coarse three-token sequence alone, and
the checker's near-match score ignores the `media` field, which is the entire difference between them.

### Found only by rendering

- `001` — a ~590px void between the text column and the portrait, with the facts scattered across the
  full frame and the third stranded under the image. The facts moved inside the text column so the
  portrait anchors the whole of it rather than only the title.
- `003` — **the badge was unreadable.** `.site > span` (0,2,1) overrode `.badge` (0,2,0), so the pill
  text rendered in slot-ink on the accent. Third occurrence of this cascade class after `S12-001` and
  `S14-001`; fixed with `.site > span:not(.badge)`.
- `003` — the 16:9 area rendered 677px tall, which makes the header a stage and contradicted the
  study's own note saying *strip proportion*. Reset to `24 / 7`.
- `005` — the disqualifier set to three lines against the title's two, so the dim line dominated by
  volume and inverted the hierarchy the study argues for. Measure widened to `32ch`; both are now two
  lines at the same size.
- `002`, `004` — measures tightened where a line wrapped a single word or a framing sentence filled
  half its column.
