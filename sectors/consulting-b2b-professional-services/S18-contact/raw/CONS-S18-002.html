Contact

A form is a way of not giving you an address.

So there is no form here. Four routes instead, each naming who is at the other end — and one of them is us telling you not to write yet. The photograph is a desk rather than a lobby, because the claim is ownership.

Image area — the desk this arrives on

  • A first conversation

    The person who would run the work. We do not have salespeople.

    We ask the five questions. By the end you know which engagement model we would propose, and which we would refuse to sell you.

  • A direct address

    One named person, not a shared inbox.

    If they are the wrong person they tell you who is right, rather than forwarding you into a queue.

  • A reference call

    A client of ours, not us.

    We introduce you to the one closest to your situation, and then we are not on the call.

  • Not yet

    Nobody. This route is you leaving.

    Take the two reports that cost nothing and the five questions. Come back when there is a decision waiting.

Three things that will not happen

  • We do not ask for your budget, timeline or company size.
  • Writing to us does not add you to a list.
  • If you do not reply, we do not chase.
