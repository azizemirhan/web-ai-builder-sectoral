Contact

What stops you writing is not the form.

It is what one message turns into. So here is that part first.

If you do not reply, we do not chase.

One message stays one message. There is no sequence behind it, and no colleague picking it up when the first one gives up.

  • We do not ask for your budget, timeline or company size.
  • Writing to us does not add you to a list.
  • A form is a way of not giving you an address, so there is not one here.

Four routes, and who is at the end of each

  • A first conversation

    We ask the five questions, and you leave knowing which model we would refuse to sell you.

    The person who would run the work.

  • A direct address

    If they are the wrong person they say who is right, rather than forwarding you into a queue.

    One named person, not a shared inbox.

  • A reference call

    We introduce you to the client closest to your situation, then we are not on the call.

    A client of ours, not us.

  • Not yet

    Take the two reports that cost nothing. Come back when there is a decision waiting.

    Nobody. This route is you leaving.
