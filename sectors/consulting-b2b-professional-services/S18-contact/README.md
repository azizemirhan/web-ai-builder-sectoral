# Section Role

How to reach a person.

# The Trap

Name, email, company, phone, message, **we will get back to you shortly.**

Nobody filling that in knows who receives it, whether anyone owns it, or what happens next — and the
opacity is the point of it. The section that exists to make a firm reachable is the one that most
reliably hides it.

# The Governing Idea

> **A form is a way of not giving you an address.**

There is no form in this batch — no `<form>`, no `<input>`, no submit button anywhere. Four routes
instead, and every one names the person or the thing at the other end.

# The Four Routes

| Route | Who receives it |
| --- | --- |
| **A first conversation** | The person who would run the work. We have no salespeople |
| **A direct address** | One named person, not a shared inbox |
| **A reference call** | A client of ours, not us |
| **Not yet** | **Nobody. This route is you leaving** |

All five studies carry the same four. Each resolves into the section that carries it — `S11`'s five
questions, `S06`'s people, `S09`'s reference call, `S14`'s ungated reports.

# The Fourth Route

*Not yet* is `S11`'s first question — *what decision is this actually for?* — turned around and
pointed at the reader.

> **A contact section that cannot say *do not write yet* wants the enquiry more than the
> engagement.**

# Three Things That Will Not Happen

- We do not ask for your budget, timeline or company size.
- Writing to us does not add you to a list.
- **If you do not reply, we do not chase.**

The third is the one that actually stops people writing, and nobody prints it.

# No Response-Time Promise

*We respond within 24 hours* is unverifiable and is omitted sector-wide. What is printed instead is
**ownership**, which a reader can check the moment they get a reply.

# The Media Question

One honest photograph, and it is not a lobby or a receptionist: **the desk this arrives on.** The
claim is ownership, and a desk with one chair at it is the only image that supports it. Two studies
carry it; three carry none.

# What This Section Will Not Do

- **No form.** No `<form>`, `<input>`, `<textarea>` or submit button anywhere in the batch.
- **No response-time promise.** Ownership is printed instead.
- **No `info@` inbox.** A route with no owner is not a route.
- **No map, no social icon set, no lobby photograph, no portrait.**
- **No route without a named owner** — including the one whose owner is *nobody*.
- **No global header or navigation.** The section-shell rule.
