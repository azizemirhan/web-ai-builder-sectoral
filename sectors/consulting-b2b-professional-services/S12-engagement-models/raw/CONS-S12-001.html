How we can be engaged

Every fee model pays us to do something.

Read the one that pays us to do the wrong thing. The misalignment is usually what decides how an engagement ends.

  • Fixed fee

    Suits a question whose shape is already known.

    Pays us to Finish early. If the question changes in week three, the scope becomes the argument.

  • Time and materials

    Suits a problem nobody has framed yet.

    Pays us to Take longer. The defence is a weekly decision to stop or continue, written into the contract.

  • Retainer

    Suits a decision that comes back every quarter.

    Pays us to Still be here. The hardest thing on this page to cancel, so we cap it at six months and make you re-sign.

  • Secondment

    Suits wanting the capability rather than the answer.

    Pays us to Be agreeable. Someone inside your organisation stops being able to say the unwelcome thing.

Outcome-based fees. We do not offer them.

Paying on outcome requires knowing what would have happened otherwise, and nobody runs the company twice. A fee resting on a number neither of us can produce is a dispute with a date on it.

Tell us the situation and we will say which we would propose — and which we would refuse to sell you.
