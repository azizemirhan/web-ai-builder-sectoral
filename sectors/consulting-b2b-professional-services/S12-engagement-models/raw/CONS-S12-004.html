How we can be engaged

Start with the one we will not sell you.

Every fee model pays us to do something. Read the one that pays us to do the wrong thing.

Outcome-based fees. We do not offer them.

Paying on outcome requires knowing what would have happened otherwise, and nobody runs the company twice. A fee resting on a number neither of us can produce is a dispute with a date on it.

  • Fixed fee

    Suits a question whose shape is already known.

    Pays us toFinish early, and argue about scope if the question moves.

  • Time and materials

    Suits a problem nobody has framed yet.

    Pays us toTake longer. Weekly stop-or-continue, written into the contract.

  • Retainer

    Suits a decision that returns every quarter.

    Pays us toStill be here. Capped at six months, then you re-sign.

  • Secondment

    Suits wanting the capability, not the answer.

    Pays us toBe agreeable, and stop saying the unwelcome thing.
