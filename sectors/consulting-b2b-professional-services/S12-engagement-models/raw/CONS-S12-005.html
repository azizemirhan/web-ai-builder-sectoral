How we can be engaged

What each one pays us to do.

Four ways to hire us, set the way a reader actually scans them. None of these incentives is hidden or shameful. All four are compromised, in different directions.

  • Finish early.

    Fixed fee

    Suits a question whose shape is known. If it changes in week three, the scope becomes the argument.

  • Take longer.

    Time and materials

    Suits a problem nobody has framed. The defence is a weekly stop-or-continue, written into the contract.

  • Still be here.

    Retainer

    Suits a decision that returns every quarter. Capped at six months, then you re-sign.

  • Be agreeable.

    Secondment

    Suits wanting the capability rather than the answer. Someone inside your organisation stops saying the unwelcome thing.

Image area — the weekly meeting where the engagement is stopped or continued

Outcome-based fees. We do not offer them.

Paying on outcome requires knowing what would have happened otherwise, and nobody runs the company twice. A fee resting on a number neither of us can produce is a dispute with a date on it. If you want our money at risk, ask for the fixed fee.
