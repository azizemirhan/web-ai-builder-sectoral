We carry the timing risk

One model

  • Fixed fee

    Suits a question whose shape is already known.

    Pays us toFinish early. If the question changes in week three, the scope becomes the argument.

You carry it

Three models

  • Time and materials

    Suits a problem nobody has framed yet.

    Pays us toTake longer. The defence is a weekly stop-or-continue, written into the contract.

  • Retainer

    Suits a decision that returns every quarter.

    Pays us toStill be here. Capped at six months, then you re-sign.

  • Secondment

    Suits wanting the capability, not the answer.

    Pays us toBe agreeable. Someone inside your organisation stops saying the unwelcome thing.

Only one of these puts the risk on us, and it is the one that works only when the question is already clear. The safest model is available on the easiest problems.

Outcome-based fees. We do not offer them.

Paying on outcome requires knowing what would have happened otherwise, and nobody runs the company twice.
