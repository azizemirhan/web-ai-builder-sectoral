Three models

  • Time and materials

    Suits
    A problem nobody has framed yet.
    Pays us to
    Take longer. The defence is a weekly stop-or-continue, written into the contract.

  • Retainer

    Suits
    A decision that returns every quarter.
    Pays us to
    Still be here. Capped at six months, then you re-sign.

  • Secondment

    Suits
    Wanting the capability, not the answer.
    Pays us to
    Be agreeable. Someone inside your organisation stops saying the unwelcome thing.

Only one of these puts the risk on us, and it is the one that works only when the question is already clear. The safest model is available on the easiest problems.

Outcome-based fees. We do not offer them.
