# Section Role

How the firm thinks about a problem before it starts working on one.

# The Trap, And The Permission

The named, trademarked, diagrammed model is the most parodied artefact in this sector. Almost none of
them are discoveries about business — they are marketing artefacts with a pyramid attached.

`../CONSULTING-THEME-CONTRACT.md` bans the 2×2 matrix as ornament, along with maturity ladders,
pyramids, funnels and wheels. It then makes one explicit exception:

> A framework diagram is legitimate only where the section role is the framework itself, and never in
> a hero.

**That is this section.** The diagram was available here and is not used in any of the five studies.
**The batch is declining a permission, not obeying a prohibition** — a distinction each study records
in its own header.

# The Governing Idea

> **Every firm arrives with a framework. A framework is a claim to already know the shape of your
> problem.**

So the section opens on the five questions asked before anything is drawn.

# The Five Questions

| # | Question | Why it is asked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | **What decision is this actually for?** | Most engagements are commissioned without one. If no decision is waiting, there is no engagement |
| 02 | **Who has already made it?** | Sometimes it is made and the work is meant to support it. A legitimate job, but a different one |
| 03 | **What would change your mind?** | If nothing would, the evidence stage is theatre and we will say so |
| 04 | **What happens if you do nothing?** | The most common alternative, and the one nobody costs |
| 05 | **Who has to live with it afterwards?** | The people in the room are rarely the people who operate the result |

All five studies carry the same five, so the batch varies by composition rather than by copy.

Question 04 applies the same position `S08` takes about its own metrics — the counterfactual nobody
runs — to the client's decision instead of the firm's results. Deliberate continuity: it is the one
belief the firm holds consistently enough to apply to itself.

# Why Questions Rather Than Principles

A list of principles would have been `S03` again with different nouns: a name, a claim, a limit.
Questions have a different grammar and a different owner.

> **The firm supplies the questions. The client supplies the answers.**

# The Media Problem

A section about thinking has almost nothing to photograph, and the worst possible image would be a
whiteboard covered in a finished model — it would show the arrival of the framework this section says
it does not bring.

There is exactly one honest photograph, and two studies carry it:

> **The wall at the start of an engagement, before anything is on it.**

The other three carry no reserved area at all.

# No Placeholder Data

The first `CONS` batch with none. Nothing here is a countable fact — no counts, dates, names or
figures, only the firm's own questions and its own reasons for asking them.

# What This Section Will Not Do

- **No named or trademarked model.** No ™, no ®, no capitalised house methodology.
- **No matrix, pyramid, funnel, wheel or maturity ladder** — including the one the contract would
  have permitted here.
- **No text-only card grid.** A card without media is a table cell with rounded corners; `003` uses a
  rail for exactly this reason.
- **No numeral gutter used as a column.** Numerals sit above the question as graphic elements.
- **No jargon compounds.** *Synergistic*, *holistic*, *end-to-end*, *value unlock* — all scanned for
  and absent.
- **No global header or navigation.** The section-shell rule.
