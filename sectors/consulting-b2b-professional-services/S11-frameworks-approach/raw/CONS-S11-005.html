How we approach it

We walk in with questions, not a model.

A framework is a claim to already know the shape of your problem. No named model, no trademark, no pyramid — these five, asked before anything gets drawn.

  • 01

    What decision is this actually for?

    Most engagements are commissioned without one. If no decision is waiting, there is no engagement.

  • 02

    Who has already made it?

    Sometimes it is made and the work is meant to support it. Better said in week one than discovered in week six.

  • 03

    What would change your mind?

    If nothing would, the evidence stage is theatre and we will say so.

  • 04

    What happens if you do nothing?

    The most common alternative, and the one nobody costs.

  • 05

    Who has to live with it afterwards?

    The people in the room are rarely the people who operate the result. This one steps back to the margin because that is where they are standing.

We will draw you a diagram.

After these, not before — and it will be a picture of your problem rather than a model we brought with us.
