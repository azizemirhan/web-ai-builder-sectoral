How we approach it

Take these into your next meeting. You do not need us in the room.

A framework is a claim to already know the shape of your problem. We arrive with five questions instead.

  • 01

    What decision is this actually for?

    Most engagements are commissioned without one. If no decision is waiting, there is no engagement — and this is the single most common way consulting money is wasted.

  • 02

    Who has already made it?

    Sometimes it is made and the work is meant to support it. A legitimate job, but a different one.

  • 03

    What would change your mind?

    If nothing would, the evidence stage is theatre and we will say so.

  • 04

    What happens if you do nothing?

    The most common alternative, and the one nobody costs.

  • 05

    Who has to live with it afterwards?

    The people in the room are rarely the people who operate the result.

Ask them yourself. If the answers are uncomfortable, that is the moment worth a conversation.

We will draw you a diagram afterwards — a picture of your problem, not a model we brought with us.
