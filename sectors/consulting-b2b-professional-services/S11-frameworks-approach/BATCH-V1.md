## The Section Role

How the firm thinks about a problem before it starts working on one.

## The Trap

The named, trademarked, diagrammed model is the most parodied artefact in this sector, and
deservedly. Almost none of them are discoveries about business — they are marketing artefacts with a
pyramid attached, and every buyer senior enough to hire a consultancy has seen forty of them.

`../CONSULTING-THEME-CONTRACT.md` already bans the 2×2 matrix as ornament, along with maturity
ladders, pyramids, funnels and wheels *"deployed to look rigorous"*. But it then makes one explicit
exception, and it is worth quoting because it applies to exactly one section in the catalog:

> A framework diagram is legitimate only where the section role is the framework itself, and never in
> a hero.

**That is this section.** So the diagram was available here and is not used — in all five studies.
**The batch is declining a permission rather than obeying a prohibition**, and each study says so in
its own header, because the distinction changes what the refusal means.

## The Governing Idea

> **Every firm arrives with a framework. A framework is a claim to already know the shape of your
> problem.**

So the section does not open on a model. It opens on **the five questions asked before anything is
drawn.**

## The Five Questions

Shared across all five studies, so the batch varies by composition rather than by copy.

| # | Question | Why it is asked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | **What decision is this actually for?** | Most engagements are commissioned without one. If no decision is waiting, there is no engagement |
| 02 | **Who has already made it?** | Sometimes it is made and the work is meant to support it. A legitimate job, but a different one |
| 03 | **What would change your mind?** | If nothing would, the evidence stage is theatre and we will say so |
| 04 | **What happens if you do nothing?** | The most common alternative, and the one nobody costs |
| 05 | **Who has to live with it afterwards?** | The people in the room are rarely the people who operate the result |

Question 04 is the same intellectual position `S08` takes about its own metrics — the counterfactual
nobody runs — applied to the client's decision instead of the firm's results. That is deliberate
continuity, not repetition: it is the one belief the firm holds consistently enough to apply to
itself.

## Why Questions Rather Than Principles

A list of principles would have been `S03` again with different nouns: a name, a claim, and a limit.
Questions have a different grammar and, more importantly, a different owner.

> **The firm supplies the questions. The client supplies the answers.**

That is the actual division of labour in an engagement, and it is the reason this section is not a
restatement of the capability list.

## Boundary With The Sections Around It

| Section | Owns |

## The Media Problem

A section about thinking has almost nothing to photograph, and the worst possible image here would be
a whiteboard covered in a finished model: it would show the arrival of the framework the section says
it does not bring.

So there is exactly one honest photograph, and two studies carry it:

> **The wall at the start of an engagement, before anything is on it.**

It is the literal subject — the state the firm turns up in. The other three studies carry no reserved
area at all.

## The Conversion Insight, Which `004` Is Built On

The obvious move was the one `S10-004` already makes: put the offer first and fill it. Repeating that
gesture in the section directly below would have made the assembled variant-004 page the same
movement twice, so this study converts a different way.

> **It gives the method away.**

The five questions are printed to be used *without* the firm. The panel does not ask for a briefing
call; it says that if the answers turn out to be uncomfortable, that is the moment worth a
conversation. A firm that hands over its opening move is claiming the value was never in the
questions but in what happens after them — which is both true and the strongest thing this section
can say about itself.

Four consecutive sections, four different conversion bases:

| Study | Converts on |
| --- | --- |
| `S08-004` | **Audit** — ask about the number we would rather not print |
| `S09-004` | **Verification** — quotes sorted by who will take a call |
| `S10-004` | **Relevance** — the wall cannot tell you whether we have met your problem |
| `S11-004` | **Giving the method away** — take these into your own next meeting |

## Notes Per Study

**`001`** — **shape C.** Five questions on six tracks, the first two at double width and larger type
so the set has a reading order without a rule anywhere. The numeral sits above the question as a
graphic element, never as a gutter column — the contract bans the latter specifically.

**`002`** — the one plate, and the type carries the rest. Questions set two to a row at standfirst
size, the fifth spanning the full measure to close the run. The caption states why the image is a
blank wall.

**`003`** — **a rail, not a grid.** The obvious structured answer was five modules, and it was
rejected twice over: five text-only boxes are five cards without media, which the contract calls *a
table cell with rounded corners*; and `S09-003` and `S10-003` already sit on this variant as uneven
coloured blocks, so a third would have made three consecutive sections one texture. A rail is a
different structure rather than a restyled grid, and it matches the content — five questions asked in
sequence, with the next one visibly waiting past the frame edge.

**`004`** — **the give-away.** See above. Question 01 is the one held in the accent because it is the
one most often unanswerable, and a reader recognising themselves in that block is the whole
conversion.

**`005`** — **the staircase.** Questions 01 to 04 each indent further than the one above, so the
section visibly walks into the problem. Question 05 returns to the left margin, at the width the
section opened on — because **05 is the question about the people who are not in the room**, and the
composition steps back out to where they are standing. The indent is the argument; nothing labels it.

## What Was Drafted And Rejected

**A descending type scale for `005`.** `S09-005` already uses size as its variable at this variant,
one section away, and two cascades on the same assembled page would read as a house style rather than
two ideas. In `005` the type size is constant and only the left edge moves.

**A five-module grid for `003`.** Rejected for the two reasons above.

**A framework diagram, in all five.** Permitted here by the contract and declined anyway.
