Capabilities

A service is a promise. A capability is what makes it keepable.

Every firm lists the same six. What separates them is where each one stops.

Quantitative modelling

The numbers the decision actually rests on.

Not a model you cannot maintain once we leave.

Primary research

Talking to your market rather than reading about it.

Named interviews, not a survey panel.

Organisation design

Structure, roles, and who is allowed to decide what.

We will not name individuals for the roles.

Commercial analysis

Margin, mix, and what your contracts actually cost.

We do not set your prices.

Programme direction

Running it after the decision has been made.

We advise it or we run it. Not both.

Facilitation

Getting a divided board to one answer.

We will not manufacture agreement you do not have.

The limits are the part worth reading twice.
