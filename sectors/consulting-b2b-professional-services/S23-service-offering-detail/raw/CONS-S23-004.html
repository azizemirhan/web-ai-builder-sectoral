Operating model redesign

Redrawing who decides what, and who has to be in the room for them to decide it. The chart is the last thing we draw, not the first.

Before the stages, what it costs you.

  • Your COO

    About a day a week, for the whole of it.

  • Eight to twelve interviews

    An hour each, with people who will not enjoy them.

  • One decision you have already made

    Reopened. There is always one.

How it runs

  1. STAGE 01

    Decision map

    Every decision above a threshold, and who currently makes it.

  2. STAGE 02

    Where it breaks

    The ones that take four weeks, and the ones taken twice.

  3. STAGE 03

    Redesign

    New ownership, tested against decisions you actually made last quarter.

  4. STAGE 04

    First ninety days

    We are in the room for the first ones. Then we are not.

  • Not included

    Implementation. Systems. Anything after the first ninety days.

  • When it will not work

    If the strategy is still moving, the map is out of date before it is finished.
