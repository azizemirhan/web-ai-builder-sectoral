The contents page, before the proposal exists.

Four of these six are in every proposal you will ever read. Two are in almost none, so they go first.

  • 05

    What we would not do

    The boundary, so the scope argument happens now rather than in week six.

  • 06

    What would make us wrong

    The assumption it rests on, written down so you can test it.

  • 01

    The decision

    The one it exists to serve, in your words. Without this line there is no proposal.

  • 02

    The stages

    What happens in each, and where you may stop.

  • 03

    The people

    Named, with the stages they are not on.

  • 04

    The fee model

    Which of the four, and what it pays us to do.
