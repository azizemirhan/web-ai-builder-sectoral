The terms before the pitch, not after it.

A proposal is the only document you will be asked to sign. Here is what it costs and what is in it.

What the conversation costs

  • One hour of yours. We do not call it free; an hour of somebody who can sign is the most expensive thing here.
  • Nothing else. The five questions and whatever we draw stay with you either way.
  • It can end in a no from us. Roughly one in four does.
