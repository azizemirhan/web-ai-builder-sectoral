# Section Role

The opening proposition of a firm that sells judgement. It answers, before anybody decides whether
to scroll: **what kind of decisions do you work on, at what altitude, and who actually shows up?**

# The Governing Constraint — Judgement Has No Photograph

    Architecture has buildings. Construction has sites. Wellness has treatment rooms.
    Consulting has a decision that already happened in a room nobody photographed.

This is why the sector's websites default to stock handshakes, boardroom tables and glass towers,
and it is the problem every study in this section has to solve rather than avoid.

**The one honest photographic subject is people — specifically the people who would actually be on
the engagement.** Every media slot here states *what the photograph is* and never who is in it.

**And the pose is the failure, not the subject.** A slot reserving space for a posed executive
portrait has not escaped the cliché, it has reserved room for one. The slot labels say so.

# What Is Real, And What May Not Be Invented

| Element | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| The kind of decisions worked on, and at what altitude | **Real** |
| Practice and discipline names | **Real** — generic vocabulary, not a claim |
| What the first conversation is, and who is in it | **Real** — a policy the firm can honour |
| Year founded, engagements, offices | **Reserved fields** |
| Every portrait | **Reserved** — the slot names the role, never a person |
| Named clients or logos | **Forbidden** — confidentiality, sector-wide |
| Value delivered, ROI, retention %, satisfaction % | **Omitted** — asserts a measurement nobody ran |
| Awards, rankings, tier placements | **Omitted** |
| A response time or answer-within | **Forbidden** |

# What This Section Will Not Do

- **No global header or navigation.** The section-shell rule; a hero study contains the hero only.
- **No 2×2 matrix, framework diagram, pyramid, funnel or maturity ladder.** The sector's signature
  ornament, ruled out by `../CONSULTING-THEME-CONTRACT.md`. A framework belongs to `S11`, and even
  there as content rather than decoration.
- **No counter of invented achievement.** Reserved fields only, and `004` carries none at all.
- **No jargon compound.** Consulting copy earns authority by being plainer than expected.
