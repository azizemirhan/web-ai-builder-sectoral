# Section Role

How an engagement actually runs, stage by stage.

# The Governing Constraint — Every Five-Box Process Is The Same Five Boxes

Discover, Analyse, Design, Implement, Review. This is the most interchangeable section in consulting,
and a reader learns nothing from it because nothing in it could have been different.

> **A process is only credible if it says where you can get off.**
> Every stage names its exit: stop here, and this is what you keep. A process with no stated exit is
> a sales ladder drawn as a diagram.

# The Five Stages, At Full Capacity

Every stage in every study carries all four fields:

| # | Stage | What happens | You get | Stop here and | Weeks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 01 | Frame | What is actually being decided, and by whom | A one-page decision statement | You keep the decision written down | 2 |
| 02 | Evidence | The work that makes an answer arguable | The model and the interviews, assumptions visible | You keep the evidence base, and can re-run it | 4 |
| 03 | Options | Two or three real choices, costed against each other | Options, with what each one forecloses | You keep the right to pick none | 3 |
| 04 | Decision | The room where somebody chooses | The recommendation, with the dissent recorded | You keep a paper your board can defend | 2 |
| 05 | Delivery | Standing it up, or handing it to whoever will | Named owners and the first ninety days | This is the end | 12 |

Durations are placeholder demo values marked `data-placeholder="true"`.

**Why the exits are not fabrication:** they are policy commitments a firm can honour, the same
category as the limits in `S03`. None is a claim about past performance.

# Full Capacity Is The Rule Here

This is a **structured content section**: the role carries fields, and the text budget for it is
170–230, not 90–170. See *The band measures prose, not completeness* in the theme contract. Cutting
a required field to hit a word count produces a section that looks tidy and cannot be used. What
stays short is prose — no stage description runs past one sentence.

# What This Section Will Not Do

- **No flow chart, funnel, maturity ladder, or five boxes with arrows between them.** The sector's
  reflex here, ruled out in `../CONSULTING-THEME-CONTRACT.md`. None of the five studies contains a
  connector, a spine or an arrow between stages.
- **No ruled row list.** A five-item sequence in one column is one hairline from a schedule; `001`
  and `002` separate stages with air and numerals instead.
- **No guaranteed timeline.** Durations are typical and marked as placeholder data, never a promise.
- **No global header or navigation.** The section-shell rule.
