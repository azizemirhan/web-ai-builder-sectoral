How we work

Five stages, and you may get off at any of them.

A process with no stated exit is a sales ladder drawn as a diagram. Each card ends with what you keep.

  1. One

    2 weeks

    Frame

    What is actually being decided, and by whom.

    You get
    A one-page decision statement.
    Stop here and
    You keep the decision written down.

  2. Two

    4 weeks

    Evidence

    The work that makes an answer arguable rather than asserted.

    You get
    The model and the interviews, assumptions visible.
    Stop here and
    You keep the evidence base, and can re-run it.

  3. Three

    3 weeks

    Options

    Two or three real choices, costed against each other.

    You get
    Options, with what each one forecloses.
    Stop here and
    You keep the right to pick none of them.

  4. Four

    2 weeks

    Decision

    The room where somebody chooses, and the paper that survives it.

    You get
    The recommendation, with the dissent recorded.
    Stop here and
    You keep a paper your board can defend.

  5. Five

    12 weeks

    Delivery

    Standing it up, or handing it to whoever will.

    You get
    Named owners and the first ninety days.
    This is the end
    There is no sixth stage to buy.

Most engagements stop at stage three. That is a result, not a failure.
