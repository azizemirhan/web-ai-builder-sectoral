How we work

Five stages, and you may get off at any of them.

A process with no stated exit is a sales ladder drawn as a diagram. Each card ends with what you keep.

  • 01
    2 weeks

    Frame

    What is actually being decided, and by whom.

    You getA one-page decision statement.

    Stop here andYou keep the decision written down.

  • 02
    4 weeks

    Evidence

    The work that makes an answer arguable rather than asserted.

    You getThe model and the interviews, assumptions visible.

    Stop here andYou keep the evidence base, and can re-run it.

  • 03
    3 weeks

    Options

    Two or three real choices, costed against each other.

    You getOptions, with what each one forecloses.

    Stop here andYou keep the right to pick none of them.

  • 04
    2 weeks

    Decision

    The room where somebody chooses, and the paper that survives it.

    You getThe recommendation, with the dissent recorded.

    Stop here andYou keep a paper your board can defend.

  • 05
    12 weeks

    Delivery

    Standing it up, or handing it to whoever will.

    You getNamed owners and the first ninety days.

    This is the endThere is no sixth stage to buy.

Most engagements stop at 03. That is a result, not a failure.
