How we work

Every stage ends somewhere you are allowed to stop.

Five stages, and each one names its exit. A process with no stated exit is a sales ladder drawn as a diagram.

Image area
  • 01

    Frame

    What is actually being decided, and by whom.

    You get A one-page decision statement. Stop here You keep the decision written down.
    2 weeks
  • 02

    Evidence

    The work that makes an answer arguable rather than asserted.

    You get The model and the interviews, assumptions visible. Stop here You keep the evidence base and can re-run it.
    4 weeks
  • 03

    Options

    Two or three real choices, costed and argued against each other.

    You get Options, with what each one forecloses. Stop here You keep the right to pick none of them.
    3 weeks
Image area
  • 04

    Decision

    The room where somebody chooses, and the paper that survives it.

    You get The recommendation, with the dissent recorded. Stop here You keep a paper your board can defend.
    2 weeks
  • 05

    Delivery

    Standing it up, or handing it to whoever will.

    You get Named owners and the first ninety days. The end There is no sixth stage to buy.
    12 weeks

Most engagements stop at 03. That is a result, not a failure.
