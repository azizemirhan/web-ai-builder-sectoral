# Section Role

The questions a buyer actually arrives with.

# The Trap, Which Is Two Traps

**The questions are invented.** *What makes you different.* *Why choose us.* Nobody has ever typed
those. They are marketing copy wearing a question mark, and *frequently* is the word doing the lying.

**The answers are hidden.** Every FAQ in this sector is a stack of collapsed rows, so the reader pays
in interaction to find out whether the answer was worth reading — which is also how a section
conceals that its answers are thin.

# The Governing Idea

> **"Frequently" is a claim, and almost no FAQ can support it.**

So every entry carries **when it is actually asked** — the field that makes the claim checkable. Two
of the six are entries the firm says are *not* frequently asked, which is the part no competitor will
print.

# The Six Questions

| When it is asked | The question |
| --- | --- |
| First call, every time | How much will this cost? |
| First call, every time | Who will actually do the work? |
| Right after the first call | Can you show me a client like us? |
| **Rarely, and it should be first** | What happens if it is not working? |
| Once the proposal is out | Will you put your fee at risk on the outcome? |
| **Never out loud** | Is the webinar a sales call? |

All five studies carry the same six. The four states describe the buying process rather than a
category scheme, and **a question nobody asks is the one worth putting in front of them.**

# The FAQ As An Index

Every answer resolves into the section that carries the detail — the engagement models, the people
and their stages, the naming policy, the stage-gate exits, the counterfactual, the difference between
a broadcast and a room.

**An FAQ is an index to the page it sits on.** On the assembled page this is the section that proves
the other fifteen agree with each other.

# No Accordion

> **Nothing here is collapsed. A question worth answering is worth reading without a click.**

Stated at the foot of every study rather than left as an absence — and the reason the batch carries
no JavaScript at all.

# The First All-C Batch

A question has nothing to photograph, and an FAQ that reaches for a stock image of somebody looking
thoughtful has already lost. **Zero reserved media across all five studies.**

# What This Section Will Not Do

- **No invented question.** No *what makes you different*, no *why choose us*.
- **No accordion, no `<details>`, no collapsed row.** Every answer prints in full.
- **No question without a *when*.** *Frequently* is a claim and it has to be supported.
- **No satisfaction figure and no response-time promise.**
- **No stock photograph.** The section carries no reserved media at all.
- **No global header or navigation.** The section-shell rule.
