Rarely, and it should be first

What happens if it is not working?

You stop at the next stage gate. The exits are in the contract, and clients use them. Asking before you sign gets you a different engagement from asking in month four.

Ask us this one

The five that do get asked

  • First call, every time

    How much will this cost?

    Not until we know which model fits. In the first hour we can say which one we would refuse to sell you.

  • First call, every time

    Who will actually do the work?

    The people in the room when you meet us. Each names the stages they are not on.

  • Right after the first call

    Can you show me a client like us?

    Not on this website. Tell us the situation and we will name the closest one we can.

  • Once the proposal is out

    Will you put your fee at risk on the outcome?

    No. That needs a counterfactual nobody can produce. For our money at risk, ask for the fixed fee.

  • Never out loud

    Is the webinar a sales call?

    The broadcast is a broadcast. The room of twelve is work. We label which is which.
