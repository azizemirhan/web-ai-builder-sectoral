A central function should own the standard, not the delivery

We now think this was wrong. Two engagements since went badly for exactly this reason. Nothing below has been edited.

Central delivery becomes a queue, and the queue becomes the reason nobody uses the standard.

The argument

A standard nobody owns decays. Put the standard and the delivery in one place and the standard survives.

Where it broke

The central team became the only route. Waiting for them cost more than the inconsistency ever did.

What we would write now

Own the standard. Publish it. Let delivery sit where the work is, and audit it quarterly.

Nothing is taken down. A piece we no longer believe keeps its address and gets this note.
