# Section Role

Where the firm actually is.

# The Trap

**The map.** An embedded one is a remote runtime dependency, forbidden outright. A drawn one is
worse: a world map with four pins is a picture of a network, and **four pins is not a network.**

**The skyline.** Glass towers and cities at dusk are in the anti-patterns. This is the section that
reaches for them hardest.

**The inflation.** A registered address becomes an office, a person who lives somewhere becomes a
presence, and twelve cities become a global footprint.

# The Governing Idea

> **An address is not an office.**

Most locations lists are a count of cities, and most of those cities are one of four things: a floor
with people on it, somebody who lives there, a registered address nobody sits at, or a desk borrowed
inside a client's building. **Printing which is which is the only version a reader can check.**

# The Four Entries

| Kind | City | What is actually there | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The office** | `London` | A floor with `18` desks | Meet us here |
| **No office** | `Manchester` | `Three` of us live here and work from home | We will meet you in the city |
| **Registered address** | `Frankfurt` | Nobody sits here; it exists so we can invoice in euros | Post something to it |
| **Borrowed desk** | `Singapore` | Inside a client's building, for one engagement | Nothing |

All five studies carry the same four. **One of the answers is *nothing*,** printed at the same size
as the rest.

# The Entry With An Expiry

> **We do not list a city because someone flew there. This page loses an entry when a place stops
> being one.**

The fourth comes off when the engagement ends, and the removal policy sits next to the thing it will
remove — the same discipline `S10` applies to its logo wall.

# Why Never More Than One Image

**Three of the four cannot honestly be photographed.** There is no office in the second, only a door
in the third, and the fourth is inside a client's building, which `S07`'s confidentiality rule puts
out of reach.

So one is the honest maximum, and the asymmetry is left visible rather than evened out with stock
photography.

# What This Section Will Not Do

- **No map.** No embed, no drawn world, no pins. Four pins is not a network.
- **No skyline, no glass tower, no lobby.**
- **No *global presence*, no country count, no square footage.**
- **No address without what is actually at it.** A city name on its own is the inflation this
  section exists to correct.
- **No entry that has stopped being a place.** The removal policy is printed.
- **No global header or navigation.** The section-shell rule.
