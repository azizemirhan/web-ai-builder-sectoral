Where we are

An address is not an office.

So each entry leads with what the place actually is, and the city name is the caption beneath it. Only one of the four is a floor with people on it.

  • The office

    London

    A floor with 18 desks. The room of twelve is this room.

    You can meet us here.

  • No office

    Manchester

    Three of us live here and work from home. There is nothing to visit.

    We will meet you in the city, and say where when you ask.

  • Registered address

    Frankfurt

    Nobody sits here. It exists so we can invoice in euros.

    You can post something to it. That is all.

  • A borrowed desk

    Singapore

    Inside a client’s building, for one engagement. Not ours, and not open.

    It comes off this page when the engagement ends.

We do not list a city because someone flew there.

This page loses an entry when a place stops being one, which is why there is no map and no count of countries.
