Where we are

An address is not an office.

Four entries, and only one is a floor with people on it. The other three say what they actually are, and only one of the four can honestly be photographed.

Four kinds of address: a floor, people with no office, a door, and a desk in somebody else’s building.

    The office

    London

    A floor with 18 desks. The room of twelve is this room.

    You can meet us here.

  • No office

    Manchester

    Three of us live here and work from home. There is nothing to visit.

    We will meet you in the city, and say where when you ask.

  • Registered address

    Frankfurt

    Nobody sits here. It exists so we can invoice in euros.

    You can post something to it. That is all.

  • Borrowed desk

    Singapore

    A desk inside a client’s building, for one engagement. Not ours, and not open.

    It comes off this page when the engagement ends.

We do not list a city because someone flew there.
