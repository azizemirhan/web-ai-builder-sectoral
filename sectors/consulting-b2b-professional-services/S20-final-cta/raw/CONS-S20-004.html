Before you go

Three of your four reasons are good ones.

If you have read this far you are not undecided. You have a reason, and we agree with most of them.

The three we agree with

  • There is no decision waiting

    Come back when there is one. It is the first thing we would ask anyway, and without it there is no proposal.

    Then do not write.

  • It is not your call

    Take the five questions to whoever's call it is. They work perfectly well without us in the room.

    Then do not write.

  • You think we are wrong about something

    Say which, rather than booking an hour. We have revised a published piece on a reader's argument before.

    Then do not write.
