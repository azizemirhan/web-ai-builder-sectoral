Industries

Naming an industry is not knowing it.

So here is what each one actually constrains. If we have yours wrong, we have not worked in it.

  • One

    Financial services

    The regulator is a stakeholder in every decision, including the ones about cost.

  • Two

    Healthcare and life sciences

    Clinical staff cannot be reallocated by a spreadsheet.

  • Three

    Energy and utilities

    The price control cycle sets your horizon, not your ambition.

  • Four

    Industrials and manufacturing

    The plant sets the pace. A shorter changeover is a wish, not a plan.

  • Five

    Retail and consumer

    Margin is decided in the supply chain, not in the deck.

  • Six

    Public and regulated sectors

    Defensible or it does not happen. Speed is the second virtue.

Work somewhere not on this list? Say what constrains it.
