# Section Role

Which markets the firm works in — and whether the reader believes it.

# The Governing Constraint — Everybody Lists Every Industry

The default is a grid of twelve industry icons, and it fails because **nothing on it could have been
left off**, so it carries no information. Worse, an industry claim is specifically a claim to know
that market's constraints, and an icon makes no such claim.

> **Naming an industry is not knowing it.**
> Each entry names the binding constraint that makes work in that market different — which
> demonstrates knowledge instead of claiming it, and is checkable in one sentence by the only reader
> who matters.

# The Six Markets

| Industry | The binding constraint |
| --- | --- |
| Financial services | The regulator is a stakeholder in every decision, including the ones about cost |
| Healthcare and life sciences | Clinical staff cannot be reallocated by a spreadsheet |
| Energy and utilities | The price control cycle sets the horizon, not ambition |
| Industrials and manufacturing | The plant sets the pace; a shorter changeover is a wish, not a plan |
| Retail and consumer | Margin is decided in the supply chain, not in the deck |
| Public and regulated sectors | Defensible or it does not happen; speed is the second virtue |

All five studies carry the same six, so the batch varies by composition rather than by copy.

# Why The Constraints Are Not Fabrication

They are **general structural facts about how those industries operate** — the same category as
generic vocabulary, which the sector direction permits as real. None is a claim about this firm's
record, a named client, a figure or a measurement. Nothing here asserts the firm has worked in any
of these markets; it asserts that it knows what would make the work hard.

# How This Differs From S03

`S03` states a **limit** — what the firm will not do, a policy it must accept.
`S04` states a **constraint** — what the market imposes on anyone working in it, which the firm did
not choose. One is a promise about conduct; the other is a demonstration of knowledge.

# The Text-Forward Trap

Four of these five studies are text-led, and that is exactly the move that reintroduces the schedule
idiom this catalog already corrected once. **None of them uses a hairline under each item, a numeral
gutter column, or a right-aligned label column.** Items are separated by air, by an accent numeral
in a pill, by a hover surface, or by a colour field — never by a line.

# What This Section Will Not Do

- **No client logo wall.** Confidentiality is sector-wide; `S10` owns whatever is permissible there.
- **No coverage bars, maturity scores, sector-strength ratings or heat maps.** Every one is a
  measurement nobody ran. Where `003` signals depth it does so by tile size only, and refuses to put
  a number on it.
- **No market-share figures, deal counts or years-in-sector counters.**
- **No industry-versus-capability matrix.** Ruled out in `../CONSULTING-THEME-CONTRACT.md`.
- **No global header or navigation.** The section-shell rule.
