  1. Artefact · 3:2our own artefact

    Case One

    The strategy was agreed and nothing had changed.

    What we did
    Redrew decision rights across the top three layers.
    What changed
    The next capital decision took one board meeting, not four.
    Withheld
    The sector. Two firms operate in it; naming one identifies the other.

  2. Artefact · 4:3the cost model

    Case Two

    It cost more to run than it should.

    What we did
    Rebuilt the model from the contracts up, not the budgets down.
    What changed
    Two assumptions everyone relied on were wrong.
    Withheld
    The site count. It is public, and it would name them.

  3. Artefact · 4:3the re-baselined plan

    Case Three

    The programme was late and reporting said it was fine.

    What we did
    Re-baselined against what had actually been delivered.
    What changed
    The board learned the real date eleven months earlier.
    Withheld
    Every date. The programme is still running.

  4. Artefact · 4:3the wall of interview notes

    Case Four

    A decision was coming and the evidence was thin.

    What we did
    Interviews across the market, with assumptions exposed.
    What changed
    They chose the option nobody had arrived wanting.
    Withheld
    The decision. It has not been announced yet.
