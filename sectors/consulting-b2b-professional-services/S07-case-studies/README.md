# Section Role

Evidence of having done the work — under the sector's hardest constraint.

# The Governing Constraint — Confidentiality

The sector brief records it: **client confidentiality applies.** This section has to prove the firm
has done the work **without naming who it was for.**

The default failure is *"a leading global financial services organisation"*. Everybody writes it,
nobody believes it, because the anonymisation strips out exactly the part that made it evidence.

> **Anonymity removes the client, not the problem.**

**1. The situation is stated in full.** It is the reader's situation, not the client's identity. The
four situations are the ones from `CONS-S02`, so a reader who recognised themselves there recognises
themselves again here.

**2. The redaction is made visible.** Every case ends on a **withheld** line naming what cannot be
said *and why*.

# The Four Cases

| Case | Situation | Withheld, and why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | The strategy was agreed and nothing had changed | The sector. Two firms operate in it; naming one identifies the other |
| 02 | It cost more to run than it should | The site count. It is public, and it would name them |
| 03 | The programme was late and reporting said it was fine | Every date. The programme is still running |
| 04 | A decision was coming and the evidence was thin | The decision. It has not been announced yet |

All five studies carry the same four, so the batch varies by composition rather than by copy.

# The Media Problem, Which Only This Section Has

Ask this section's photograph what it is **of**. It cannot be the client's premises, their people or
their product — that is the whole constraint.

What remains honest is **the firm's own artefact**: a model on a screen, a wall of interview notes,
the room a decision was taken in. Every slot label says so. `004` carries none at all.

# Outcomes Are Placeholder Data

The outcome line in every case is marked `data-placeholder="true"`. **Outcomes are the field this
section fabricates**, so they are marked and clearable rather than asserted. Situations, actions and
withheld lines are not placeholder data — they are generic vocabulary, generic professional work,
and statements about the firm's own disclosure policy.

# What This Section Will Not Do

- **No client names, logos or testimonial attributions.** Confidentiality, sector-wide.
- **No anonymised inflation.** *"A leading global player"*, *"a Fortune 500 organisation"*, *"a
  top-tier institution"* — all say nothing while implying everything.
- **No value figures, percentages, ROI multiples or savings claims.** Every one is a measurement
  nobody published.
- **No censor bar, stamp or CONFIDENTIAL watermark.** A redaction is one hairline from a
  classified-memo metaphor, which `../CONSULTING-THEME-CONTRACT.md` rules out. The withheld line is
  a colour field with a plain sentence in it.
- **No global header or navigation.** The section-shell rule.
