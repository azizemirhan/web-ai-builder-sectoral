Getting hold of us

A message is not a booking.

Nothing is held until somebody replies, and the somebody is whoever would be treating you rather than a shared inbox. Three ways to reach us, and what each one is actually good for.

The quickest

Ring

Telephone
Telephone number—

Whether it would suit you, whether there is anything left this week, whether you should be here at all — a minute of talking settles all three.

Nobody here works on commission, so the answer is sometimes no.

The most useful in writing

Write

Email
Email address—

Better for anything with a list or a photograph in it, and for questions you would rather put in your own words.

It does not hold a time. Only a conversation does that.

Before you commit

Come in

Address
Street address—

To see the room and meet whoever would be treating you. No appointment and nothing booked at the end of it unless you want one.

That gets you a conversation, not a treatment there and then.

Who replies

The person who would be doing the treatment. Not a shared inbox and not somebody reading from a script, which is also why an answer sometimes takes until the end of the day.

What we will not answer in writing

Whether a treatment suits you in particular is a conversation rather than an email. And if it is about something changing on your skin, that is a doctor's question — we will say so rather than guess.
