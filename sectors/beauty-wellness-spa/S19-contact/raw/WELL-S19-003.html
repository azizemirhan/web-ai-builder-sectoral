Getting hold of us

Which way in suits which question

Ring us

Telephone Telephone number—

  • Whether something would suit you at all
  • Whether there is anything left this week
  • Moving or cancelling something already booked

Not forAnything you want a record of afterwards. If it needs to be in writing, write.

Write to us

Email Email address—

  • Questions with a list or a photograph in them
  • Anything you would rather set down carefully
  • Telling us something before you arrive

Not forHolding a time. Writing in does not reserve anything until somebody has answered you.

Come in

Address Street address—

  • Seeing the room before you commit to it
  • Meeting whoever would be treating you
  • Asking the thing you would rather not put in writing

Not forBeing treated there and then. Turning up gets you a conversation, not an appointment.

After you get in touch

Three things happen, in this order, whichever way you came in.

First
Somebody reads it properly — whoever would be doing the treatment, not a shared inbox and not anything automatic.
Then
You get an answer to what you asked, including when the answer is that we are not the right people for it.
After that
Only then is a time held, once you have said yes to one. Nothing is reserved before that point.
