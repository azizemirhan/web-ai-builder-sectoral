Getting hold of us

Ask first, book later

Three ways in, and they are genuinely good for different things. Pick whichever suits the question you have.

Room area · 3:1 · the desk

  • 01

    Ring us

    Telephone
    Telephone number—

    Best for anything that starts with would this suit me or could you fit me in. Both are quicker to sort out in a minute of talking than in a chain of messages.

  • 02

    Write to us

    Email
    Email address—

    Better when there is a list or a photograph involved, or when you would rather set the question down properly than say it out loud.

  • 03

    Come in

    Address
    Street address—

    For when you want to see the room and meet whoever would be treating you before you commit to anything. No appointment needed to do that.

  • A message is not a bookingNothing is held until one of us has replied and you have said yes. If a time matters to you, say so and we will tell you straight away whether it is still there.
  • Who answersWhoever would be doing the treatment, rather than a shared inbox. It means replies are slower than an automatic one and worth more.
