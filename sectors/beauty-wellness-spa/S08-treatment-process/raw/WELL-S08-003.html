What happens

Five steps, and where you get a say in each

The sequence is the same whatever you booked. What changes is what you ask for along the way.

  1. One

    Arriving

    You come in, leave your things, and sit down.

    Your sayAsk for the room warmer or cooler before you settle.

  2. Two

    A short conversation

    We ask what you want from the session. No clipboard.

    Your sayTell us anything you would rather we left alone.

  3. Three

    The treatment

    You lie down and the work begins, adjusted as we go.

    Your sayLighter, heavier, or stop — at any point.

  4. Four

    Coming back up

    Time to sit, have something to drink, and come round.

    Your sayTake as long as you want over this part.

  5. Five

    Afterwards

    If something would suit you at home, we say so now.

    Your saySay no. It is genuinely not a problem.
