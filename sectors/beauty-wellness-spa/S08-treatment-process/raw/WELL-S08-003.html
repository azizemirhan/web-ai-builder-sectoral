What happens

Five steps, and where you get a say in each

The sequence is the same whatever you booked. What changes is what you ask for along the way.

  1. The door

    01

    Arriving

    What happens

    You come in, leave your things, and sit down.

    Your say

    Ask for the room warmer or cooler before you settle.

  2. Two chairs

    02

    A short conversation

    What happens

    We ask what you want from the session. No clipboard.

    Your say

    Tell us anything you would rather we left alone.

  3. The couch

    03

    The treatment

    What happens

    You lie down and the work begins, adjusted as we go.

    Your say

    Lighter, heavier, or stop — at any point.

  4. The window seat

    04

    Coming back up

    What happens

    Time to sit, have something to drink, and come round.

    Your say

    Take as long as you want over this part.

  5. The desk

    05

    Afterwards

    What happens

    If something would suit you at home, we say so now.

    Your say

    Say no. It is genuinely not a problem.
