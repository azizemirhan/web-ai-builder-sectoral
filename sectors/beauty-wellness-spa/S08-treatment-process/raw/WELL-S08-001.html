What happens

A visit, from the door onwards.

The same five steps whatever you booked, laid out across one line so you can see the end of the visit from the start of it. Nothing in them is a surprise, and you get a say at each one.

  1. Step one

    Arriving

    You come in, leave your things somewhere safe, and sit down. Nothing starts until you say you are ready.

  2. Step two

    A short conversation

    We ask what you want from the session and what your skin has been doing lately. No clipboard, no questionnaire.

  3. Step three

    The treatment

    You lie down and the work begins. Pressure, warmth and what gets used are adjusted as we go, whenever you say so.

  4. Step four

    Coming back up

    Time to sit, have something to drink and come round properly before you stand up and go anywhere.

  5. Step five

    Afterwards

    If something would suit you at home, we say so now — after the treatment, never during it.

Why it reads across rather than down

A list running down a page makes a visit look like a procedure with stages to get through. Five steps on one line is closer to what it is — an afternoon with a beginning and an end you can already see.

What is not here

No times, no lengths and no count of sessions. A sequence with a clock against it stops being a visit and becomes a protocol, and the length of yours depends on what you booked and how the day is going.
