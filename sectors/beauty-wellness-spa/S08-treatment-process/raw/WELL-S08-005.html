What happens

One room, five steps, no surprises

Treatment room
  1. One

    Arriving

    You come in, leave your things somewhere safe, and sit down. Nothing starts until you say you are ready.

  2. Two

    A short conversation

    We ask what you want from the session and what your skin has been doing. No clipboard.

  3. Three

    The treatment

    You lie down and the work begins. Pressure, warmth and what gets used are adjusted as we go.

  4. Four

    Coming back up

    Time to sit, have something to drink and come round properly before you stand up.

  5. Five

    Afterwards

    If something would suit you at home, we say so now — after the treatment, never during it.

The same five steps whatever you booked.
