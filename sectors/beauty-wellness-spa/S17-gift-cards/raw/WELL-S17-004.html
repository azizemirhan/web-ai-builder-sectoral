For someone else

The five things that make people hesitate

A gift card is easy to buy and easy to get wrong. These are the parts people worry about, and what we do about each of them.

  1. 01

    They will see what I spent

    The amount is not printed on the card, front or back. A present should not arrive as a receipt.

  2. 02

    It will expire

    It does not. There is no date on it and nothing is lost by leaving it a while.

  3. 03

    They will feel stuck with my choice

    It can go on anything, or toward something dearer. A named treatment is still swappable.

  4. 04

    There will be an awkward balance left

    Whatever is left stays on it. Nothing is written off and nothing is topped up in front of them.

  5. 05

    It will feel impersonal

    Your message is reproduced exactly as you write it. We do not tidy it or add anything of ours.
