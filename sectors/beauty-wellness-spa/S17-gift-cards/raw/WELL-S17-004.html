The five things that make people hesitate

A gift card is easy to buy and easy to get wrong. These are the parts people worry about, and what we do about each of them.

  • They will see what I spentThe amount is not printed on the card, front or back. A present should not arrive as a receipt.
  • It will expireIt does not. There is no date on it and nothing is lost by leaving it a while.
  • They will feel stuck with my choiceIt can go on anything, or toward something dearer. A named treatment is still swappable.
  • There will be an awkward balance leftWhatever is left stays on it. Nothing is written off and nothing is topped up in front of them.
  • It will feel impersonalYour message is reproduced exactly as you write it. We do not tidy it or add anything of ours.
