# Section Role

The questions people ask before they book, answered. The section removes the small uncertainties
that stop someone finishing a booking.

It answers: *the thing I would have to phone up to ask.*

# The Governing Constraint — And The Move That Resolves It

An FAQ has to answer the questions people actually ask, and in this sector several of those
questions ask for exactly what the catalog may not invent: **how long does it take, how much is it,
when are you free.** A placeholder cannot answer any of them.

The easy failure is to leave those questions out, which produces an FAQ that answers only what was
convenient. The move this batch uses instead:

> **The FAQ names the questions it will not answer in writing, and says why.**

That is not a dodge, it is the studio's actual position — and it is consistent with every other
section:

    "How long will it take?"
    "It depends on what we find once we start, so we agree it with you in the room rather than
    printing a number you would then be held to."

`../WELLNESS-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md` and `S08` already established that this sector states no
durations anywhere. `S15` is where a visitor finds out that the omission was deliberate. An FAQ
that explains its own silences is more trustworthy than one that fills them.

**Every answer in this batch is grounded in a commitment established elsewhere in the catalog** —
`S04` for choosing a practitioner, `S06` for booking, `S07` for what holds on every visit, `S08`
for what happens in the room, `S11` for how pricing works, `S13` for looking round first. Nothing
is invented for the FAQ. That also makes the section self-checking: an answer that contradicts its
source section is a defect visible from inside the catalog.

**Not present in any study:** an invented duration, price, figure, currency symbol, opening hour,
availability, waiting time or response time; an efficacy claim; a medical or diagnostic answer;
a fabricated policy that no other section supports.

# Boundary With S06, S14 And S19

| Section | What it owns |
| --- | --- |
| `S06` Consultation Booking | The booking **interaction** |
| `S14` About Philosophy | The **reasoning** behind how the studio works, at length |
| `S15` FAQ | **Short practical answers** to specific questions |
| `S19` Contact | Contact **facts** — how to reach the studio |

    "Why do you run one room?" is S14, and gets an argument.
    "Will I be sold anything?" is S15, and gets two sentences.

An FAQ answer that turns into an argument has taken `S14`'s role. Answers here stay short.

# Out Of Scope

- A global site header, primary navigation or footer
- The booking form, which belongs to S06
- Extended reasoning, which belongs to S14
- Contact details and opening hours, which belong to S19
- A support-desk or knowledge-base register — ticket numbers, categories, article counts,
  "was this helpful?" controls

# Authoring Questions

**1. What is the primary visual element of this role?**

Disclosure. An FAQ is a decision about what is visible before a click: everything open, everything
closed, or grouped. The batch spreads across that axis deliberately, and one study argues that
nothing should be hidden at all.

**2. What must a visitor understand immediately?**

That the awkward question they were going to phone up about is already answered here — including
the ones the studio has chosen not to print a number for.
