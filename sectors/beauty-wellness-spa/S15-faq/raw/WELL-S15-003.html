Before you book

Sorted by when the question turns up

The same eight answers, grouped by the point at which people usually think to ask them rather than by which department they belong to.

Before you book

  • Do I need to know what I want? ▾

    No. "Not sure yet" is a normal answer, and the short conversation at the start is where it gets decided.

  • What does it cost? ▾

    Every price is listed in full. No separate consultation charge, nothing added at the desk, and a shorter treatment costs less.

  • Can I look round first? ▾

    Yes. Come and see the room with no appointment and no treatment involved.

On the day

  • How long will it take? ▾

    We do not print a length, on purpose. It depends on what we find once we start, so we agree it with you in the room rather than giving you a number you would then be held to.

  • Can I ask for a particular person? ▾

    Yes, by name. You can also take whoever is free first — both are completely normal here.

  • Is it awkward to say stop? ▾

    No, and you would not be the first. Lighter, heavier or stop are all fine at any point, and none of them has to be explained.

Money and changes

  • Will I be sold anything? ▾

    Not while you are in the room. If a product would suit you we say so afterwards, standing up, when saying no is easy.

  • What if I need to move it? ▾

    Send a message. Changing or moving an appointment costs nothing and does not need a reason.
