The one people actually worry about

How long will it take?

We do not print a length, on purpose. It depends on what we find once we start, so we agree it with you in the room rather than giving you a number you would then be held to. If that sounds like a way of avoiding the question, here is what it protects you from.

  • A treatment that runs shorter than planned costs less, not the same.
  • Nothing is booked so tightly that yours has to be cut short for the next one.
  • You will be told what we are planning before anything starts.
Ask us directly

Or come and look round before deciding anything.

  • Do I need to know what I want?

    No. "Not sure yet" is a normal answer, and the conversation at the start is where it gets decided.

  • What does it cost?

    Every price is listed in full, with no separate consultation charge and nothing added at the desk.

  • Will I be sold anything?

    Not while you are in the room. If a product would suit you we say so afterwards, standing up.

  • Can I ask for a particular person?

    Yes, by name — or take whoever is free first. Both are completely normal here.

  • What if I need to move it?

    Send a message. It costs nothing and does not need a reason.

  • Is it awkward to say stop?

    No. Lighter, heavier or stop are all fine at any point, and none has to be explained.

  • Can I look round first?

    Yes. Come and see the room with no appointment and no treatment involved.
