About this one

It is the plainest thing on our list, and the one we book most. A face, cleaned properly, by somebody who is paying attention to it and will tell you what they see.

It starts with a conversation rather than with a product. Someone looks at your skin, asks what it has been up to lately and what you put on it at home, and then decides how firm to be with it. That decision is the whole treatment, really — everything after it is doing what was decided, slowly.

What follows is unremarkable, deliberately. Cleansed twice, steamed, exfoliated as gently or as thoroughly as your skin can take that day. Extractions only if you have said yes to them, and we ask beforehand rather than while your eyes are shut. Then a mask, and the part where most people fall asleep and we leave them to it.

There is no peel in it and no acid. Nothing is sold to you at the end, and you leave with the aftercare written down rather than explained at you on the way out. If your skin is sore, broken or doing something new, tell us when you book — we would rather move it, or send you to a doctor, than work over the top of it.

Most of it is not the products. It is somebody paying attention to your face and being honest about what they find.
