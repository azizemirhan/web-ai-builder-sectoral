What happens, in order

  1. 01A conversation. What your skin has been doing, and what you use on it at home.
  2. 02Cleansed twice. Once to take the day off, once properly.
  3. 03Steam. Warm, not hot, and you can say if it is too much.
  4. 04Exfoliation. How thorough depends on what your skin is like on the day.
  5. 05Extractions, if you asked for them. Agreed beforehand, stopped when you say.
  6. 06A mask, and quiet. Nobody talks to you during this part unless you start it.
  7. 07Finished, and explained. You are told what went on your face and why.

Included, and not

  • Included

    • The conversation at the start
    • Everything in the sequence above
    • Aftercare written down, on paper
    • Being told what was used on you

  • Not included

    • Any peel or acid treatment
    • Extractions, unless you ask
    • Anything sold to you at the end
    • A course, or a plan to come back

Who it is for

  • Suits

    • A first facial
    • Coming back after a long gap
    • Wanting a proper clean, plainly done

  • Ask us instead if

    • You want something aimed at one thing
    • You are booking before an event
    • Your skin is sore, broken or changing

Tell us before you book

If you are pregnant+−

Say so and we will adjust what goes on your face and how you are laid out. It is a common conversation and not an awkward one.

If you use a prescribed skin treatment+−

Bring the name of it. Some of them change what we can do, and we would rather find that out before you are on the bed than after.

If your skin is sore, broken or changing+−

Then this may not be the week for it. Something changing on your skin is a question for a doctor rather than for a spa, and we will say so rather than guess.

If you have had a reaction before+−

Tell us what to, even if you are not certain. We keep it on file and work around it every time you come, not just this once.
