What it is

A thorough clean, done slowly. It is the facial we suggest when somebody has not had one before, or has not had one in a long while and would rather start with something straightforward than with something aimed at a particular thing.

What happens

  • You talk first. Someone looks at your skin, asks what it has been doing lately, and asks what you use on it at home.
  • Cleansed twice. Once to take the day off, once properly.
  • Steam, then a gentle exfoliation. How gentle depends on what your skin is like when we get there.
  • Extractions, only if you want them. We ask before, not during, and we stop when you say so.
  • A mask, and the quiet bit. Most people go to sleep here and we let them.
  • Finished off. Whatever is right for your skin that day, and you are told what it was.

Includes

  • The conversation at the start
  • Everything described above
  • Written aftercare, on paper

Does not include

  • Any peel or acid treatment
  • Extractions, unless you ask
  • Anything sold to you afterwards

Who books it

People who want their face properly cleaned and would rather be told the truth about it than sold a course. It suits most skin, and if yours is sore, broken or changing, say so when you book — we may suggest waiting, or suggest a doctor instead of us.
