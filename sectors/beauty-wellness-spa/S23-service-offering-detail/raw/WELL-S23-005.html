What the appointment is like

An hour, described as it happens.

Not what it will do to your skin — what happens in the room, in the order it happens. Everything you need in order to decide is in the column on the right.

  1. First

    You sit down and talk first.

    Somebody looks at your face in proper light, asks what it has been doing lately, and asks what you put on it at home. Nothing has started yet and nothing is decided.

  2. Then

    You lie down and it goes quiet.

    Your hair is put out of the way, the light comes down, and somebody starts cleaning your face — twice, because the first pass is only taking the day off.

  3. Then

    Warm, then a bit of scrub.

    The steam is warm rather than hot and you can say if it is too much. How thorough the exfoliation gets depends on what your skin is like when we reach it, not on what it says on a card.

  4. If asked for

    Extractions, if you asked for them.

    Agreed before your eyes were shut, and stopped the moment you say so. If you did not ask, this part does not happen.

  5. Then

    The mask, and the bit people remember.

    Something cool goes on, your shoulders get done while it sits, and most people fall asleep. You will not be talked at.

  6. Last

    You sit up slowly.

    You are told what went on your face and why, in words rather than product names, and it is written down for you to take away.

Where the description stops

What your skin does afterwards is between you and your skin. We can tell you what we did and what usually helps, and we will not promise you a result. If something on your face is sore, broken or changing, that is a question for a doctor rather than for us.
