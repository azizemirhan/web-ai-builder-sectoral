# Authoring Questions

**1. What is the primary visual element of this role?**

The room and the person in it. A spa hero is carried by one dominant image of the treatment
space, the practitioner's hands or the guest at rest — at a scale that lets the visitor feel the
calm before reading anything. Every study in this batch reserves that dominant area first and
composes the copy around it.

**2. What must a visitor understand immediately?**

That this is a place for unhurried, hands-on care, and where to go next — the treatment range, or
a way to arrange a visit. Nothing else in the hero competes with those two.
