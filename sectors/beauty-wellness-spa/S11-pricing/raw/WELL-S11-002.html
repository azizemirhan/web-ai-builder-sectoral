How pricing works here

  • The price shown is the price paidNothing is added at the desk, and there is no separate consultation charge.
  • A shorter treatment costs lessIf a treatment runs shorter than planned, you pay for the shorter one.
  • You are told before, not afterIf anything would change the figure, it is said in the room before it happens.

The figures are reserved rather than invented, and no currency appears anywhere — a symbol would decide which country this studio is in. What the layout is settling is the only real question a price list has: how loudly the number is set against the name of the thing it belongs to.
