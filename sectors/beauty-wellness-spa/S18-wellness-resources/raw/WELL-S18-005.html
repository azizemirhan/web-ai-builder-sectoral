  • Why we write them downThese exist so you do not have to ring us about the same things. The aftercare notes are given to you on paper when you leave anyway; this is the same paper, kept somewhere you can find it at eleven at night.
  • Why the titles are blankA wellness index is where the big promises get in. The titles here are reserved rather than written, so a claim cannot arrive by accident, and the measures show exactly how much room a real one has.

None of this is medical advice. If something on your skin is changing, sore or worrying you, that is a question for a doctor rather than for a spa — and we will say so rather than guess.
