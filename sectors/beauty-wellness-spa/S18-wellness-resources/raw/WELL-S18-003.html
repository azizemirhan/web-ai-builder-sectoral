Three kinds of thing

Everything here is one of three. Knowing which is which saves you opening the wrong one.

Aftercare note
What we hand you on paper on the way out, in case it went in the bin. Short, and specific to one treatment.
A guide
Longer, and about a subject rather than an appointment. Written when we got tired of explaining the same thing.
A question answered
One question somebody actually asked, answered properly. Usually the ones people feel silly asking out loud.

None of this is medical advice. It is what we tell people in the room, written down so you do not have to ring us for it. If something on your skin is changing, sore or worrying you, that is a question for a doctor rather than for a spa — and we will say so rather than guess.
