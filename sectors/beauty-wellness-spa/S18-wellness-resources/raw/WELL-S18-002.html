Why the titles are drawn rather than written

A plausible-sounding placeholder title on a page like this is nearly always a claim — boost, detox, reset your skin. Reserving them keeps the claim out by construction, and what is being settled here is how a title sits against its category, which the measures show exactly.

What this is not

None of this is medical advice. It is what we tell people in the room, written down so you do not have to ring us for it. If something on your skin is changing, sore or worrying you, that is a question for a doctor rather than for a spa — and we will say so rather than guess.
