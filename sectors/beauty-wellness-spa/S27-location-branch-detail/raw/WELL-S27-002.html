Why there are so few of these

Rooms are not the difficult part; people are.

It would be straightforward to have more rooms than this. Every studio needs somebody in it who has been doing this long enough to know when to say no, and there are only so many of those at any one time. So the number of places we have is set by the number of people we would be happy to be treated by ourselves, and it moves slowly. When it does move it is because somebody is ready, not because a lease came up.

The practical consequence is the one worth knowing before you travel: not everything we do happens in every studio. Ring before a long journey and we will tell you whether this is the right room for what you are asking about, or whether it is the other one.

Why there is no map and no photograph

A call tells you more than a picture of a door.

There is no map on this page and no photograph of the door. If you want to know whether the building will suit you, ring and ask somebody who works in it — you will get a better answer than a picture would give you, and the column beside this carries the answers a picture would have been standing in for.
