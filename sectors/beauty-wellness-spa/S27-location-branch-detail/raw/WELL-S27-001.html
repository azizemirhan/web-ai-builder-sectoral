Not everything we do happens in every studio. If you are travelling for something in particular, ring first and we will tell you whether this is the right one.

When you arrive

  • Come in and sit down. There is no check-in to do and nobody expects you to announce yourself.
  • Early is fine. If you are early you can sit; the room may not be ready but the chair is.
  • Late is fine too, up to a point. Ring rather than rushing, and we will tell you honestly whether it still works.
  • Somebody comes out to get you. You are walked in rather than called for.

Getting in, and getting about

Tell us what you need and we will tell you what this building can do. Not with a row of symbols — with somebody who knows the place describing it to you honestly, including when the answer is that it will not work and another studio would be better.

Ask before you travel rather than after. It is a short conversation and it is nobody’s inconvenience.
