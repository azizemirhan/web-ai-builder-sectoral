Ring before you travel.

It is the one thing worth doing before a journey to a room you have not been to, and it takes less time than reading this page.

Ask about coming here
  • Whether this studio does what you want. Not everything we do happens in every room, and finding that out on arrival is nobody’s idea of a good start.
  • What the building is actually like. The door, what is underfoot, what is upstairs — described by somebody who works in it rather than ticked on a list.
  • What is nearby for leaving a car or a bike. What it is usually like at the time you are coming, without a promise about a space we do not own.
  • Whether somebody can come in with you. Say so and the room is picked accordingly.

Sometimes the answer is that the other studio is the better one — and you will be told that before you set off rather than after you have arrived.
