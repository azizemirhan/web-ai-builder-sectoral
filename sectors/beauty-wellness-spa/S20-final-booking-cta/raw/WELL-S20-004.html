When you are ready

Two ways to end up in the room, and both of them start small.

  • You know what you want

    Ask for a time and we will confirm one with you. You are agreeing to an appointment, not to a course of anything.

    Book a time

  • You are still deciding

    Then a question is a better first move than a booking, and it costs you nothing but the asking. Most people who write in start here.

    Ask us instead
  • Moving it is easyTell us and we will find another time. No fuss, and no lecture about it.
  • Asking is not buyingA conversation about whether something suits you is not a treatment and is not charged for.
  • Saying no is allowedAt the end of the conversation you can decide it is not for you, and that is a perfectly good outcome.

You will not find a countdown on this page. No timer, no last few places, and no offer that expires while you are thinking about it. If the day you want has gone we will tell you when you ask, and if we are quiet we will tell you that too.
