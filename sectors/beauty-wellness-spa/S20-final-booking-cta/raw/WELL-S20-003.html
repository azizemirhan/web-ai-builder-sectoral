The three things people want to know before they press it are answered beside this, rather than hidden behind a link at the foot of the page.

Book a time

Not sure yet? Ask us a question instead.

What happens next

One of us gets in touch to agree a time with you. Nothing is confirmed until you have both settled on one, so the step you are taking now is small.

It is a conversation, not a queue. Nobody is assigned to you by a system and no time is taken out of the diary until you have agreed one.

What can still change

  • The time, right up to the day itself
  • The treatment, once we have talked about it
  • Who does it, if you would rather somebody else

Tell us rather than quietly not turning up — it is easier for everybody, and nobody is cross about it.

What you are not agreeing to

Not a course, not a plan, and not a treatment you have not discussed. If it turns out that what you asked for is not right for you, we say so and nothing has been spent.

Changing your mind in the room is normal and we would rather you did.
