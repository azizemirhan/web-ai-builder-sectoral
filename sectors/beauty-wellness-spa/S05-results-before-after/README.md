# Section Role

The comparison. The section presents paired imagery from the studio's own work so a visitor can
see what a treatment looks like on either side of it.

It answers: *what does this actually look like?*

# The Governing Constraint

This is the highest-risk section role in the sector, and the one
`../WELLNESS-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md` names first. Everything the section is *for* is evidence, and a
placeholder study has no evidence and may not invent any.

**Structure is authored; evidence is reserved.** These five studies compose the comparison
*structure* — how a real studio would present paired imagery — and leave every position that would
carry a claim empty.

What that means concretely. No study in this batch contains:

- a fabricated before image, after image or comparison of any kind
- an outcome statement, result claim, improvement description or efficacy word
- a percentage, success rate, satisfaction figure or count of results
- a number of sessions, a treatment course length, or any time-to-result
- an identifiable client, a client name, an age, a testimonial or a rating
- a treatment presented as producing a named effect

What the studies **do** carry, because these are structural and non-fabricated:

- `Before` and `After` as slot labels. These name the two positions in a comparison, they do not
  assert what happened between them.
- The category and treatment name for each case, drawn from the same set used in `S02` and `S03`.
- Reserved media areas at the scale and pairing the real experience needs.

# Consent And Compliance — A Handoff Note, Not Page Copy

In most markets, before-and-after imagery for beauty and aesthetic treatments is regulated: it
normally requires documented client consent, and claims attached to it are restricted. A real
implementation of this section needs a consent line and a compliance review before any image is
placed.

That line is **deliberately absent from the visible compositions**. A study with no images cannot
truthfully say images are shown with consent, and the *Visible Website Copy vs Research Notes* rule
keeps policy explanation off the page. The requirement is recorded here and in `./BATCH-V1.md` so
that whoever fills these slots meets it.

# Out Of Scope

- A global site header, primary navigation or footer
- Treatments presented as the subject, which belongs to S02
- Testimonials and written client feedback, which belong to S10
- One case explored as a full page, which belongs to S24 and is itself scoped to anonymised
  programme context by the sector brief
- Prices, packages and session counts, which belong to S11 and S12

# Authoring Questions

**1. What is the primary visual element of this role?**

The pair itself — two images at the same scale, in the same crop, close enough together to be read
as one comparison rather than two pictures. If the pairing is not visually obvious with both slots
empty, the study has not built the structure.

**2. What must a visitor understand immediately?**

That these are two views of the same thing, which is which, and that seeing more means talking to
the studio rather than reading a number.
