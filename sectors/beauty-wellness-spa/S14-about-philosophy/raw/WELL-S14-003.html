Why it is like this

Four things we think, and what each one costs us.

Read across. Each position produces a way of working, and each way of working costs something — the third column is the one worth checking us on, because it is the one nobody prints.

We think So, in practice Which costs us

  • Quiet is the product, not the treatment.

    There is one guest in the building at a time, and nobody is shown in or out while you are in the room.

    Which costs usWe take less in a day than a studio running two rooms at once.

  • A treatment is a conversation, not a service you receive.

    What gets used, and how firmly, is decided with you in the room and changed whenever you say.

    Which costs usNo two appointments take the same shape, which makes the diary harder to run.

  • Nobody should be sold to lying down.

    If a product would suit you we say so afterwards, standing up, when saying no is easy.

    Which costs usA fraction of the retail a spa this size would normally take.

  • Committing should not be a trap.

    Nothing renews without telling you, sessions do not expire, and a course can be shared or stopped.

    Which costs usBundles are harder to sell when there is no saving attached to them.

Why the cost column exists

A position with nothing on the other side of it is a slogan. Every line above is checkable against the rest of this site, and each one is something we would rather you knew than found out.

What none of it claims

None of this makes the treatments better than anybody else's. It makes the hour behave the way we would want it to behave if we were the one lying down, which is the only test we have been able to apply honestly.
