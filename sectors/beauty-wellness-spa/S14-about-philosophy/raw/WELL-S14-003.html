Why it is like this

Four things we think, and what each one costs us

Each position produces a way of working, and each way of working costs something. The third step is the one nobody prints.

  1. 01

    We thinkQuiet is the product, not the treatment.

    So, in practiceThere is one guest in the building at a time, and nobody is shown in or out while you are in the room.

    Which costs usWe take less in a day than a studio running two rooms at once.

  2. 02

    We thinkA treatment is a conversation, not a service you receive.

    So, in practiceWhat gets used, and how firmly, is decided with you in the room and changed whenever you say.

    Which costs usNo two appointments take the same shape, which makes the diary harder to run.

  3. 03

    We thinkNobody should be sold to lying down.

    So, in practiceIf a product would suit you we say so afterwards, standing up, when saying no is easy.

    Which costs usA fraction of the retail a spa this size would normally take.

  4. 04

    We thinkCommitting should not be a trap.

    So, in practiceNothing renews without telling you, sessions do not expire, and a course can be shared or stopped.

    Which costs usBundles are harder to sell when there is no saving attached to them.

  • Why the cost step existsA position with nothing on the other side of it is a slogan.
  • What none of it claimsNone of this makes the treatments better than anybody else's.
