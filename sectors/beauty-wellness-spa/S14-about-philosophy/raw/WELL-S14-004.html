What we turned down

  1. 01

    We do not sell anything in the room

    No product is offered to you while you are on the couch. If something would suit you, we say so afterwards, standing up.

  2. 02

    We do not discount a bundle

    A course costs what the treatments in it cost. It exists so you can stop thinking about booking, not so you can chase a saving.

  3. 03

    We do not run two rooms at once

    One guest is in the building at a time. It halves what a day can earn and it is the single thing we would change last.

  4. 04

    We do not put a clock on the treatment

    Nothing is booked so tightly that a treatment has to be cut short, and a session that finishes early costs less.

  5. 05

    We do not ask you for a score

    No ratings, no review requests by text, no reminder emails asking how we did. If you want to say something, you will.

Why it is like this

Five things we decided not to do

Anyone can list what they offer. What tells you more is what a place has turned down, because every item here costs us money and nobody writes those unless they mean them.

Every one of them is checkable against the rest of this site. If you find us doing any of it, we have got something wrong.
