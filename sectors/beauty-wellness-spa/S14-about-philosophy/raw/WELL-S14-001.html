Everything on this site that sounds like a promise started as a choice about how to run the room. Here are three of those choices, and what each of them costs us.

Room area · 4:5 · the studio as it is

    Quiet is the product

    Not the treatment, not the products. If the room is not quiet, none of the rest of it works, so there is one guest in the building at a time.

    What it costsWe earn less in a day than a studio running two rooms at once.

    A treatment is a conversation

    You are not receiving a service that was decided before you arrived. That is why we ask first and keep changing things as we go.

    What it costsEvery appointment is slightly unpredictable to schedule.

  3. 03

    Nobody gets sold to lying down

    Being on a couch with your eyes closed is not a position to be asked to spend money in. If something would suit you, we say so afterwards.

    What it costsWe sell a fraction of the product we could.
