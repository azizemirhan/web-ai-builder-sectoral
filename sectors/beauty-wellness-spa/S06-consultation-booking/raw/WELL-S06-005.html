I would like , ideally . My name is and you can reach me on .

Nothing is booked and nothing is charged by sending this. It is a sentence, and the day and the time are settled between us afterwards.

  • Why a sentence

    A booking form asks you to fill in a table about yourself before anybody has spoken to you. This asks for the same four things in the order you would say them out loud.

  • What it does not decide

    Not the day, not the time, not the treatment. Choosing one above is a starting point, and changing your mind in the room costs nothing.

  • If you would rather talk

    Choose “to talk first” in the sentence and leave the rest. We will call.
