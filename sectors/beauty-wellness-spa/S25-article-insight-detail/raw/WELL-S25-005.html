None of this is medical advice. It is what we tell people in the room, written down. If something on your skin is changing, sore or worrying you, that is a question for a doctor rather than for a spa.

  • Why it looks like paperYou were probably handed this on paper. It is here as well because paper goes in a bag and stays there, and because the person who wrote it would rather you read it than remembered it.
  • Why the sheet is centredEvery other study in this section holds the frame with columns. This one is a sheet on a table, so it sits in the middle with the same margin either side — deliberately, rather than by default.
