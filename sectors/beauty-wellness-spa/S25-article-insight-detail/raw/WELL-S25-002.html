Where this stops

None of this is medical advice. It is what we tell people in the room, written down. If something on your skin is changing, sore or worrying you, that is a question for a doctor rather than for a spa.

Who writes them

These are written by whoever does the treatment, not by anybody paid to write. They are corrected when we change how we work, and nothing on this page has been paid for by a brand.
