Tell us what you need to get in

Most spas answer this with a symbol on a page. We would rather you asked us, because a symbol cannot tell you about the step at the door, how heavy it is, or where the nearest place to be dropped off is.

  • Ask before you travel and we will describe the building properly, including the parts that are awkward.
  • If one studio suits you better than another, we will say which and why.
  • If none of them works, we will tell you that too rather than let you find out on the day.
