What happens if things change

The thing that stops most people committing to a series is not the money, it is the feeling of being tied to it. So this is the part we would rather you read first.

  • If you pauseA membership waits and picks up where it left off. Nothing is lost while it is stopped.
  • If you miss oneThe visit rolls forward. It does not disappear and it is not charged for.
  • If you stopIt ends when you say so. Nothing renews without telling you first.
  • If you have paid aheadThe visits you have paid for stay yours, and can be given to someone else.
