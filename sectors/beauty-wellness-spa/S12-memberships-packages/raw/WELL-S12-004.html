Coming back

Before you commit, here is how you get out

The thing that stops most people committing to a series is not the money, it is the feeling of being tied to it. So this is the part we would rather you read first.

  1. 01

    If you pause

    A membership waits and picks up where it left off. Nothing is lost while it is stopped.

  2. 02

    If you miss one

    The visit rolls forward. It does not disappear and it is not charged for.

  3. 03

    If you stop

    It ends when you say so. Nothing renews without telling you first.

  4. 04

    If you have paid ahead

    The visits you have paid for stay yours, and can be given to someone else.
