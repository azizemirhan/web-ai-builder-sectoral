Three ways to keep coming

They differ in how the visits are spread out, not in how good they are. Pick the one that matches how you actually want to come in.

  • A course

    The same treatment, booked as a series and paid for once.

None of these are cheaper for being bought together — they exist so you can stop thinking about booking, not so you can chase a deal.
