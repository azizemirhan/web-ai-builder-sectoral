The difference is the rhythm, not the price.

Three arrangements drawn as what they actually are. One stops, one runs out, and one carries on — and that is the whole of the choice between them.

One visit on a regular rhythm with no end to it — which is why this rail runs off the edge rather than stopping. Pause it when you need to, stop it whenever, and nothing renews without telling you first.

Why the marks are not a count

Six of them on every rail, because the drawing is about spacing rather than quantity. The number of visits is a reserved field above, and inventing one in a picture would be inventing it twice.

And why there is no saving

None of the three is cheaper for being bought together. They exist so you can stop thinking about booking, not so you can chase a deal.
