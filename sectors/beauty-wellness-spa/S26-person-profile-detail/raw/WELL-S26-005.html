What meeting this practitioner is like

If you book with them, this is how it goes.

Five things that happen every time, in order. None of them is about qualifications — they are about what the hour is like when this particular person is running it.

  1. First

    They come out to get you.

    Rather than calling your name across a room. You are walked in, shown where to put your things, and told what is about to happen before anything happens.

  2. Then

    They look at your face in proper light and ask you about it.

    Most of the questions are not about your skin — what you use at home, what you stopped using, whether anything has reacted before.

  3. Then

    They say what they are going to do, and what they are not.

    If what you asked for is not what they would suggest, this is where you hear that, and you can disagree.

  4. Then

    They ask whether you would rather talk or not.

    Both are normal. Whichever you pick is what you get for the rest of it, without having to remind anybody.

  5. Last

    Then it goes quiet, and they get on with it.

    You are told before anything cold, anything warm, and anything that might sting. Nothing happens to your face that was not mentioned first.

  • How to get this personAsk for them by name when you book. If they are away you will be told rather than quietly given somebody else — and if what you are asking about is closer to somebody else's work, you will be pointed there instead.
  • What is not on this pageNo qualifications, memberships or awards, and not as empty fields either. What you are choosing is how somebody works, and the five beats beside this are the whole of it.
