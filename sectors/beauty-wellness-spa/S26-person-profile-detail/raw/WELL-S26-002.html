What you are choosing is how somebody works, and that is entirely describable without a face or a biography.

Before anything is decided

A long set of questions, most of which are not about your skin.

What you use at home, what you have stopped using, what happened the last time somebody did something to your face, and what you are actually hoping for — which is usually not what people say first. Nothing is chosen until that conversation is finished, and the answer to it is sometimes that today is not the day.

When the plan is wrong

It gets said out loud, during, rather than written in a note afterwards.

If something is not doing what was expected, you will hear about it while you are still on the bed, and what happens next is decided with you rather than for you.

What gets turned down

The day before something important, and skin that is changing.

A first appointment the day before something important, because there is nowhere to put it if it goes differently. Working over skin that is sore, broken or changing, which is a question for a doctor first. And a course of anything, when coming back twice a year would do the same thing for less of your money.
