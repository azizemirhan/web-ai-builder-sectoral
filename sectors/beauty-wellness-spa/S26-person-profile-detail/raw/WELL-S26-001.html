One practitioner, in full

How they work

  • Asks a lot at the start. What your skin has been doing, what you use at home, and what you are hoping for — before anything is decided.
  • Explains while working, if you want it. Says at the beginning that you can ask for quiet instead, and means it.
  • Writes it down. What was used and why, so the next appointment starts where this one finished rather than from scratch.
  • Changes the plan out loud. If something is not doing what was expected, you are told during, not afterwards.

What they will say no to

  • A first appointment the day before an event. Not because it will go wrong, but because there is nowhere to put it if it does.
  • Working over something that is changing. If your skin is sore, broken or doing something new, you will be asked to see a doctor first.
  • Selling you a course. If you would get the same from coming back twice a year, that is what you will be told.

Asking for them

Ask by name when you book. If they are away or full, you will be told rather than quietly given somebody else — and if somebody else would suit you better for what you are asking about, you will be told that too.
