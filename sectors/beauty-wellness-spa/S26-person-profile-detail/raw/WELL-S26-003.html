One practitioner, in full

  • How they work

    • Asks a lot first.Most of it is not about your skin.
    • Explains, or does not.You are asked at the start which you would rather.
    • Writes it down.So the next appointment starts where this one stopped.
    • Says it during.A plan that is not working is changed out loud, not in a note.

  • What they turn down

    • The day before something important.Nowhere to put it if it goes differently.
    • Skin that is changing.Sore, broken or new means a doctor first.
    • A course of anything.If twice a year would do, that is what you are told.

  • If you want the same person each time

    • Ask by name.It is written on the booking and it is kept to.
    • If they are away.You are told, and offered a choice rather than a substitute.
    • Notes travel.Whoever sees you has read what happened last time.

  • What this page does not listThree things you would normally find on a profile like this are missing on purpose, and it is fairer to say why than to leave you wondering.

  • QualificationsThey exist and you can ask to see them. They are not printed here because a list of certificates is how a treatment room starts pretending to be a clinic, and this one is not.
  • Time servedNobody here is described by how long they have been doing it. Some of the best hands in the building belong to the newest person in it.
  • Ratings and reviewsWe do not score people. Being averaged by strangers changes how somebody works, and not in the direction you would want.
