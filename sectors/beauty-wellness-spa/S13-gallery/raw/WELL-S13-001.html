The studio, on a rail

Scroll sideways — nine areas, three in view.

Why a rail rather than a grid

Nine areas in a grid is a wall of thumbnails, and a wall reads as a catalogue. Three in view at a time keeps every area large enough to be worth looking at, and the other six are a scroll away rather than shrunk to fit.
