# Section Role

The place itself. The section shows the rooms, the surfaces and the details of the studio, so a
visitor can see what they are walking into before they arrive.

It answers: *what does it look like in there?*

# The Governing Constraint — Density, Not Claims

This is the first `WELL` section in some time whose difficulty is **not** a claims problem. Nothing
here needs reserving beyond the imagery itself, and imagery is already reserved everywhere in this
workspace. The difficulty is the opposite one: a gallery is the section most likely to fail by
**putting too much in**.

`../WELLNESS-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md` states it directly:

> *Prefer fewer, larger, better-proportioned areas over many small thumbnails — a wall of treatment
> thumbnails reads as a catalogue, not as a spa.*

There is also a specific instruction on record from the user, given about the architecture sector's
gallery: **too many images, and it should be 3 × 3.** That is a stated preference about galleries in
general, not a one-off correction, and this batch carries it across:

- `001` is exactly **3 × 3**, honouring that instruction directly.
- No study exceeds nine areas, and three carry fewer than six.
- Every study is composed so its areas differ in scale, proportion or grouping. **Nine identical
  small squares would satisfy the count and still fail the rule** — the count is not the point, the
  reading is.

| Study | Areas | Why that many |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `001` | 9 | The stated 3 × 3 |
| `002` | 3 | Editorial pacing — few, large, captioned |
| `003` | 6 | Two per group across three groups |
| `004` | 4 | One dominant plus three supporting |
| `005` | 4 | A sequence, each larger than the last |

# What The Gallery Is Of

The rooms, the surfaces, the light and the details of the place. **Not results, and not products.**

| Section | What its imagery is |
| --- | --- |
| `S05` Results Before After | Paired outcome evidence, bound by the consent and claims rule |
| `S09` Technology Products | Objects — equipment and product categories |
| `S13` Gallery | The place — rooms, materials, light, hands at work |

A gallery that fills with before-and-afters has taken `S05`'s role and escaped `S05`'s constraint.
No study in this batch carries a paired comparison, an outcome caption or a treatment result.

# Consent

The rule established in `S04` and `S05` applies unchanged: **a filled slot showing a person means a
real person who has consented to appear.** Slot labels in this batch name what the area is for —
a room, a surface, hands at work — and where a person would appear, the label says so, so the
consent requirement is visible at the point it applies rather than buried in a policy document.

# Out Of Scope

- A global site header, primary navigation or footer
- Before-and-after imagery, which belongs to S05
- Equipment and product photography, which belongs to S09
- Practitioner portraits as the subject, which belong to S04
- Treatment descriptions, prices and durations
- A lightbox, carousel or slideshow requiring JavaScript

# Authoring Questions

**1. What is the primary visual element of this role?**

Proportion and interval. A gallery has no copy to compose with, so every decision is about how big
each area is, how they differ from one another, and how much air sits between them. A study that
gets those wrong cannot be rescued by its captions.

**2. What must a visitor understand immediately?**

That it is calm, that it is looked after, and roughly what the room they will be in looks like.
