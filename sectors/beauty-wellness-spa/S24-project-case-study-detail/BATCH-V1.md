## The Governing Constraint — A Case Study Is An Outcome Claim By Construction

**This is the hardest section in the sector, and the difficulty is structural.** The canonical role
asks for *"what it produced"*. This sector may not say: results imagery and outcome claims are
regulated, `S05` already forbade before-and-after evidence, and `S10` forbade inventing a client.
Remove those from a case study and it looks as though nothing is left.

Something is left, and it is more interesting than what was removed:

    The page cannot document a result. So it documents a method.
    Not "here is what we achieved" — "here is how a programme is agreed, run, and stopped."

That is a real account of capability, it is what the canonical role actually asks for under *"the
approach taken and the decisions that shaped it"*, and every word is authorable because it describes
the studio's practice rather than somebody's face.

| Element | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| The person | **Reserved token** — `Guest 01` |
| What they came in for | **Reserved area** — their account, not ours |
| Anything they said | **Reserved area** — the `S10` rule |
| Dates, reference | **Reserved fields** |
| The method | **Real**, and it is the whole page |
| A result, improvement or change | **Forbidden** |
| Before-and-after imagery | **Forbidden** |
| Number of visits, weeks, sessions | **Omitted, not reserved** |

**Why the number of visits is omitted rather than reserved.** A reserved *"visits: —"* asserts that
programmes have a fixed length. They do not — it is decided per person and revised. An empty field
that asserts a system the studio does not run is the `S10` empty-star failure and the `S17`
empty-expiry failure. The batch states the opposite instead, and `004` makes it the whole argument.

## The Canonical Interaction Note, Overridden — And Recorded

The scaffold says *"galleries, before/after comparisons and stage navigation are all plausible."*
**In this sector before/after comparison is not plausible, it is forbidden.** Recorded rather than
silently ignored: the conflict between a canonical role and a sector limitation is exactly the
research information this workspace exists to capture. Galleries and stage navigation survive; the
comparison does not.

## The Media Problem, And The Batch's Answer

The scaffold calls media **central** to this role — *"usually the largest documented media set in the
catalogue"*. The one set this role would normally carry is the one the sector forbids.

The answer: **the documented set is of the programme, not of the person** — the room, the paper
record, what was used, hands, the aftercare sheet, the empty studio. And in `005` every reserved slot
**carries its own rule about what may and may not go in it**, which turns the limitation from a
subtraction into the subject of the page.

**No slot in this batch may hold an identifiable person, a face, or a comparison of two states**, and
every slot label says so.

## Study Records

### WELL-S24-001 — Universal / Safe

- **Layout model:** The anonymised record — a facts column beside the reserved account of what the
  guest came in for, then the four stages as cards, a reserved media pair, and the limits.
- **The four stages are the batch's spine:** agreed, underway, reviewed, ended. `Ended` is the one
  that does the work — *"stopping is one of the outcomes of a review, and it costs nothing."*
- **Responsive strategy:** stages 4 → 2 → 1; the head unstacks at 768px.

### WELL-S24-002 — Premium / Editorial

- **Layout model:** Four headed prose passages at a controlled measure, one reserved plate, the
  limits at the foot.
- **The client is almost absent by design.** The subject is the studio's reasoning: why it was
  written as a plan and not a package, why one practitioner did all of it, why it changed, why it
  stopped. That is *"the decisions that shaped it"* — the only part of a case study this sector can
  tell in full.
- **It ends on a decision rather than a result, and says why.** *"You will notice this account has no
  result in it. That is not modesty and it is not an omission."*

### WELL-S24-003 — Dense / Information-heavy

- **Layout model:** A five-item metadata strip, the stage record, a two-column account of what is
  recorded against what is deliberately not recorded, and the confidentiality position as native
  disclosures.
- **The device — what is not recorded is given equal weight to what is.** A case study normally
  lists its evidence; this one lists the evidence it refuses to collect: no photographs of a face,
  no measurements, no scoring of anybody's skin, nothing we would not read out to the guest. **In a
  sector where the evidence is regulated, the record of what you refuse to record is the more
  informative half.**
- **No media at all**, leaving the media problem to `005`.
- **Correction made, recorded:** the closing line originally said inventing outcomes *"for a
  placeholder"* would be worse than leaving them out — a study describing its own placeholder status
  in visible copy, which the catalog forbids. Rewritten to say the same thing in the studio's voice.

### WELL-S24-004 — Conversion-led

- **Layout model:** A plan against a package, in two columns of equal weight, above three
  commitments, a reserved plate and one action.
- **The page sells a way of working, not a result** — which is the only thing it is allowed to sell.
  What converts is the difference between a plan and a package: nothing bought up front, stopping
  costs nothing, and the review is allowed to conclude that you should stop. **Somebody deciding
  whether to commit to a series of appointments is deciding whether they can get out of it**, and
  this page answers that first.
- **The package column is unusually blunt** — *"a number chosen before anybody knows it"*, *"reviewed
  by whoever sold it: a conversation nobody in it can win."* It is a criticism of a practice, not of
  a competitor, which is what keeps it authorable.
- **What it avoids:** no before-and-after, outcome, success rate, satisfaction figure, testimonial,
  count of visits or weeks, price, urgency, tier or saving. The `S05`, `S10`, `S12` and `S20` rules
  all hold at once, and a programme page is where every one of them would normally break.

### WELL-S24-005 — Sector-native / Distinctive

- **Layout model:** Six reserved areas in a mosaic on a stone ground, **each with its own publication
  rule written underneath it.**
- **The device:** the canonical role wants the catalogue's largest media set; this sector forbids the
  set it would normally hold. **So the slots carry the rule instead of the picture** — *"hands and
  forearms only, no face"*, *"the printed side only, nothing handwritten about a person"*, *"empty,
  no diary open on the desk."*
- **Why it works while empty:** a reviewer learns the studio's publication policy from the layout
  alone, before a single image exists. That is more than a filled gallery of stock faces would have
  told them, and no other sector would design this page this way — which is what the direction asks.
- **Largest reserved media set in the `WELL` sector** — six areas, not one of which may hold a face.
- **Correction made, recorded:** the opening line used the exact phrase *before and after*, which is
  the one phrase this sector's scan bans outright. Rewritten as *"at the start and again at the
  end"*, which says the same thing and keeps the hard ban intact. **When a refusal needs the banned
  phrase, rewrite the refusal — do not weaken the ban.**
