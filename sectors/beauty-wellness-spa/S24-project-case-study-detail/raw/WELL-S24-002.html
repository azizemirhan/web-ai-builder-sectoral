One programme, anonymised

Written as a plan, not sold as a package.

Four decisions, in the order they were taken. None of them is about a result — they are about how the work was agreed, run, changed and stopped.

Decision one · Agreed

It was written as a plan rather than sold as a course.

A course commits somebody to a number before anybody knows what the number is.

Somebody came in for something that was going to take more than one visit. The obvious thing to sell at that point is a course, paid for at the start, which is good for us and bad for them. So it was written as a plan instead — a piece of paper saying what we think will happen and what we will do first. It has no invoice attached to it, and it can be abandoned after the first visit without anybody being out of pocket.

Decision two · Underway

The same person did all of it.

Continuity is not a luxury in this work; it is most of the information.

Someone who saw a face last month can see what has changed; someone reading a note about it cannot. So the plan was written around one practitioner's diary rather than around the next available slot, which made it slower to start and better afterwards.

Still life · 21:9 · the plan, rewritten · no names, no faces

Decision three · Revised

It changed partway through, in the room.

A plan that never changes is either lucky or is not being looked at.

The plan we wrote is not the plan that was followed. It rarely is. Something in it was not doing what we expected, so it was rewritten in front of the guest, on the same piece of paper, with the old version crossed out rather than replaced quietly. Rewriting a plan in the room is uncomfortable and it is the honest version.

Decision four · Ended

It stopped, and stopping was allowed.

A programme that ends is not a programme that failed, and one that never ends is usually being sold rather than run.

A review said there was no reason to keep going, and stopping was one of the things the review was allowed to conclude. That is the part we would most like people to read.

What is not in this account

You will notice there is no result here. That is not modesty and it is not an omission — results imagery and outcome claims are regulated in this sector, and we would not print somebody's face in any case. What a page like this can honestly show is how the work is decided, so that is what it shows.
