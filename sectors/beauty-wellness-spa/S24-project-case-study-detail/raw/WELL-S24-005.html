The method, in four moments

No result, and no face. Results imagery and outcome claims are regulated in this sector, and consent to be treated is not consent to be published. What this page can honestly show is how the work was decided.

  • Agreed

    Written as a plan rather than sold as a course. No invoice attached, and abandonable after the first visit.

  • Revised

    Rewritten in the room, on the same sheet, with the old version crossed out rather than replaced quietly.

  • Reviewed

    Looked at with the guest, not reported to them. One practitioner throughout, because continuity is most of the information.

  • Stopped

    Because the review said there was no reason to keep going. A programme that ends is not a programme that failed.
