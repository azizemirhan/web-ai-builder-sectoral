If you are thinking about a programme

What you would be agreeing to, and what you would not.

A plan, which is what we do

  • Written down, and yours. A sheet of paper that says what we think will happen and what we will do first.
  • Paid for as you go. One visit at a time, so stopping never costs you anything you have already spent.
  • Revised out loud. When it stops being right it is rewritten in the room, with the old version crossed out rather than replaced quietly.
  • Reviewed on purpose. The review is in the plan from the start, and it is allowed to conclude that you should stop.

A package, which we do not sell

  • A number chosen before anybody knows it. Committing you to a length that was decided at the point of least information.
  • Paid up front. Which makes stopping expensive, and makes us the wrong people to ask whether you should.
  • Hard to change. Because changing it means unpicking something already bought.
  • Reviewed by whoever sold it. A conversation nobody in it can win.
  • Stopping costs nothingNot a fee, not a conversation you have to have twice, and not a worse appointment next time you come back.
  • The same person throughoutContinuity is most of the information in this work, so the plan is built around one practitioner rather than the next free slot.
  • Nothing is promised about youWe will tell you what we intend to do and why. What your skin does with it is not something anybody can promise, and we will not.
