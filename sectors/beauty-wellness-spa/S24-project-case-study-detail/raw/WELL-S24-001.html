What they came in for, in their words

Reserved — the guest’s own account
two or three lines

  • Agreed

    A conversation, then a plan written down that the guest keeps. Nothing is bought up front and nothing is booked past the first visit.

  • Underway

    Same practitioner each time, notes kept between visits, and what was used written down so it can be repeated or avoided.

  • Reviewed

    Reviews are built into the plan rather than requested. The plan is revised more often than it is followed to the letter.

  • Ended

    Stopping is one of the outcomes of a review, and it costs nothing. Programmes end when there is no reason to keep going.
