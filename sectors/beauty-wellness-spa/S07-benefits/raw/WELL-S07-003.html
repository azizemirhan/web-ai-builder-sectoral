What is different here

Six commitments, and what each one means

Three that hold inside the room, and three that hold around the visit. All of them are things we decide, so all of them are things we can keep.

In the room

Image area · the room

  • One guest at a time

    In practice

    The room is yours for the whole appointment. Nobody else is brought in and out around you.

  • The same hands throughout

    In practice

    Whoever you booked does the treatment from start to finish. You are not passed between people.

  • Nothing sold in the room

    In practice

    If a product would suit you, we say so afterwards. Not while you are lying down.

Around the visit

Image area · the desk

  • Unhurried appointments

    In practice

    Nothing is booked so tightly that a treatment has to be cut short to make the next one.

  • Decided when we see you

    In practice

    What gets used is chosen in the room on the day, not fixed when you booked.

  • Easy to move

    In practice

    Changing an appointment is a message, not a process. Things come up.
