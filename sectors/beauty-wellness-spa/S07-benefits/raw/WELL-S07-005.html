What is different here

Four things that hold, every visit

Things we decide, so things we can keep.

  • 01

    One guest at a time

    The room is yours for the whole appointment. Nobody else is brought in and out around you.

  • 02

    The same hands throughout

    Whoever you booked does the treatment from start to finish. You are not passed between people.

  • 03

    Nothing sold in the room

    If a product would suit you, we say so afterwards. Not while you are lying down.

  • 04

    Unhurried appointments

    Nothing is booked so tightly that a treatment has to be cut short to make the next one.
