# Section Role

What is different about coming here. The section sets out the small number of things a visitor can
count on from this studio, as distinct from what any studio offers.

It answers: *why here rather than anywhere else?*

# The Governing Constraint — And The Distinction That Resolves It

`Benefits` is the most claim-prone section name in this sector. A benefits section exists to say
what you get out of it, and "what you get out of a treatment" is precisely the efficacy language
`../WELLNESS-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md` forbids.

The distinction that makes the role authorable:

| Kind of benefit | Example | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Treatment outcome** | "Clearer skin", "reduced tension", "a younger appearance" | **Forbidden.** An efficacy claim about a body. |
| **Experience and service** | "One guest at a time", "the same practitioner start to finish", "nothing sold during a treatment" | **Allowed.** A fact about how the business chooses to operate. |

**This section is about the benefits of coming here, not the benefits of the treatment.** A studio
controls how it works; it does not control what a body does. Every benefit in this batch is
therefore an operating commitment — something the studio decides and can keep — rather than a
physiological result it would have to prove.

This is the same move as the procedural-description rule established for `S02`, applied one level
up: `S02` describes what happens in the room, `S07` describes how the room is run.

It is also the stronger position commercially. Every spa can claim relaxation; not every spa runs
one guest at a time.

**Not present in any study in this batch:** an efficacy claim, an outcome description, a
percentage, a rating, a review, a client, a superlative comparison to other studios ("the best",
"the leading"), an award, a certification, a price, a duration, or an invented statistic.

# Boundary With S08 And S14

| Section | What it owns |
| --- | --- |
| `S07` Benefits | The **qualities** of coming here — what holds true on every visit |
| `S08` Treatment Process | The **sequence** of a visit — what happens first, then next |
| `S14` About Philosophy | The studio's **beliefs and story** — why it works the way it does, in its own voice |

    "One guest at a time" is S07. "First a consultation, then the treatment" is S08.
    "We started this because..." is S14.

`S07` states the commitment. `S14` explains the thinking behind it. They must not become two
versions of the same paragraph.

# The Benefit Set

Six operating commitments, used consistently across the batch so the five studies read as one
section:

| Commitment | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| One guest at a time | The room is yours for the whole appointment |
| The same hands throughout | Whoever you booked does the treatment start to finish |
| Nothing sold in the room | If a product would suit you, we say so afterwards, not during |
| Unhurried appointments | Nothing is booked so tightly that a treatment has to be cut short |
| Decided when we see you | What gets used is chosen in the room, not at the booking |
| Easy to move | Changing an appointment is a message, not a process |

# Out Of Scope

- A global site header, primary navigation or footer
- The order of a visit, which belongs to S08
- The studio's story and beliefs, which belong to S14
- Treatments presented as the subject, which belongs to S02
- Practitioners presented as the subject, which belongs to S04
- Prices, packages and memberships, which belong to S11 and S12

# Authoring Questions

**1. What is the primary visual element of this role?**

Restraint. This is a section of short statements, and the composition has to carry it without
turning six lines of text into six identical boxes — so the visual work is in rhythm, scale
contrast and grouping rather than in imagery. Media is supporting here, not leading.

**2. What must a visitor understand immediately?**

That these are commitments rather than promises about their body, and that there are few enough of
them to be believed.
