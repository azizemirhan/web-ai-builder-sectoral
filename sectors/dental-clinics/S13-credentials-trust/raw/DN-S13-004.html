Checking us

Half of what a trust section usually lists is the law, and obeying it is not an achievement. We have marked which half.

Required by law

This is the part a competitor would put badges on. It is the law.

  • Every one of us is on the public register. Practising without it is a criminal offence.
  • Instruments are sterilised to a national standard and the log is kept.
  • The practice carries indemnity insurance.
  • There is a complaints procedure, and it is on the wall.

Not required. We do it anyway

  • Last year's complaints are published here, with what changed after each one.
  • The inspection report is linked whether it reads well or not.
  • If a treatment is the first of its kind for the person doing it, you are told before you agree.
  • Ask at the desk and you can read the sterilisation log.

No letters appear after any name on this site. They are on the public register, which is the only place they mean anything.

Most trust sections are the first list with badges around it.
