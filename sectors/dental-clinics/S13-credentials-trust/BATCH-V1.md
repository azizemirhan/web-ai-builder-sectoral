## The Problem This Section Had

Two constraints, pulling in opposite directions.

**The conventional content is reserved.** `S04` settled it four sections ago: no letters after any
name, no invented registration number, membership or inspection grade. That removes the badge wall,
the acronyms and the association marks — which is to say, everything a trust section normally is.

**And `S04` already used the obvious replacement.** *The letters after our names are on the public
register, not on this page* is `S04`'s line, about people. A `S13` built on it would be `S04` again
with a different heading.

So the section needed a subject of its own, and it had one that nobody uses.

## The Governing Idea

> **Half of what a trust section lists is the law, and obeying it is not an achievement. This one
> marks which half.**

Registration, sterilisation to standard, indemnity cover and a complaints procedure are not
distinctions. They are the conditions of being open at all, and **printing them as achievements is
claiming credit for obeying the law.** The trick is not in the copy — it is in the containers. A legal
minimum in a card with a tick on it looks exactly like a distinction, and sameness of container is
what does the persuading.

## The Two Halves

| Required by law | Not required. We do it anyway |
| --- | --- |
| Every one of us is on the public register. Practising without it is a criminal offence | Last year's complaints are published here, with what changed after each one |
| Instruments are sterilised to a national standard and the log is kept | The inspection report is linked whether it reads well or not |
| The practice carries indemnity insurance | **If a treatment is the first of its kind for the person doing it, you are told before you agree** |
| There is a complaints procedure, and it is on the wall | Ask at the desk and you can read the sterilisation log |

**The third item on the right is the load-bearing one.** Three of the four voluntary items are
decisions about publishing — real, checkable, and adoptable by a competitor in an afternoon. That one
is a rule about what happens in the room. Every dentist has a first time for every procedure, nobody
says so, it costs bookings every time it is honoured, and **there is no way to display it without also
doing it.** `002` is built entirely on that observation.

## The Five Compositions

- **`001`** — the safe reading: the two halves side by side with the same weight, labelled, and the
  argument carried by the labels rather than by the layout.
- **`002`** — the editorial cut is a judgement about which of the eight is worth the page, and it is
  the first-time disclosure. The other seven set beneath it at the weight they have earned.
- **`003`** — **the two halves are not the same kind of object.** The required four are a ruled notice
  — hairlines, no fill, no radius, the visual language of the thing already screwed to the waiting-room
  wall. The voluntary four are a raised plate on the card ground. **A reader who never reaches the
  headings can still see that one of these is not a claim.**
- **`004`** — the conversion-led reading spends the badge wall on demoting the badges. The band is the
  loudest element the theme has and the place a competitor puts its marks; here it holds the four
  compulsory facts and labels them compulsory. **The page spends its strongest visual asset taking
  credit away from itself**, and the trade is exact: this page has no badge wall, and the badge wall
  converts.
- **`005`** — **the compulsory half is set as small print, because that is what it is.** Not labelled
  as small print; set as it, one grey block at the size a footer runs.

`005` is the sector-native reading and the argument is typographic rather than editorial.
Registration, sterilisation, indemnity and a complaints procedure are **terms of trading**, and they
belong in the place a business puts its terms of trading. Every other treatment — here and across the
sector — is a decision to promote them out of it. Every other study in this batch *says* which half is
the law; this one does not need to, **because the type sizes already have.**

## Adjacent Devices, Named

`003` and `005` both give the two halves different containers, and they are not the same idea. In
`003` they are two objects side by side at comparable weight and the reader is invited to compare
them. In `005` **there is no comparison — one half is simply not competing**, which is a stronger
claim and a quieter page. The distinction is recorded here so a later batch does not collapse them.

### Found by the parity diff, before rendering

- **`004` had lost the `Required by law` label.** Its band heading — *this is the part a competitor
  would put badges on, it is the law* — carries the meaning, and the shared label had been dropped
  because the heading seemed to cover it. **This is the second occurrence of the same fault**, after
  `S10-005` delegated its commitment sentence to a moment label. The rule stated there applies
  unchanged: *a study may not delegate a shared field to its own furniture.* The label is back, above
  the heading, at label weight.

### Found only by rendering

- **`005`'s display items were set to a `30ch` measure** against a full-width reserved area beneath
  them, so the text read as a narrow strip beside a much wider picture. Widened to `38ch`. Unlike
  `S12-005`, the `ch` here resolves correctly — it is set on the `li` that carries the display size —
  so this was a proportion judgement rather than the unit fault recorded in that batch.

## What This Batch Fixes For The Rest Of The Sector

- **Separate the compulsory from the chosen.** Any section listing what a practice does should mark
  which parts it had no choice about.
- **The persuasion is in the container, not the copy.** Two facts of different kinds in identical
  cards is an argument made by the layout, and it can be refused by the layout.
- **Terms of trading belong in the register of terms of trading.** Promoting them out of it is the
  decision, and leaving them there is available as a design move.
- **A study may not delegate a shared field to its own furniture.** Second occurrence; the rule now has
  two citations.
