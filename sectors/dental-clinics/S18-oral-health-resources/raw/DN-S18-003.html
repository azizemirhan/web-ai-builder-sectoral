Looking after them

Six things in the cupboard, and three that are not.

What each is for, and who does not need it. Nothing here changed in twenty years.

  • Image area — manual brush
    A manual brush

    Soft, small head, twice a day.

    Not if you use an electric one.

  • Image area — electric brush
    An electric brush

    It does not clean better. It makes cleaning properly easier.

    Not if you already do it well by hand.

  • Image area — interdental brushes, the size range
    Interdental brushes

    Between the teeth, where a brush reaches three of five surfaces.

    Everybody. The one most people skip.

  • Image area — floss
    Floss

    Only for gaps too tight for the smallest brush.

    Not if that brush fits.

  • Image area — a pea of toothpaste
    Fluoride toothpaste

    A pea of it. Spit, do not rinse.

    Everybody. The only one not optional.

  • Image area — mouthwash
    Mouthwash

    Never straight after brushing. It rinses the fluoride off.

    Most bought, least needed.

Three that are not in the cupboard

  • Sugar

    How often, not how much. Six times in a day does more than once.

  • Smoking

    The largest thing on the list, least about teeth.

  • Bleeding gums

    Information, not normal.
