Looking after them

Six things work. This page is all of them.

All of it is free, already public, not ours, and unchanged in twenty years.

What works

  • Twice a day, with fluoride

    The one last thing at night is the one that matters.

  • Spit, do not rinse

    Rinsing washes off the fluoride you have just put on.

  • Clean between the teeth

    A brush reaches three of the five surfaces. Something else must reach the other two.

  • How often, not how much

    Sugar six times in a day does more than the same sugar once.

  • Do not smoke

    The largest thing on this list, and the one least about teeth.

  • Bleeding is information

    Gums that bleed when you brush are telling you something. It is not normal.
