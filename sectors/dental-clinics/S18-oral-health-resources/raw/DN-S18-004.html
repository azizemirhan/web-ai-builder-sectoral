And no email to leave. Twenty years of advice that has not changed does not need a mailing list, and the guide would only ever have been this page as a file.

The contents of the guide we did not write

  • Twice a day, with fluoride

    The one at night is the one that matters.

  • Spit, do not rinse

    Rinsing removes what you have just applied.

  • Clean between the teeth

    A brush reaches three of the five surfaces.

  • Sugar: how often, not how much

    Six times in a day does more than once.

  • Do not smoke

    The largest item, and the least about teeth.

  • Bleeding gums are information

    Not normal, and the earliest thing you can see yourself.

No whitening, no charcoal and no oil pulling. Each of those has a product attached to it, and nothing here is posted monthly.

The most useful document about your mouth is your own record, not an article.
