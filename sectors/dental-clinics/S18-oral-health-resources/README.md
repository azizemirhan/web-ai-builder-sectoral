# The Trap

The clinic blog. *Five tips for a whiter smile*, a stock photograph, a category tag, a date, and an
author who works at an agency. It exists to be found rather than read, and **the reason it can be
published monthly is that it is not saying anything.**

Beneath it, the lead magnet: a free guide in exchange for an email address.

# The Governing Idea

> **Everything that works is free, already public, and fits in one paragraph.**

Nothing on the list has changed in twenty years.

# The Six

| What works | The part people get wrong |
| --- | --- |
| **Twice a day, with fluoride** | The one last thing at night is the one that matters |
| **Spit, do not rinse** | Rinsing washes off the fluoride you have just put on |
| **Clean between the teeth** | A brush reaches three of the five surfaces |
| **Sugar: how often, not how much** | Six times in a day does more than the same sugar once |
| **Do not smoke** | The largest item on the list, and the least about teeth |
| **Bleeding gums are information** | Not normal, and the earliest thing you can see yourself |

**Three of the six cannot be bought.** That is the reason this section is not a shop.

# What We Will Not Publish

Whitening, because it sells and is not oral health. Charcoal and oil pulling, because **each has a
product attached to it.** Anything monthly, because nothing above changed.

# The Substitute

> **The most useful document about your mouth is your own record, not an article.**

Nothing to download, no email to leave. Ask instead, and you get what we told you, in writing, on the
day.

# Nothing Here Is Named, Dated Or Numbered

`S13` reserved named bodies, associations and brands — **a toothpaste is a brand**, so none is named in
a section whose subject is toothpaste. `S10` reserved the figures. There is no author, no byline and no
date, because a date is the device that makes unchanging advice look like news.

# Media Relationship

Areas run 2 / 0 / 6 / 1 / 3.

`003` takes the batch's `A`. The card grid a clinic publishes here is **six photographs of nothing**;
this one is six photographs of **six objects that exist**, each answering what the text cannot — what
size, what shape, how worn. **The form is the same and the content is its opposite.**

`005` reproduces everything at **actual size** on a shared baseline, because everything people get
wrong about this kit is a question of size.

`002` has none: **the claim is about length**, and a photograph would add height to a page whose point
is how little there is.
