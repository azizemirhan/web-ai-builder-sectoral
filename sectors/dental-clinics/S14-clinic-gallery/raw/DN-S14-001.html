The rooms

Six rooms, in the order you meet them.

These are in the order you will see them, and the caption on each is what happens there rather than what it is.

  • The door

    Street level, no step, and the bell is on the left.

  • The waiting room

    Where you wait if we are running late. Somebody will tell you how late.

  • The desk

    Where the plan is gone through, and where you ask to see the sterilisation log.

  • The surgery

    There are two. This is the one on the ground floor.

  • The ceiling above the chair

    The only thing you will look at for most of the appointment.

  • The sterilisation room

    Behind the desk. The one room here you will not be in.

  • Nothing was tidied for these

    Taken on an ordinary working day, in this order, and nothing was moved.

  • What is not here

    Nobody's face, no treatment in progress, nothing from a supplier's catalogue.

You will be in five of these six. The sixth you can ask to see.
