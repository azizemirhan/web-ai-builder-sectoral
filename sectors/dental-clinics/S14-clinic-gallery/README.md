# The Trap

Eight wide shots of scrubbed, unoccupied rooms at dusk, lit like an estate agent's listing. It is the
only section where every clinic spends real money and the only one where none of them says anything,
because **a photograph of an empty room answers a question nobody asked.**

# The Governing Idea

> **A room is not what it looks like. It is what happens in it, so that is the caption.**

The visitor is not choosing a room. They are trying to work out what will happen to them, and every
caption here answers that.

# The Six Rooms, In Route Order

| Room | The caption |
| --- | --- |
| The door | Street level, no step, and the bell is on the left |
| The waiting room | Where you wait if we are running late. Somebody will tell you how late |
| The desk | Where the plan is gone through, and where you ask to see the sterilisation log |
| The surgery | There are two. This is the one on the ground floor |
| The ceiling above the chair | The only thing you will look at for most of the appointment |
| The sterilisation room | Behind the desk. The one room here you will not be in |

Each caption is a fact somebody could check on the day, and each ties to a commitment made elsewhere:
the two surgeries to `S08`, the log to `S13`, the ceiling to `S01-005`.

# The Anti-Pattern, And How This Section Avoids It

The contract bans **the empty white surgery** — and a clinic gallery is largely photographs of empty
rooms, so this is the section where that ban bites hardest.

The ban is not on photographing a surgery. It is on the estate-agent shot: scrubbed, unoccupied,
over-lit, presented as evidence of quality it cannot carry. Three things keep these clear of it:

- **Taken on an ordinary working day, in this order, and nothing was moved.** Stated on the page.
- **Every caption is a fact about what happens**, never an adjective about how it looks.
- **`005` fixes it at the cause** — the vantage. A gallery is shot from where a photographer stands;
  every frame there is shot from where the visitor will be.

# What Is Not Photographed

> **Nobody's face, no treatment in progress, nothing from a supplier's catalogue.**

`S04` reserves portraits and `S05` reserves the mouth. The third refusal is this section's own.
