Smile design

Three of the five stages are reversible.

A smile design is a plan you are allowed to reject. Knowing exactly when that stops being free is the part nobody explains.

Below this line, nothing comes off.

  1. 04

  2. 05

    Fitting

    Irreversible

    Two appointments, and then they are yours.

No before-and-after gallery here. Those photographs are lit, chosen and unverifiable. The ones we can show, and the rules that apply to them, are in smile results.

Nobody is prepared on the day they first ask. The decision sits at the end of stage three, and it is the last one that costs you only the fee for the plan.
