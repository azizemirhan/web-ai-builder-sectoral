Smile design

Three of the five stages are reversible.

A smile design is a plan you are allowed to reject. Knowing exactly when that stops being free is the part nobody explains — so this page changes colour at the point it stops.

  1. 01 Reversible

    Photographs and a scan

    Twenty minutes. Nothing is touched.

  2. 02 Reversible

    The digital plan

    You see it on a screen before it exists anywhere else.

  3. 03 Reversible

    The trial smile

    Worn over your own teeth for a week or two. It comes off.

Below this line, nothing comes off. The page does not change back, and neither does this.

  1. 04 Irreversible

    Preparation

    Enamel is removed, and it does not grow back.

  2. 05 Irreversible

    Fitting

    Two appointments, and then they are yours.

No before-and-after gallery here. Those photographs are lit, chosen and unverifiable. The ones we can show, and the rules that apply, are in smile results.

Nobody is prepared on the day they first ask. The decision sits at the end of stage three, and it is the last one that costs you only the fee for the plan.
