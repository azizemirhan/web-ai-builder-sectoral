Smile design

Three of the five stages are reversible.

A smile design is a plan you are allowed to reject. Knowing when that stops being free is the part nobody explains.

Image area — the scanner used at stage one
  1. 01 Reversible

    Photographs and a scan

    Twenty minutes. Nothing is touched.

  2. 02 Reversible

    The digital plan

    You see it on a screen before it exists anywhere else.

  3. 03 Reversible

    The trial smile

    Worn over your own teeth for a week or two. It comes off.

This is the last point where no is free.

Say it here and you have lost the fee for the plan, and nothing else. Below this line, nothing comes off.

  1. 04 Irreversible

    Preparation

    Enamel is removed, and it does not grow back.

  2. 05 Irreversible

    Fitting

    Two appointments, and then they are yours.

No before-and-after gallery here. Those photographs are lit, chosen and unverifiable. The ones we can show, and the rules that apply, are in smile results.

Nobody is prepared on the day they first ask. There is no version of this where you decide in one appointment.
