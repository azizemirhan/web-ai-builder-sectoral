## The Section Role

What a smile design is, and what part of it can honestly be sold.

## The Trap

Cosmetic dentistry sells an outcome — the result photograph, the finance figure, the limited offer.
The outcome is the one thing about this work that **cannot** honestly be sold: it is not knowable in
advance, the regulator restricts saying otherwise, and the patient can tell.

The contract rules out all three. That is not a handicap; it is the brief.

## The Governing Idea

> **A smile design is a plan you are allowed to reject. Knowing exactly when that stops being free is
> the part nobody explains.**

What *can* be sold is the process, and specifically that three of its five stages are reversible: the
patient sees a plan and wears a trial before anything is touched. **The product is the last free no.**

## The Line

Between stage three and stage four, *no* stops being free. Every study draws that line, labels it with
the same sentence — **below this line, nothing comes off** — and prints the same fact beneath it:

> **Enamel is removed, and it does not grow back.**

No cosmetic dentistry page in this sector states irreversibility. *Minimal preparation* and
*conservative technique* are the two phrases used instead, and both are ways of not saying it. It is
a **material fact rather than a claim**, so it is sayable where an outcome is not, and it is the
single most trust-bearing sentence available to this section.

## The Five Stages

| | Stage | State | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Photographs and a scan | Reversible | Twenty minutes. Nothing is touched |
| 02 | The digital plan | Reversible | You see it on a screen before it exists anywhere else |
| 03 | The trial smile | Reversible | Worn over your own teeth for a week or two. It comes off |
| — | **the line** | — | **Below this line, nothing comes off** |
| 04 | Preparation | Irreversible | Enamel is removed, and it does not grow back |
| 05 | Fitting | Irreversible | Two appointments, and then they are yours |

The decision is not a stage. It **is** the line, which is why every study renders the line as a
compositional event rather than a list item.

## No Before-And-After Gallery

The contract scopes before/after to `S05` and `S24` under rules — one case, not a typical result,
never a face. This section refuses it outright and says why: **the photographs are lit, chosen and
unverifiable.** Pointing at the section that owns them, with its rules attached, is more useful than a
grid of strangers.

## The Five Compositions

- **`001`** — the safe reading: three stages, a labelled rule, two stages. The line is a mark on the
  page.
- **`002`** — cosmetic dentistry puts its best photograph at the top and it is always the result. Here
  the one image sits **on the line**, so the reader passes it and then reads *enamel is removed*.
  **A section that shows you the last reversible moment immediately before telling you it has passed
  is doing the only honest thing this subject allows.**
- **`003`** — the line is **the seam itself**: five modules share a `2px` join everywhere except
  between three and four, where the ground changes and the row inverts to ink. The break is the only
  irregular thing in an otherwise even row, which is exactly what it is in the process.
- **`004`** — **selling the exit.** The band in the middle is not the offer, it is the way out, and it
  is the largest thing on the page. This section spends its most valuable space telling the reader how
  to stop, and forfeits every enquiry that would have come from a photograph of somebody else's result.
- **`005`** — **the ground changes at the point of no return and never changes back.** The other four
  draw marks *on* a page; here the line **is** the page. The reader does not read that it is
  irreversible — they scroll into it. It is the only composition in the batch that cannot be
  reordered: move the colour change and the section stops being true.

## Media

on a screen**, and **the review appointment**. All three are named in the copy. Four studies reserve
one or two; `005` carries none because two grounds and one line are already the whole composition.

### The slot that was rejected

`002`'s reserved area was first drafted as **the trial smile on its tray, off the patient**, with a
header paragraph arguing that an object is not a mouth. **That reasoning was written to permit the
picture rather than to test it** — a trial smile photographed close up is a set of teeth whatever the
tray is doing underneath, which is exactly what the contract bans.

It was replaced with **the review appointment**, which occupies the same position, makes the same
argument, and is a room with people in it: one of the three subjects the contract actually names. The
rejected version is recorded in the study's own header so the reasoning is not repeated.

### Found only by rendering

- `005` — **the ground reverted to the lighter colour below the last paragraph**, directly
  contradicting the sentence immediately above it. The host element now carries the *after* colour and
  only the top band carries the *before* one, so any space beneath the section is already on the far
  side of the line. A study whose argument is irreversibility had been built so that it reversed.

### A note on the batch's shape

`005` is the third consecutive shape `C` at variant 005 (`S01`, `S02`, `S03`). The contract bans more
than two consecutive **A**, not **C**, and every variant page already contains a C — but *a page made
only of C is a document*, so the run is recorded here. `S04` is people and will carry portraits at
every variant, which ends it.
