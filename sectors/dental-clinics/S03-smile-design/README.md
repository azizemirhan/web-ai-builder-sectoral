# The Trap

Cosmetic dentistry sells an outcome — the result photograph, the finance figure, the limited offer.
The outcome is the one thing here that **cannot** honestly be sold: it is not knowable in advance, the
regulator restricts saying otherwise, and the patient can tell. The contract rules out all three, and
that is the brief rather than a handicap.

# The Governing Idea

> **A smile design is a plan you are allowed to reject. Knowing exactly when that stops being free is
> the part nobody explains.**

Three of the five stages are reversible: the patient sees a plan and wears a trial before anything is
touched. **The product is the last free no.**

# The Line

Between stage three and stage four, *no* stops being free. Every study draws it, labels it **below
this line, nothing comes off**, and prints the same fact beneath: **enamel is removed, and it does not
grow back.** No page in this sector states irreversibility — *minimal preparation* and *conservative
technique* are the phrases used instead, and both are ways of not saying it. It is a material fact
rather than a claim, so it is sayable where an outcome is not.

The decision is not a stage. It **is** the line, which is why each study renders the line as a
compositional event rather than a list item.

# No Before-And-After Gallery

Refused outright, with the reason given: the photographs are lit, chosen and unverifiable. `S05` owns

section points at it.

# Media Relationship

Three honest subjects exist in this process and none is a mouth: **the scanner**, **the plan on a
screen**, and **the review appointment**. All three are named in the copy. `005` carries none, because
two grounds and one line are already the whole composition.
