Where we are

One address. Nobody was ever late because they did not know it, so this is the rest of what happens on the way.

Why people arrive late

None of the three reasons is traffic.

  • There is no sign

    The door sits between two shops. Look for the number, not a sign.

  • There is nowhere to leave a car

    There is none here, the street is permit-only, and the nearest public car park is five minutes away.

  • Nobody asked which floor you needed

    There is no lift, and a stairs-only appointment found out about on the day cannot be fixed on the day.

Getting in

  • The door

    Street level, no step, and the bell is on the left.

  • The hallway

    Wide enough for a wheelchair to the ground-floor surgery.

  • The toilet

    Not accessible, and we say so when you book.

  • Hearing

    There is no loop. Ask and we will write things down.

Image area — the door from across the street

Taken from the far pavement, which is where you will be when you start looking for it. The bus stops are on the same street, on that side, and the station is about ten minutes on foot.

If any of this stops you coming, ring before you book rather than after.
