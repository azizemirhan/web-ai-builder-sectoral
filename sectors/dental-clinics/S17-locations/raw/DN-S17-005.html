Where we are

One address, and the parts of it that will not work for you.

Every clinic says accessible. This is that page with the word taken out and the specifics left in. The notes are what we do instead, and they are notes because they are not equal to the thing missing.

  • There is no lift.

    Say so when you book and all of it is done on the ground floor. No appointment is refused over it.

  • The toilet is not accessible.

    You are told when you book, not when you arrive.

  • There is no hearing loop.

    Ask and we will write things down.

  • There is no parking, and the street is permit-only.

    Nearest public car park, five minutes. Bus stops on the same street, station about ten minutes on foot.

  • There is no sign.

    The door sits between two shops. Look for the number, not a sign.

The parts that do work

The door is street level, no step, and the bell is on the left. The hallway is wide enough for a wheelchair, straight through to the ground-floor surgery.

Ring before you book. It is the only line here that asks you for anything.
