Where we are

One address, and three parts of it that are not accessible.

One address. The useful part is not where it is but whether you can get in, and this says exactly which parts you can.

Getting in

  • The door

    Street level, no step, and the bell is on the left.

  • The corridor

    Wide enough for a wheelchair to the ground-floor surgery. Not to the one upstairs, and there is no lift.

  • The toilet

    Not accessible. We will tell you that when you book rather than when you arrive.

  • Hearing

    There is no loop. Ask and we will write things down.

Getting here

  • Parking

    There is none. The street is permit-only, and the nearest public car park is five minutes away.

  • By bus and train

    The bus stops are on the same street. The station is about ten minutes on foot.

  • Finding the door

    It is between two shopfronts and people walk past it. Look for the number, not a sign.

If any of this stops you coming, ring before you book and we will tell you what we can and cannot do.
