Where we are

You will walk past the door. So here is the walk.

One address, in the order you meet it. The stairs are here because they are the part that fails.

  • One

    The corner

    On the left, about halfway down.

  • Two

    The shopfronts

    Between two shops. Look for the number, not a sign.

  • Three

    The door

    Street level, no step, and the bell is on the left.

  • Four

    The hallway

    Wide enough for a wheelchair, straight through to the ground-floor surgery.

  • Five

    The stairs

    The second surgery is up these and there is no lift. Say so when you book and you stay downstairs.

  • Six

    Reception

    The toilet here is not accessible. There is no loop either; ask and we will write things down.

Not on the walk

  • Parking

    None. The street is permit-only, and the nearest public car park is five minutes away.

  • Bus and train

    The bus stops are on the same street. The station is about ten minutes on foot.

  • Before setting out

    If any of this stops you, ring before you book.
