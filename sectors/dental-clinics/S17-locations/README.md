# The Trap

An embedded map, a pin, and the phrase *conveniently located*. The map answers *where*, **which nobody
was in doubt about**, and the phrase answers nothing at all. Underneath it, the word *accessible*, with
no indication of which parts are not.

# The Governing Idea

> **`Accessible` is a word. These are the parts that are and the parts that are not.**

# The Admissions

| The failure | What happens instead |
| --- | --- |
| **There is no lift.** The second surgery is upstairs | Say so when you book and all of it is done on the ground floor. No appointment is refused over it |
| **The toilet is not accessible** | You are told when you book, not when you arrive |
| **There is no hearing loop** | Ask and we will write things down |
| **There is no parking**, and the street is permit-only | Nearest public car park a short walk; bus stops on the same street |
| **There is no sign** | The door is between two shops. Look for the number, not a sign |

The parts that do work — street level, no step, the bell on the left, a hallway wide enough for a
wheelchair — appear in all five studies, and in none of them do they lead.

for.**
