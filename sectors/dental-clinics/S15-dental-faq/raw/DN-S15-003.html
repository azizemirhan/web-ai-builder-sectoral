What gets asked

Two voices, and the ground says which.

These are the six we are asked at the desk, ordered by how often. The first one is money, because it always is.

Asked

How much is it going to be?

Answered

Nobody can tell you before someone looks. The number comes at the end of the first appointment, in writing.

Asked

Will it hurt?

Answered

We do not know, and anyone who promises is guessing. You are told before each part, and if you raise your hand we stop.

Asked

Do I actually need this?

Answered

Sometimes not yet. The plan says which, and doing nothing is priced with everything else.

Asked

Can I stop part way?

Answered

Yes. You pay for what was done and nothing more.

Asked

How long will I be here?

Answered

About forty minutes for the first appointment. The work varies, and the plan says.

Asked

Will I see the same dentist?

Answered

Yes, unless they are away, and then we move the appointment rather than swap the person.

Three we cannot answer
  • How long a filling will last.
  • Whether you will need more work later.
  • Whether the pain comes back.

We can tell you what usually happens. Nobody can tell you what will happen to you.

An FAQ nobody asks is an advertisement with question marks in it.
