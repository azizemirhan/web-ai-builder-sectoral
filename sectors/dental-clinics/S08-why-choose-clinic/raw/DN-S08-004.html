Whether to come here

Every reason here is a fact you could check by asking. The three below are reasons to go somewhere else.

Read this part first. Go somewhere else if:

  • You need sedation

    We do not offer it. Ask, and we will give you two names.

  • You want the cheapest price

    Some treatments here cost more than at a chain, and the plan says so before you agree.

  • You need someone at two in the morning

    We are not open. The out-of-hours number is on the contact page.

  • You see the same dentist

    If they are away, we move the appointment rather than swap the person.

  • We say when something can wait

    Not everything we find needs treating now, and the plan says which.

  • Two surgeries, four of us

    Small enough that the person you saw last time is still here.

  • We write down what we did not do

    Every note records what we found and left alone, and why.
