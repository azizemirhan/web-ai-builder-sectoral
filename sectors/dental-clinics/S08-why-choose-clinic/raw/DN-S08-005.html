Whether to come here

Seven sentences, and they all start the same way.

Every reason here is a fact you could check by asking. The last three are reasons to go somewhere else.

  • If you want the same dentist every visit

    If they are away, we move the appointment rather than swap the person.

  • If you would rather be told something can wait

    Not everything we find needs treating now, and the plan says which.

  • If you want a small practice

    Two surgeries, four of us. Small enough that the person you saw last time is still here.

  • If you want to know what we left alone

    Every note records what we found and left alone, and why. We write down what we did not do.

  • If you need sedation

    We do not offer it. Ask, and we will give you two names.

  • If you want the cheapest price

    Some treatments here cost more than at a chain, and the plan says so before you agree.

  • If you need someone at two in the morning

    We are not open. The out-of-hours number is on the contact page.
