Whether to come here

Every reason here is a fact you could check by asking. The three at the end are reasons to go somewhere else.

We write down what we did not do.

Every note records what we found and left alone, and why. Three of the four reasons on this page could be copied by a competitor next week. This one is a change to the record itself.

And three more, a line each

  • You see the same dentist

    If they are away, we move the appointment rather than swap the person.

  • We say when something can wait

    Not everything we find needs treating now, and the plan says which.

  • Two surgeries, four of us

    Small enough that the person you saw last time is still here.

Go somewhere else if

  • You need sedation. We do not offer it. Ask, and we will give you two names.
  • You want the cheapest price. Some treatments here cost more than at a chain, and the plan says so before you agree.
  • You need someone at two in the morning. We are not open. The out-of-hours number is on the contact page.
