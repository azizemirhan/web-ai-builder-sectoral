## The Section Role

Whether this practice is the right one, including the cases where it is not.

## The Trap

Six icons: *Experienced Team. Latest Technology. Comfortable Environment. Flexible Hours. Affordable
Prices. Personalised Care.* Every clinic in the sector has that list. None of it can be checked, none
of it could have been otherwise, and a visitor who has read two other clinic sites this morning has
already stopped reading.

## The Governing Idea

> **The useful half of this page is the part that tells you not to come.**

**A reason is only a reason if it could have been otherwise.** Every item here is a fact somebody
could confirm by asking at the desk, and the section carries three cases where this practice is the
wrong answer.

## The Four Reasons

Shared across all five studies, so the batch varies by composition rather than by copy.

| Reason | What makes it checkable |
| --- | --- |
| **You see the same dentist** | If they are away, we move the appointment rather than swap the person |
| **We say when something can wait** | Not everything we find needs treating now, and the plan says which |
| **Two surgeries, four of us** | Small enough that the person you saw last time is still here |
| **We write down what we did not do** | Every note records what we found and left alone, and why |

**The fourth is the only load-bearing one.** Three of the four could be adopted by a competitor within
a week — a rota decision, a script, an accident of premises. Writing down what was found and left
alone is a change to the clinical record itself, and a practice that keeps that record has given a
future dentist, and a future complaint, something to read. `002` is built entirely on that
observation.

## The Three Refusals

| Go somewhere else if | |
| --- | --- |
| **You need sedation** | We do not offer it. Ask, and we will give you two names |
| **You want the cheapest price** | Some treatments here cost more than at a chain, and the plan says so before you agree |
| **You need someone at two in the morning** | We are not open. The out-of-hours number is on the contact page |

These are the three things a small practice genuinely cannot cover. A visitor who needs one of them
will otherwise find out after booking — a wasted appointment for them and a bad first visit for the
practice. Stating it costs the appointments that were going to be lost anyway and buys the only thing
this section can earn: **the reader believing the other four.**

## The Five Compositions

- **`001`** — the safe reading: four reasons in two runs, **both surgeries paired as one image**
  because the countable reason is about rooms, then the refusals on a raised white ground so they read
  as a separate object rather than as small print.
- **`002`** — the editorial cut, and it is a judgement about which reason is true. **Three of the four
  could be copied by a competitor next week; one could not.** So `We write down what we did not do`
  takes the measure at display size and the other three take a line each. **A section that weights all
  its reasons equally is telling you it does not know which one is load-bearing.**
- **`003`** — **one object, split down the middle.** Four reasons left, three refusals right, one
  seam, equal height. The reader is not sold to and then warned; they are handed a decision with both
  sides already drawn. **The filled panel is the refusals** — the accent, the loudest thing the theme
  has, goes to the half that loses the practice appointments.
- **`004`** — the conversion-led reading puts the disqualifiers first, under a heading that says to
  read them first. **A section that spends its opening on three ways to lose the reader has given up
  the entire conventional asset of the format.** The photograph moves to the foot: the first screen is
  spent on the refusals, so the room cannot be at the top.
- **`005`** — **every item begins `If you`.** Seven of them, one column, one grammar. The conventional
  section changes register between its pitch and its small print — adjectives above, hedging below —
  and holding one grammar across both refuses that. *If you want the same dentist every visit* and
  *if you need sedation* are answered in the same voice, at the same size, and **the reader finds out
  which is which by reading rather than by looking at the styling.** Only colour separates them, and
  it arrives at item five.

`005` is the sector-native reading. Fit is not a property of a clinic — it is a relation between the
clinic and the reader, and a grammar that opens `If you` puts the reader on the correct side of it.
No list of adjectives about a practice can do that.

### What was decided before any composition was drawn

The honest material for a `why choose us` section is already distributed across the sector: the
commitments are in `S01`, the register status in `S04`, the equipment in `S06`, the written plan in
`S07`. **That is precisely why the conventional version fills up with adjectives** — the facts have
already been used elsewhere, so the section has nothing left but praise.

The way out was not to restate those sections. It was to find the one thing that belongs only here:
**who this practice is wrong for.** No other section in the catalogue can carry it, and it is the only
content on this page that a competitor would not copy.

### Recorded, not chased

- `003`'s accent panel ends about 110px above the panel it is paired with, because it holds three
  items against four. Spreading the three to fill would have pushed the gaps to roughly 80px and made
  the list read as disconnected. **A filled colour field with space at its foot reads as a panel; a
  list with tripled gaps reads as a fault.** Left as is.

## What This Batch Fixes For The Rest Of The Sector

- **Name who you are wrong for.** It is the only claim on a `why choose us` page that a competitor
  will not copy, and it is what makes the rest of the page credible.
- **A reason is only a reason if it could have been otherwise.** Anything that survives being negated
  is an adjective wearing a heading.
- **Weight the reasons.** Presenting four as equals is a claim that they are, and `002` is the study
  that refuses it.
- **One grammar across the pitch and the small print.** `005` holds `If you` through both halves, and
  the register change every competitor makes between them is itself a tell.
