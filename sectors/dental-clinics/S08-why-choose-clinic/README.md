# The Trap

Six icons: *Experienced Team. Latest Technology. Comfortable Environment. Flexible Hours. Affordable
Prices. Personalised Care.* Every clinic in the sector has that list, none of it can be checked, and a
visitor who has read two other clinic sites this morning has already stopped reading.

# The Governing Idea

> **The useful half of this page is the part that tells you not to come.**

A reason is only a reason if it could have been otherwise. So every item is a fact somebody could
confirm by asking at the desk, and the section ends — or in two studies, begins — with the three cases
where this practice is the wrong answer.

# The Four Reasons

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **You see the same dentist** | If they are away, we move the appointment rather than swap the person |
| **We say when something can wait** | Not everything we find needs treating now, and the plan says which |
| **Two surgeries, four of us** | Small enough that the person you saw last time is still here |
| **We write down what we did not do** | Every note records what we found and left alone, and why |

The fourth is the load-bearing one. The other three are a rota decision, a script and an accident of
premises; **that one is a change to the clinical record itself.**

# The Three Refusals

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **You need sedation** | We do not offer it. Ask, and we will give you two names |
| **You want the cheapest price** | Some treatments here cost more than at a chain, and the plan says so before you agree |
| **You need someone at two in the morning** | We are not open. The out-of-hours number is on the contact page |

These are the three things a small practice genuinely cannot cover. A visitor who needs one will
otherwise find out after booking, which costs them a wasted appointment and costs the practice a bad
first visit.

# Not One Adjective

No *experienced*, *caring*, *friendly*, *dedicated*, *passionate* or *state-of-the-art*. No rating, no
star strip, no award, no testimonial, no *voted best*. A scan enforces it across the batch.
