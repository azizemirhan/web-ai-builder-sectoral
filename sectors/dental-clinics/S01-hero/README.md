# The Hero Test

**A hero is the one section in this sector where photography is not decoration.** It is the first
answer to *what is this place like*, which is the question a nervous visitor is actually asking, and a
hero that answers it in type has answered a different question.

So every study here opens on a reserved area, and the five differ by **what the picture does** rather
than by whether there is one. Contract rule 3 — *a section is not entitled to media just because it
could have some* — is a rule about restraint in the middle of a page, not at the top of one.

# The Governing Constraint

> **Nobody arrives at a dentist's website in a good mood.**

The sector designs for a visitor who is comparing. There is no such visitor — they are **bracing**,
and the anxiety has three sources: it will hurt, it will cost more than they said, and **they will be
judged for leaving it.** The third keeps people away for years and no competitor addresses it. So it
is the proposition: *we are not going to ask why you left it.*

# The Three Commitments

Shared by all five studies, so the batch varies by composition rather than by copy. Each is checkable
by asking, which is the only kind of claim this sector should make.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **No lecture** | What we found, not what you should have done |
| **The whole cost, in writing** | Before anything starts, including what you can leave |
| **Nobody works on commission** | Nothing is recommended because it pays better |

The second is how a study states cost **without a price**: a stated price must be complete, so what is
given is the policy instead. The one action is the same in all five — *book the first appointment* —
and so is its qualifier: an examination and a conversation, nothing done unless you ask.

# Media Relationship

The contract rules out the sector's three default photographs: the stock smile model, the open mouth,
and the instrument near a face. What is honest is **the room, the people and the named equipment.**

Every study reserves media: the surgery in use, the same room as a 21:9 band, a three-panel rail of
the waiting room and the surgery and **the dentist labelled by role, never by name**, the room
carrying the refusals band, and **the ceiling above the chair.**

**No equipment area anywhere.** The contract licenses equipment as a subject only when the copy names
it, and no hero here names a scanner or a camera. `003` had a fourth rail panel available and did not
take it.

# Interaction Notes

`003` carries a **horizontal scroll-snap rail** — a slider with no JavaScript, no autoplay and no
timer. It moves only when the visitor moves it. The other four are static. One action per study, no
form, no remote dependency.

# Responsive Considerations

The proposition must remain the first thing read at every width, and the commitments must never
collapse below the reserved area.
