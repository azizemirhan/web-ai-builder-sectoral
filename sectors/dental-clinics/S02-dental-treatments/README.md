# The Trap

Six procedure names — *implants, veneers, endodontics, orthodontics, hygiene, whitening*. That is the
clinic's vocabulary, not the patient's, and **it asks them to translate their problem into clinical
language before they are allowed to proceed.**

# The Governing Idea

> **Nobody looks up composite bonding. They look up the thing that is wrong.**

So the symptom is the title and the procedure name is the small label — the same correction `S01` made
to the register.

# The Six

Shared across all five studies. A tooth that is keeping you awake (**now**); a filling that has come
out and gums that bleed (**soon**); a missing tooth, crooked teeth, and *it has been years and you do
not know where to start* (**when you are ready**). The sixth is `S01`'s proposition arriving as an
entry of its own.

Each carries **how soon it needs looking at**, because an anxious person's first question is whether
they have left it too late. **The two that cost the most are both marked non-urgent.**

# Media Relationship

This section is where the contract's first test bites. Six symptoms **do not have six honest
photographs** — three have none, and the images a clinic reaches for to fill the gap are the three the
contract bans. So no study carries six: one anchors, one places a band at the hinge, one builds a
bento of six modules and two image cells, and two carry none. Entries that are not cards are type on
the ground.
