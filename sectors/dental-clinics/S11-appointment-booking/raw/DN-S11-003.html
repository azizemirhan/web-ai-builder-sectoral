Three ways to book. The phone is the fastest, and we would rather you used it.

  • The phone

    Answered by someone in the building, during opening hours. If you get the machine, both of us are with patients; leave a number and we ring back the same day.

  • The online form

    It goes into the same book, but nobody opens it until the morning. Use it outside hours.

  • Walking in

    We will not turn you away. You will wait, and if nothing is free that day we will book you rather than rush you.

  • What booking commits you to

    One appointment, about forty minutes. Nothing else is agreed by making it.

  • If you cannot come

    Tell us the day before and there is no charge. Less than that and we may charge for the time — said when you book, and again when you cancel, never on a later bill.
