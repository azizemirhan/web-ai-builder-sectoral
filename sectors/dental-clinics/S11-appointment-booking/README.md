# The Trap

A large button, a calendar widget and *we will get back to you within twenty-four hours.* None of it
is checkable, and none of it is what makes somebody hesitate over a dental appointment.

# The Governing Idea

> **Three ways to book. The phone is the fastest, and we would rather you used it.**

Every clinic in the sector pushes online booking, because a form costs the practice nothing to receive
and a call costs somebody stopping what they are doing. The visitor is told this is convenience. It is
not: **a form nobody opens until the morning is slower than a call answered in four rings**, and the
person sending it at eleven at night has no way of knowing that.

# No Form In Any Study

A booking form with no system behind it is a picture of a form. It would collect nothing, confirm
nothing and mean nothing. **The workspace does not draw a control it cannot honour** — the same rule
that keeps prices, credentials and testimonials off these pages.

The action in every study is a link and a phone route, both of which are real things. The scan for
this section rejects `<form>`, `<input>`, `<select>`, `<textarea>` and `<button>` outright.

# The Three Routes

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **The phone** | Answered by someone in the building, during opening hours. If you get the machine, both of us are with patients; leave a number and we ring back the same day |
| **The online form** | It goes into the same book, but nobody opens it until the morning. Use it outside hours |
| **Walking in** | We will not turn you away. You will wait, and if nothing is free that day we will book you rather than rush you |

# The Two Terms

- **What booking commits you to.** One appointment, about forty minutes. Nothing else is agreed by
  making it.
- **If you cannot come.** Tell us the day before and there is no charge. Less than that and we may
  charge for the time — **said when you book, and again when you cancel, never on a later bill.**

The second is what actually stops a booking. People who have been charged for a missed appointment
without warning do not book again, and every clinic keeps that policy on a page nobody reads until
they are being charged by it.

# No Number Is Printed

A fabricated telephone number is the same class of thing as a fabricated price. `S19` Contact owns the
number; every study here points at it rather than inventing one. No opening time is stated either, and
no response-time promise is made.

# Media Relationship

Areas run 1 / 0 / 2 / 1 / 1. The honest subjects are **the desk where the phone is answered** and
**the waiting room a walk-in waits in** — the two routes that land in a room. **The online form lands
nowhere until the morning**, so it has nothing to photograph, which is the same point the copy makes.

# Interaction Notes

One link per study. No form, no remote dependency, no JavaScript.
