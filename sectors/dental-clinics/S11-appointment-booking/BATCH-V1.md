## The Constraint This Section Ran Into

`S11` is the one section in the catalogue whose role is an action, and the sector has refused forms
since `S01`. That refusal had never been tested against a section that seems to need one.

It held, and the reason is not squeamishness. **A booking form with no system behind it is a picture
of a form** — it collects nothing, confirms nothing and means nothing, and the sector already has more
than enough elements that look like function and are not. **The workspace does not draw a control it
cannot honour**, which is the same rule that keeps prices, credentials, testimonials and platform
names off these pages.

What the section does instead is more useful than a form would have been: it names the three real
routes and says how long each actually takes. The action is a link and a phone route, both real.

## The Governing Idea

> **Three ways to book. The phone is the fastest, and we would rather you used it.**

Every clinic pushes online booking because a form costs the practice nothing to receive and a call
costs somebody stopping what they are doing. The visitor is told this is convenience. **It is not: a
form nobody opens until the morning is slower than a call answered in four rings**, and the person
sending it at eleven at night has no way of knowing that.

## The Shared Content

| Route | The honest fact |
| --- | --- |
| **The phone** | Answered by someone in the building, during opening hours. If you get the machine, both of us are with patients; leave a number and we ring back the same day |
| **The online form** | It goes into the same book, but nobody opens it until the morning. Use it outside hours |
| **Walking in** | We will not turn you away. You will wait, and if nothing is free that day we will book you rather than rush you |

| Term | |
| --- | --- |
| **What booking commits you to** | One appointment, about forty minutes. Nothing else is agreed by making it |
| **If you cannot come** | Tell us the day before and there is no charge. Less than that and we may charge for the time — **said when you book, and again when you cancel, never on a later bill** |

The clause after the dash is the load-bearing one. It is not a promise about *what* the practice
charges — that would need a figure, and `S10` settled that figures are reserved. **It is a promise
about when the practice speaks**, and it is the only version of a cancellation policy a reader can
hold anybody to.

## Nothing Is Fabricated

No telephone number, no opening time, no response-time promise. A fabricated number is the same class
of thing as a fabricated price; `S19` Contact owns it and every study here points at it. The scan
rejects digit sequences that look like numbers or times, and rejects *within twenty-four hours*.

## The Five Compositions

- **`001`** — the safe reading: three routes across, the desk beneath them, the two terms raised onto
  the card ground, then the single action.
- **`002`** — **the whole section is one claim**, so the claim takes the measure at display size and
  the routes run underneath as evidence. No image: the available subject is a desk with a telephone on
  it, and **a photograph of a telephone is an illustration of the word `phone`** in a study that is
  not short of ways to say it. Contract rule 3, applied where it bites.
- **`003`** — **the terms are not a fourth option.** A booking section usually sets its routes and its
  policy as one flat list, which makes the cancellation terms look like something you might also
  choose. So the plate has two tiers with different jobs: three cells across the top that are
  alternatives to each other, and one accent band across the foot that is not. The geometry says what
  a bulleted list cannot — *pick one of these; all of them arrive at this.*
- **`004`** — the conversion-led reading puts the thing that stops the booking first. Not the button:
  **not knowing what happens if you cannot come.** The band carries it, the action is the last thing
  on the page, and the first screen is spent on the reason not to book.
- **`005`** — **the page draws the gap, not the list.** Three blocks separated by two labelled spans —
  *until the morning*, *and then you wait* — so the distance between them is a measurable thing rather
  than a claim.

`005` is the sector-native reading. Every other treatment sets the routes as three equal items and
then asserts in words that one is faster. **A route is not an item; it is a delay with a name on it**,
and a list flattens exactly the property that distinguishes the three. The empty measure between the
phone and the form does what the sentence *the form is slower* can only claim.

## Page Rhythm

Rule 1 was not binding — `S09` and `S10` carry no shape `A`, so `A` was available and none was taken.
A booking section as an item grid would need five to eight photographs of routes, and two of the three

running with the `C` in the same place would have been its own kind of flatness.

### Found before rendering

- **`002`'s action block was two paragraphs and an empty `<span>`** — the link and its supporting line
  had been split across elements that did not belong together, with a placeholder span left in. Both
  collapsed into one block.

### Found only by rendering

- **`003`'s heading named an element the reader cannot see.** *Pick one of the three. All of them
  arrive at the band* — `the band` is the name of a `div` in this study, not a thing on the page. It
  also broke to three lines with *band.* alone. **A heading that describes the layout in the layout's
  own vocabulary has stopped addressing the reader**; replaced with *Pick one of the three. The terms
  are the same*, which says the structural point in words that work without seeing the structure.

## What This Batch Fixes For The Rest Of The Sector

- **The workspace does not draw a control it cannot honour.** Forms join figures, credentials,
  testimonials and platform names on the reserved list, and the rule now has a stated reason rather
  than being a habit.
- **A cancellation policy is a promise about when you speak, not about what you charge.** That version
  needs no figure and can still be held to.
- **A route is a delay with a name on it.** Where options differ by latency, the design should show the
  latency rather than assert it.
- **Never name a `div` in a heading.** If the copy needs the reader to see the structure to parse it,
  the copy is addressing the author instead.
