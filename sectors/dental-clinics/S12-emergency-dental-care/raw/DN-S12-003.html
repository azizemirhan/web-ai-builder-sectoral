Emergencies

The instruction is at the bottom of each one, not of the page.

Most of what wakes people at night can wait until morning. Three things cannot, and they are first.

Image area — the surgery the middle list ends up in

  • Hospital, not us

    Bleeding that will not stop. Swelling closing an eye, or making it hard to swallow. A blow to the head.

    These are not dental appointments.

  • Today, and ring first

    A tooth knocked out or knocked loose. A broken tooth with the nerve showing. Swelling in the face that is growing.

    Ring as soon as you can; for a knocked-out tooth the first hour matters most.

  • It can wait until morning

    A lost filling. A lost crown. A chipped edge that does not hurt. An ulcer. Cold sensitivity.

    Frightening at midnight, and none of it is worse at nine.

  • We are not open at night or on Sunday

    The out-of-hours number is on the contact page, answered by people who do this all night.

  • We keep two appointments free every working day

    They are for the middle list. Not bookable in advance — they are for that day.

If you are not sure which list you are on, ring. Guessing from a list is not your job.

Most of this list is us telling you not to come tonight.
