# The Trap

A red banner reading *24/7 Emergency Dental Care* over a telephone icon. Most practices that print it
are not open at night, and the person who finds that out is the one ringing at one in the morning with
a face swelling.

# The Governing Idea

> **Most of what wakes people at night can wait until morning. Three things cannot, and they are
> first.**

An emergency section is a triage or it is nothing. Its job is to get somebody to the right place, and
**the right place is often not this one.**

# The Three Lists

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Hospital, not us** | Bleeding that will not stop. Swelling closing an eye, or making it hard to swallow. A blow to the head. **These are not dental appointments** |
| **Today, and ring first** | A tooth knocked out or knocked loose. A broken tooth with the nerve showing. Swelling in the face that is growing. **Ring as soon as you can; for a knocked-out tooth the first hour matters most** |
| **It can wait until morning** | A lost filling. A lost crown. A chipped edge that does not hurt. An ulcer. Cold sensitivity. **Frightening at midnight, and none of it is worse at nine** |

The first list sends people away. A clinic that files bleeding and airway swelling under its own phone
number is competing for a patient it should be redirecting, and **the cost of getting that wrong is
not a lost booking.**

The third is the longest because most people reading at midnight are on it, and *none of it is worse
at nine* is the most useful sentence on the page — the one no competitor prints, because it talks
somebody out of ringing.

# Consistent With S08

`S08` already refused this: *you need someone at two in the morning — we are not open.* This section
does not walk that back. It states the same closure and adds what to do instead:

- **We are not open at night or on Sunday.** The out-of-hours number is on the contact page, answered
  by people who do this all night.
- **We keep two appointments free every working day.** For the middle list. Not bookable in advance —
  they are for that day.

# The Escape Hatch

> **If you are not sure which list you are on, ring.** Guessing from a list is not your job.

No triage on a website is a substitute for somebody asking questions, and every study carries this.

# Media Relationship

Areas run 0 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 0. The only honest subject is **the surgery the middle list ends up in** —
the first list goes to a hospital and the third goes back to bed. Two studies carry none: a reader in
pain is not browsing, and **every pixel spent on a photograph is a pixel between them and the line
they need.**
