Do not take any of this on trust. Three ways to check it.

  1. 01 The public register

    The letters are on it, not on this page. Look us up.

  2. 02 Ask who you are booked with

    If the name changes between visits, we did not keep this.

  3. 03 Ask for the referral note

    Anything leaving the practice comes with one, addressed to a name.
