# The Trap

A grid of headshots, a string of letters nobody can parse, and a line about being passionate. None of
it answers either question a patient has: **who will see me**, and **is this person allowed to do
this?**

# The Governing Idea

> **Specialist is a protected title, and most clinics use it loosely.**

Some of these people are on a specialist register and some are not, and the page says which —
**including the principal, who is not.** A team page that marks its own most senior name as *not a
specialist* is the only kind whose specialists mean anything. The hygienist carries the third status,
*not a dentist*, which is the one clinics blur hardest.

# Credentials Are Reserved

No study prints letters after a name. What every study prints instead is where to check: **the letters
after our names are on the public register, not on this page.** That cannot be fabricated and is more
use than the letters would have been.

# Media Relationship

**The first section in this sector where photography is the honest answer** — people are one of the
three subjects the contract names, so media in every item is correct rather than forced, and this is
the sector's first shape `A`. Every portrait label names the **role**, never the placeholder person,
and no placeholder person is given a gendered pronoun.
