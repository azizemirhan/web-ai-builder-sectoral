Paying for it

Your policy is between you and your insurer. We are not part of it, and what you owe us does not change if they say no.

If the claim is refused

You owe us the same amount.

And we will have told you that amount before you agreed to anything. The risk is real. The surprise is not.

Between you and your insurer

  • We are not on anyone's panel

    You are treated here, you pay here, and you claim it back yourself.

  • What policies usually will not cover

    Cosmetic work, anything started before the policy began, and the excess.

Between you and us

  • The paperwork you need

    An itemised receipt with the treatment codes on it, on the day, without asking.

  • We do not arrange credit

    No third party, no commission to us, and nobody's application to fill in.

  • If you need to spread it

    We do it ourselves. Same total, and it stops when you stop.
