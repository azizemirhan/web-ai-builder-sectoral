## The Constraint That Designed This Batch

The contract bans attributed testimonials, and the workspace does not invent statements by real
people. **So the one thing this section conventionally contains could not be authored at all** — not
as placeholder, not marked, not in any form. A placeholder testimonial is a fabricated endorsement.

That constraint turned out to be the section's argument rather than an obstacle to it. A clinic
cannot honestly show reviews it selected, and a workspace cannot honestly write reviews it invented.
**Both are the same refusal**, and the honest page is the one that says what a review is evidence of
instead of performing five of them.

The platform name is reserved on the same principle — naming one would assert a business relationship
that does not exist. Every study writes `the public listing`.

## The Governing Idea

> **A review can tell you how you will be treated. It cannot tell you whether the dentistry was any
> good.**

A patient can judge the wait, the phone, the explanation and the bill. They cannot judge whether a
filling was placed well, and neither can the next reader. **A wall of five-star cards is a wall of
evidence about reception**, presented as evidence about dentistry — a category error the sector makes
on purpose.

## What A Review Reaches

Shared across all five studies, so the batch varies by composition rather than by copy.

| A review can tell you | It cannot tell you |
| --- | --- |
| How long you waited | Whether the filling was placed well |
| Whether the phone was answered | Whether the crown fits your bite |
| Whether anyone explained what they were doing | Whether the treatment was needed at all |
| Whether the bill matched the plan | |

> **None of that can be judged from the chair, including by the person in it.**

That last clause is the one that keeps the section from being a complaint about patients. The limit is
not that reviewers are unqualified; it is that **nobody in that position can see the work**, which is
the same asymmetry `S01-005` named for the whole sector.

## The Four Policies

- **They are not here.** They are on the public listing, where we cannot delete one or move it up.
- **We do not ask at the chair.** Asking somebody while they are still numb is how a high average gets
  built.
- **The wrong ones stay up.** Including the ones we think are unfair.
- **We reply under our own name.** Never anonymously, and never twice to the same one.

The second is the expensive one and `004` is built on it. The reliable way to manufacture a rating is
to ask at the moment of maximum compliance — a tablet at the desk, or the question asked while
somebody is still numb and relieved it is over. **Nobody says no in that chair.** A practice that
refuses that moment is choosing a lower average on purpose.

## The Five Compositions

- **`001`** — the safe reading: the two lists side by side, the room that is the reachable half, then
  the four policies on a raised ground. Nothing is argued that the two lists do not already show.
- **`002`** — the editorial cut leads with the sentence the format cannot survive: **whether the
  filling was placed well is not in any of them.** Everything after is consequence. The four
  reachable things are listed plainly rather than dismissed — **the point is not that reviews are
  worthless, it is that they are evidence about reception.**
- **`003`** — **the grid keeps going and the cards do not.** Four cells are filled cards; the next
  three are the same cells with the fill taken out, same size, same row rhythm; the eighth carries the
  reason. Nothing is styled as a warning — the module is simply not there, and a reader scanning the
  row watches the page run out of evidence before it runs out of grid.
- **`004`** — the conversion-led reading gives up the number every visitor scans for. The rating badge
  is the highest-converting element on a clinic site and it is manufactured; the band says so and
  refuses the method. The photograph moves to the foot because the first screen belongs to the
  refusal.
- **`005`** — **there is no list.** The seven items run as one continuous paragraph at display size,
  the accent starting mid-sentence at *it cannot tell you*, so the reader is already inside the
  sentence when the ground shifts under them.

`005` is the sector-native reading. **A list invites the reader to count, and counting is the wrong
operation** — four reachable things against three unreachable ones is not a score of four to three. It
is one continuous claim about where knowledge ends, and prose is the form that holds it. The
paragraph is the largest thing on the page, which is the correct weighting for a section whose entire
content is an argument rather than an inventory.

## Page Rhythm

Rule 1 was not binding — `S07` and `S08` carry no shape `A`, so `A` was available and none was taken.
An item grid with media in every item would have needed seven photographs of things that cannot be
photographed.

Rule 2 is already satisfied at every variant after `S08`, so this batch owed nothing. **`005` is a `C`
by choice rather than by debt**, and the batch is better for it: five studies at shape `B` with one
reserved area each would have been the flattest batch in the sector.

### Found before rendering

- **`003` repeated its two labels in every cell** — *A review can tell you* four times and *It cannot
  tell you* three times, twenty-four words of pure repetition in a study whose whole device is that
  **the fill already tells you which kind a cell is.** Only the first cell of each kind is labelled

### Found only by rendering

- **`003`'s eight bodies did not sit on one line.** Removing the repeated labels left cell one with a
  label and cells two to four without, so the first card's sentence sat lower than its neighbours. The
  label row is now reserved in every cell whether it holds a label or not. **This is the third time in
  the sector a shared row has broken because one item carried an extra element** — after `S06-003` and
  `S06-005`. The rule is now explicit: *reserve the row, do not rely on the content to fill it.*

### Changed after rendering, on judgement rather than defect

- **`005` was authored as shape `B` with one reserved area and is now `C`.** With all five studies at
  `B` and exactly one area each, the batch had no shape variety at all, and the photograph was the one
  cell on a page whose argument is that cells are the wrong container. Dropping it made the study
  better and the batch less flat at the same time. Word count moved 190 to 182, still in band.

## What This Batch Fixes For The Rest Of The Sector

- **Say what a piece of evidence is evidence of.** It is the same move `S05` made for photographs and
  `S06` made for machines, applied to the format that resists it hardest.
- **A placeholder testimonial is a fabricated endorsement.** Patient quotations join credentials and
  faces on the reserved list, and so do platform names.
- **Reserve the row, do not rely on the content to fill it.** Third occurrence; now a standing rule for
  any grid whose items share a horizontal line.
- **A batch with no shape variety is a defect even when every study passes.** Check the batch, not only
  the studies.
