Reviews are about the parts of a visit you could see. The dentistry is not one of them, and this page says so rather than showing you five of the good ones.

Image area — the desk and the waiting room

The half a review can reach.

A review can tell you

  • How long you waited.
  • Whether the phone was answered.
  • Whether anyone explained what they were doing.
  • Whether the bill matched the plan.

It cannot tell you

  • Whether the filling was placed well.
  • Whether the crown fits your bite.
  • Whether the treatment was needed at all.

None of that can be judged from the chair, including by the person in it.

  • They are not here. They are on the public listing, where we cannot delete one or move it up.
  • We do not ask at the chair. Asking somebody while they are still numb is how a high average gets built.
  • The wrong ones stay up. Including the ones we think are unfair.
  • We reply under our own name. Never anonymously, and never twice to the same one.

The half a review cannot reach is the half you are paying for.
