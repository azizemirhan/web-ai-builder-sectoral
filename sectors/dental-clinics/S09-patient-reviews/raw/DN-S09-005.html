What patients say

There are no reviews on this page.

Reviews are about the parts of a visit you could see. The dentistry is not one of them, and this page says so rather than showing you five of the good ones.

A review can tell you how long you waited, whether the phone was answered, whether anyone explained what they were doing, and whether the bill matched the plan. It cannot tell you whether the filling was placed well, whether the crown fits your bite, or whether the treatment was needed at all. None of that can be judged from the chair, including by the person in it.

So the reviews are not here. They are on the public listing, where we cannot delete one or move it up. We do not ask at the chair, because asking somebody while they are still numb is how a high average gets built. The wrong ones stay up, including the ones we think are unfair. And we reply under our own name, never anonymously, and never twice to the same one.
