What patients say

Reviews are about the parts of a visit you could see. The dentistry is not one of them, and this page says so rather than showing you five of the good ones.

We do not ask at the chair.

Asking somebody while they are still numb is how a high average gets built. Nobody says no in that chair, so we do not put them in the position of saying it.

  • They are not here

    They are on the public listing, where we cannot delete one or move it up.

  • The wrong ones stay up

    Including the ones we think are unfair.

  • We reply under our own name

    Never anonymously, and never twice to the same one.

A review can tell you

  • How long you waited.
  • Whether the phone was answered.
  • Whether anyone explained what they were doing.
  • Whether the bill matched the plan.

It cannot tell you

  • Whether the filling was placed well.
  • Whether the crown fits your bite.
  • Whether the treatment was needed at all.

None of that can be judged from the chair, including by the person in it.
