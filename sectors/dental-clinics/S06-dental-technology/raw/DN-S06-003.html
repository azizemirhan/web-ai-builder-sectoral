    The scanner

  • the 3D X-ray unit

    The 3D X-ray

    Replaced a referral to a hospital scanner. One appointment instead of two.

  • the milling unit, running

    The milling unit

    Replaced the temporary crown and the second visit. Nothing to come loose over the weekend.

    It does not suit every crown.
