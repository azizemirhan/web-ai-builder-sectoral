Equipment

Four machines, and what each one replaced.

Every machine here replaced something. That is the only reason it is on this page. What each one cannot do is printed underneath it.

  • The scanner

    Replaced the putty impression tray. Nothing sets in your mouth, and the scan takes about four minutes.

    It cannot see under the gum.

  • The 3D X-ray

    Replaced a referral to a hospital scanner. One appointment instead of two.

    It uses more radiation than a flat X-ray, so we take one only when a flat picture cannot answer the question.

  • The milling unit

    Replaced the temporary crown and the second visit. Nothing to come loose over the weekend.

    It does not suit every crown.

  • The screen at the chair

    Replaced our description of what we found. You see it while we are looking at it.

    A picture is not a diagnosis. Someone still has to read it.

None of it makes a filling last longer than the hands that place it.
