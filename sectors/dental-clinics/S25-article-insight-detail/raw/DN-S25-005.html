Make room for your questions.

A few thoughts on putting what matters to you into words, before and during a dental appointment.

Author[name pending] Published[date pending] Editorial draft

A question does not have to sound polished to be worth asking.

One

Start with one thing

You do not need a perfectly organised list. Begin with the question you most want answered.

It might be about the appointment itself, a possible next step, or something you found difficult to ask before.

Two

Use your own words

Everyday language is enough. You can write a short note or bring a question with you.

If an explanation is unclear, ask for it in a different way.

Three

Before you leave

Return to the question you started with. Is there anything you still want explained?

Keep a note of the next step discussed and any questions you would like to revisit.
