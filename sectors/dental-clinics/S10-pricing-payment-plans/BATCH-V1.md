## The Constraint

The contract requires a stated price to be complete and the workspace does not invent one. **A
placeholder price is a fabricated quote**, the same class of thing as a placeholder credential or a
placeholder testimonial, and no `data-placeholder` marking makes one safe to draft.

So the section had to be genuinely useful with no figure on it at all. It is, and the reason is that
**a pricing section is answering *what will this cost me*, and the honest answer has a time in it**
rather than a number. `S01` set the convention for the sector: state the policy, not the number.

## The Governing Idea

> **A number you could read before anyone has looked is not a price.**

The sector's device is the *from* figure — chosen because it is the least anybody has ever paid,
printed to win a comparison a visitor is making across four tabs. It is not exactly a lie. It is a
number that describes almost nobody, and every reader who has paid a dental bill already knows that.

## The Shared Content

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **One price, and it does not change** | The examination, the hygienist, an X-ray, the emergency appointment. Ask on the phone and you will be told |
| **Cannot be quoted until somebody looks** | A filling, a crown, an implant, aligners. What they cost depends on how much tooth is left, how many surfaces, and whether the root is involved. Nobody can know that from a website |
| **When** | The number comes at the end of the first appointment, in writing, before anything starts. Every option is on it, including doing nothing |
| **What moves it** | What is under an old filling · A material you choose after seeing both · **Nothing else** |
| **Paying** | You can pay in instalments, and the total is the same either way · If you stop part way, you pay for what was done and nothing more |

The third mover is the load-bearing one. **A list of three things that can change a quote is only
worth printing if the list is closed**, and `Nothing else. If it is not on this list, it does not move
the number` is what closes it.

## The Five Compositions

- **`001`** — the safe reading: the two kinds side by side, the commitment raised onto the card
  ground, the three movers as a row, and paying as a closing pair.
- **`002`** — **the editorial judgement here is the opposite of `S09`'s.** *There are no prices on this
  page* is true and it is not the headline. A reader arriving at a pricing section wants an answer to
  *when will I know*, not a rebuttal of the sector's habits — so the display line is the commitment
  and the refusal is demoted to the supporting line. `S09` leads with its refusal because the question
  there is *should I trust this*. **The question here has an answer, so the answer leads.**
- **`003`** — this section is about a document, and every other treatment describes it. **This one is
  shaped like it**: one plate, ruled bands, a label in the margin and content beside it. The bands are
  deliberately not uniform — the commitment band is the accent and interrupts the run, because in the
  real document that line is a cover sheet rather than a row, and the band after it subdivides into
  three cells. That non-uniformity is what keeps the plate an object rather than a table.
- **`004`** — **the conversion device is the ceiling, not the floor.** Not *how little it might be* but
  how little it can move once you have been told: three named things and then nothing. A quote that
  cannot be added to is worth more than a low number that can. The trade is severe — **the page gives
  up the comparison entirely**, because a visitor sorting four clinics by advertised price will not
  find this one.
- **`005`** — **the page is a timeline of what you know, not of what we do.** `S07` draws the treatment
  as five stages; this draws the same span as three moments of certainty about the cost — *right now*,
  when the honest answer is nothing; *then*, when it is everything in writing; *after that*, when only
  three named things can move it.

`005` is the sector-native reading. Every pricing section answers *how much*; the question underneath
is *when will I stop being in the dark*, and that one has a precise answer the practice can be held
to. **Printing the first moment — you know nothing, and neither do we — is what makes the second
credible.** A section that will not admit the state the reader is currently in has no standing to
describe the state they are about to be in.

### Found by the parity diff, before rendering

**Six failures, four of them real, and all four in `005`.** The study rephrased its material into the
three-moments structure and lost content doing it:

- **`Ask on the phone and you will be told` had gone** — replaced by *can be read out on the phone*,
  which says less and is not the shared sentence.
- **`Nobody can know that from a website` had gone entirely.** It is the reason the second list exists
  and the study had dropped it.
- **The commitment had lost its timing.** `005` read *the number comes, in writing, before anything
  starts*, with *at the end of the first appointment* carried only by the moment's label. **The label
  is 0.76rem uppercase and the sentence is the parity field**; a study cannot delegate a shared clause
  to its own furniture.

Restoring all three took `005` to 230, exactly the ceiling. The margin came from the label that had
been doing the sentence's job: *After the first appointment* became *Then*, which removes the
redundancy the restoration created and leaves the study at 227.

The other two failures were the checker's, not the studies': two shared sentences begin a clause in
some studies and a sentence in others, and the regexes were case-sensitive on the first letter. Fixed
in the checker.

## What This Batch Fixes For The Rest Of The Sector

- **A placeholder price is a fabricated quote.** Figures join credentials, testimonials, faces and
  platform names on the reserved list.
- **The honest answer to *what will it cost* has a time in it.** State when the number arrives and
  what can move it, not what it might be.
- **A list of things that can change a quote is only worth printing if it is closed.** `Nothing else`
  is the entry that makes the other two mean something.
- **A study may not delegate a shared sentence to its own labels.** If the parity field is a sentence,
  it appears as that sentence, at body weight, in every study.
