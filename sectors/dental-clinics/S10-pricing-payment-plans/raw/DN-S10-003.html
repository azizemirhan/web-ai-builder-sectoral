What it costs

Shaped like the thing you will be handed.

There are no prices on this page. A number you could read before anyone has looked is not a price.

When

The number comes at the end of the first appointment, in writing, before anything starts.

Every option is on it, including doing nothing.

Fixed

The examination, the hygienist, an X-ray, the emergency appointment.

One price, and it does not change. Ask on the phone and you will be told.

Not yet

A filling, a crown, an implant, aligners.

What they cost depends on how much tooth is left, how many surfaces, and whether the root is involved. Nobody can know that from a website.

What moves it

  • What is under an old filling

    We cannot see it until it is out. If there is more, we stop and re-quote before continuing.

  • A material you choose after seeing both

    Same work, different price, and you choose.

  • Nothing else

    If it is not on this list, it does not move the number.

Paying

  • You can pay in instalments, and the total is the same either way.
  • If you stop part way, you pay for what was done and nothing more.

The only honest number is the one written after someone has looked.
