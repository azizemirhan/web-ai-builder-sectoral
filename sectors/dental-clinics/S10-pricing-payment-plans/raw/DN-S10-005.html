What it costs

Three moments. Only the middle one has a number.

There are no prices on this page. A number you could read before anyone has looked is not a price.

  • Right now

    You know nothing, and neither do we.

    The examination, the hygienist, an X-ray, the emergency appointment. Ask on the phone and you will be told. A filling, a crown, an implant, aligners. What they cost depends on how much tooth is left, how many surfaces, and whether the root is involved. Nobody can know that from a website.

  • Then

    The number comes at the end of the first appointment, in writing, before anything starts.

    Every option is on it, including doing nothing.

    Image area — the room where the plan is gone through

  • After that

    Three things can move it, then nothing.

    • What is under an old filling. We cannot see it until it is out. If there is more, we stop and re-quote before continuing.
    • A material you choose after seeing both. Same work, different price, and you choose.
    • Nothing else. If it is not on this list, it does not move the number.
  • You can pay in instalments, and the total is the same either way.
  • If you stop part way, you pay for what was done and nothing more.

The only honest number is the one written after someone has looked.
