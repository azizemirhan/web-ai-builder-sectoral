## The Risk This Batch Had To Clear First

`S03` Smile Design is already a five-stage sequence in this sector. A treatment-journey section is the
single likeliest place in the whole catalogue to author an accidental near-duplicate, so the
separation was decided before any composition was drawn:

| | `S03` Smile Design | `S07` Treatment Journey |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Argument | **Where the plan becomes irreversible** | **Who does what** |
| Stage set | consultation, plan, trial, prep, fit | first appointment, written plan, work, healing, check |
| The break | the ground changes at stage four and does not change back | the clinic's half of stage four is the word `Nothing` |
| Fields | what it is, whether it can be undone | what we do, what you do, how long |

No governing idea, stage name, field or composition is shared between them.

## The Section Role

What a course of treatment actually consists of, on both sides.

## The Trap

Four or five numbered circles with icons, all the same size, all describing things the clinic does.
The patient appears in none of them except as the thing being worked on.

## The Governing Idea

> **Every step here has two halves, and the second one is yours.**

Half of a course of treatment is the patient turning up, reading a plan, saying no to part of it, and
doing the aftercare — and that half takes longer and decides more than the clinic's half. **Stage four
proves it: our half of it is the word `Nothing`.**

## The Five Stages

Shared across all five studies, so the batch varies by composition rather than by copy.

| Stage | Us | You | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| The first appointment | Look, and write down what we found | Say what you have noticed, and what you are worried about | about forty minutes |
| The written plan | Price every option, including doing nothing, and post it | Read it, and say no to any part of it | as long as you want |
| The work | The agreed work, in the agreed order, stopping when you raise your hand | Turn up, and tell us if anything has changed | two visits, about an hour each |
| **Healing** | **Nothing.** | The aftercare. This is the half that decides how it settles | **six weeks** |
| The check | Look again, and say whether it worked | Come back even if it feels fine | about twenty minutes |

## The Arithmetic

> **Nine weeks from the first appointment to the check. Three hours of it in our chair.**

In all five. The two figures are consistent with the five stage times — forty minutes, two hours of
work, twenty minutes — so a reader who adds them up gets the same answer. **A checkable number that
does not survive being checked is worse than no number**, which is why the durations were set to the
total rather than the other way round.

## No Lecture

`S01` commits the sector to *what we found, not what you should have done*, and a section that lists
the patient's obligations is where that commitment is easiest to break. Every line in the `You` lane
states what the visit asks for; none states what the reader failed to do. The scan checks for *should
have*, *ought to*, *neglect* and *failed to* and finds none.

*The aftercare. This is the half that decides how it settles* is the closest line to the edge. It
states a causal fact about the future, not a judgement about the past, and it stays.

## The Five Compositions

- **`001`** — **two horizontal lanes**, ours running above a rule and yours below it, with the five
  stage names sitting on the rule. Both lanes press against the rule, so it reads as the spine of two
  tracks rather than a divider between two stacks. `Nothing.` sits directly above the rule at stage
  four with the lane empty behind it.
- **`002`** — the editorial cut. A journey set as five equal boxes asserts that the five steps are
  comparable; **they are not, and setting them equal is not neutral — it is a claim, and it is
  false.** Four stages compress to a line each and **Healing breaks out across the measure at display
  size**, with `Nothing.` printed at the same size as the sentence beside it.
- **`003`** — **the seam moves once.** Two lanes held apart by one continuous vertical seam, so the
  plate reads as a division of labour rather than five records with two fields each. At stage four the
  seam jumps left: the clinic's cell collapses to a strip wide enough for one word and the patient's
  cell takes the rest of the plate. **Nothing about that row is styled differently — the geometry does
  it.**
- **`004`** — the conversion-led reading, and the fear it answers is not price or pain. It is that
  walking in for a check-up ends with three thousand pounds of agreed work and no way back out. So the
  band answers it before the journey starts: **booking commits you to forty minutes.** Stage two is a
  plan you can decline in full; stage three does not start until you reply. **A section that says the
  first appointment commits you to nothing has given up the assumed sale** — the competitor's version
  implies a funnel, this one prints the exits.
- **`005`** — **the proportions of the genre, reversed.** The patient's half is the display type and
  the clinic's half is a footnote beneath it. Read down the large type and the page is a list of
  things you will do, which is what a course of treatment is from inside it.

`005` is the sector-native reading, and stage four is where the inversion pays: a full display
sentence about the aftercare, and directly beneath it, in the smallest type on the page, `Us Nothing.`
**The punchline of the section is set at footnote size — exactly where a competitor would have put it
if they had printed it at all.** The check the reader performs is on the layout itself: count how much
of the page is you.

## Page Rhythm

**Rule 1 was not binding here.** `S06` broke the `A` runs at variants `001`, `003` and `005`, so shape
`A` was available at every variant. None was taken: a journey is a sequence of times, and an item grid
with media in every item would have required photographing five stages, four of which have nothing to
photograph.

**Rule 2 — one more debt paid.** Variant `001` had no shape `C` across `S01`–`S06`. `001` is the `C`
here, and the reason is native rather than retrofitted: **a journey is a sequence of times, not
places.** Four of the five stages have no subject, and the fifth would be the surgery that `S01` and
`S06` already show.

Variant `003` is now the only one still owing a `C`. `002` took its from `S06`, `004` and `005` from
`S02` and `S03`.

## Media Relationship

Areas run **0 / 1 / 2 / 1 / 1**, and the healing stage is unphotographed in all five. The only
available picture of six weeks of nothing is an empty surgery, which the contract bans outright as an
anti-pattern — so the batch's most important stage is the one with no image anywhere, which is itself
consistent with what the stage is.

`003` places both of its areas in the clinic's lane and says so in its header: **the clinic's half
happens in two rooms and can be photographed; the patient's half happens somewhere this practice will
never see** — at home, at work, over six weeks. The lane with no pictures is the lane the sector
leaves out.

### Found by writing the checker, before rendering

- **`003` had a `<ul>` with `<span>` and `<i>` as direct children** — the lane keys. Invalid markup
  that renders correctly, so no screenshot would have caught it. The element is a `<div>` now, and the
  scan gained a check for non-`<li>` children directly inside a list.

### Found only by rendering

- **`001`'s two lanes floated free of the rule.** Each lane's text sat at the outer edge of its track,
  so the rule read as a divider between two stacks rather than as the spine of two tracks. The lanes
  now press against it — `align-content: end` above, `start` below — and `Nothing.` lands directly on
  the rule at stage four, which is where the study wanted it.
- **`003`'s right lane looked unfinished**, not empty-on-purpose: the rows with images in the left
  lane run 300px tall, leaving the patient's sentence stranded at the top of a blank cell. Centred.
  The emptiness is the argument, but it has to read as placed rather than left over.

### A note on where the stage name sits in `003`

In four rows the stage name and duration sit in the clinic's cell; in the healing row they move to the
patient's. That is deliberate and it is the only content that moves lanes anywhere in the batch — at
the stage where the labour is entirely the patient's, the stage belongs to them. It was checked
against the alternative (keeping the name in a collapsed 30% strip beside `Nothing.`) and rejected:
the strip cannot hold a heading, and putting the name there would have made the narrow cell look like
a rendering fault rather than a statement.

## What This Batch Fixes For The Rest Of The Sector

- **Two lanes, and the second one is the reader's.** Any section that describes a process in this
  sector states the patient's half at the same weight as the clinic's.
- **A checkable total must survive being checked.** The nine weeks and the three hours add up from the
  five stage durations; the figures were set from the total rather than asserted beside it.
- **Stating what someone must do is not the same as telling them what they should have done.** The
  `No lecture` commitment survives a section built out of the reader's obligations, and the scan now
  enforces it.
- **A picture that would have to be an empty room is not a picture.** Where the honest subject does
  not exist, the area is dropped rather than filled.
