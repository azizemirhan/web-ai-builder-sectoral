# The Trap

Four or five numbered circles with icons, all the same size, all describing things the clinic does.
The patient appears in none of them except as the thing being worked on.

# The Governing Idea

> **Every step here has two halves, and the second one is yours.**

Half of a course of treatment is the patient turning up, reading a plan, saying no to part of it, and
doing the aftercare — and that half takes far longer and decides far more than the clinic's half.
Every study is two lanes, and stage four proves the point: **the clinic's half of it is the word
`Nothing`.**

# How This Differs From S03

`S03` Smile Design is also a five-stage sequence, and its argument is **where the plan becomes
irreversible.** This section's argument is **who does what**, and its punch is the stage with no
appointment in it. Neither the governing idea, the stage names, the field set nor any composition is
shared between the two.

# The Arithmetic

> **Nine weeks from the first appointment to the check. Three hours of it in our chair.**

Present in all five. Every journey diagram implies the clinic is present throughout; the division is
roughly one part in a hundred and no competitor prints it.

# The Five Stages

| Stage | Us | You |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first appointment | Look, and write down what we found | Say what you have noticed, and what you are worried about |
| The written plan | Price every option, including doing nothing, and post it | Read it, and say no to any part of it |
| The work | The agreed work, in the agreed order, stopping when you raise your hand | Turn up, and tell us if anything has changed |
| **Healing** | **Nothing.** | The aftercare. This is the half that decides how it settles |
| The check | Look again, and say whether it worked | Come back even if it feels fine |

# No Lecture

`S01` commits the sector to *what we found, not what you should have done.* This section states the
patient's half without a single reproach: it says what the visit asks of them, never what they failed
to do. A scan confirms no *should have*, *ought to*, *neglect* or *failed to* anywhere in the batch.

# Media Relationship

Areas run 0 / 1 / 2 / 1 / 1. **A journey is a sequence of times, not places** — four of the five
stages have nothing to photograph, and the healing stage's only available picture is an empty
surgery, which the contract bans outright. Where media appears it is the room the visitor will sit
in, attached to the one stage that has a place.

`003` puts both of its areas in the clinic's lane deliberately: **the clinic's half happens in two
rooms and can be photographed; the patient's half happens somewhere the practice will never see.**
The asymmetry of the media is the asymmetry the copy is about.
