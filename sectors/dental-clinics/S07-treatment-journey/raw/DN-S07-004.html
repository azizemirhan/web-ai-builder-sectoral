What happens

Every step here has two halves, and the second one is yours. That half is missing from every journey diagram in this sector.

Booking commits you to forty minutes.

Stage two is a plan you can decline in full. Stage three does not start until you reply. Nothing further happens because you booked stage one.

Image area — the consulting room

Nine weeks from the first appointment to the check. Three hours of it in our chair.

  • The first appointment

    about forty minutes

    Look, and write down what we found.

    Say what you have noticed, and what you are worried about.

  • The written plan

    as long as you want

    Price every option, including doing nothing, and post it.

    Read it, and say no to any part of it.

  • The work

    two visits, about an hour each

    The agreed work, in the agreed order, stopping when you raise your hand.

    Turn up, and tell us if anything has changed.

  • Healing

    six weeks

    Nothing.

    The aftercare. This is the half that decides how it settles.

  • The check

    about twenty minutes

    Look again, and say whether it worked.

    Come back even if it feels fine.

The longest step is the one where we do nothing.
