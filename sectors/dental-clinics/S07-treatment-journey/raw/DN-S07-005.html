What happens

Read the large type. All of it is you.

Every step here has two halves, and the second one is yours. That half is missing from every journey diagram in this sector.

Nine weeks from the first appointment to the check. Three hours of it in our chair.

  1. The first appointmentabout forty minutes

    Say what you have noticed, and what you are worried about.

    UsLook, and write down what we found.

  2. The written planas long as you want

    Read it, and say no to any part of it.

    UsPrice every option, including doing nothing, and post it.

  3. The worktwo visits, about an hour each

    Turn up, and tell us if anything has changed.

    UsThe agreed work, in the agreed order, stopping when you raise your hand.

  4. Healingsix weeks

    The aftercare. This is the half that decides how it settles.

    UsNothing.

  5. The checkabout twenty minutes

    Come back even if it feels fine.

    UsLook again, and say whether it worked.

The longest step is the one where we do nothing.
