# Section Role

Category-level navigation and discovery. The section answers "which vehicle or service category
should I explore?" — the visitor leaves having chosen a kind of thing, not a thing.

# Boundary With S03

`S03-featured-vehicles/` owns specific vehicles and inventory preview. Individual vehicles, stock,
prices, finance, mileage, model years, transmission and specifications belong there and must not
appear in this section. `SUV` and `Electric` are S02; a named model with a price is S03.
