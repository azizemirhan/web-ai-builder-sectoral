## The content decision

A closing CTA is where this sector keeps its urgency devices — the countdown, the last few left, the offer ending soon, the availability claim. The contract forbids every one of them, which leaves the honest version: **one sentence from the visitor, and what follows from it.**

> **Tell us what you want, or what is *wrong*.** One sentence from you, and the rest follows. Nothing on this page books, orders or signs anything.

- **Buying** — a test drive, an hour, on roads you actually use. *No desk afterwards unless you ask.*
- **Fixing** — a look, a sheet, and a decision that stays yours.

> **This page holds no slot and starts no clock.** If it cannot wait, ring instead.

## What was designed

**No urgency device of any kind**, and the conversion-led 004 carries the least: a measured title, two paths, and one bordered action. The section that would normally shout is the quietest in the sector.

Both paths are written as *what actually happens* rather than as a benefit — *an hour, on roads you actually use*; *a decision that stays yours* — and each ends where the visitor keeps control. The media is the object or the place a path ends at: **the key on the counter**, **the key being handed across it**, and **the road outside the workshop door**, where a test drive starts.

Every study closes on the same two sentences: the page holds no slot, and anything that cannot wait goes to the telephone.
