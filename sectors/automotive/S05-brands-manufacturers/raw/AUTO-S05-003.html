What a badge on a website means

Nothing, unless the maker put it there. A manufacturer mark on a dealer page can mean a franchise, a training certificate, or a designer with a folder of logos. You cannot tell which from looking.

What we can say

The bay has the tools and the data for the makes on the first list, and puts that in writing when you book. For anything else the honest answer is the second list.
