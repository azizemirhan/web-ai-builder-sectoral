Start with when

Cold start, first corner, over bumps, only above a certain speed. When beats what, every time.

Say what it sounds like, not what it is

Grinding, ticking, whining, knocking, a rumble that rises with the wheels. Nobody expects the right word.

Say what changes it

Braking, steering, the clutch, the heater on. If something makes it stop, that is the most useful sentence you have.

Say what you have done

Anything replaced, anything topped up, anywhere else it has been. It saves an hour of looking.

What this is not

It does not say what the noise is, what it will cost, or whether it is safe to keep driving. It says how to be understood by somebody who can find out.
