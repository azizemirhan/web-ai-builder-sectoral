Workshop / One job

A service, and everything that is in it

One job, written out in full, so you can decide whether it is the one your car needs.

What is in it

  • Oil drained and replaced, filter with it.
  • Fluids topped and the levels written down.
  • Lights, wipers and tyres looked at and reported.
  • Brakes measured, and the reading put on the sheet.

How it runs

  1. OneYou leave it, or wait. Either is fine.
  2. TwoIt goes on the ramp and somebody works through the list.
  3. ThreeYou are told what was checked, what was found, and what was left alone.

What is not in it

Anything found while looking. It gets quoted and it waits for your yes; a service that quietly becomes three jobs is not a service.

Who it is for

Anybody whose car is due one, and anybody who is not sure whether it is. If it is not due, the desk will say so.
