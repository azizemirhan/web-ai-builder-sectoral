What is on the shelf, and what the counter says when you do not need it.

THE PARTS COUNTER · 4:3

  • Tyres

    Measured off your car, not a screen.

  • Brakes

    Pads and discs. The old ones come back with you.

  • Batteries

    Tested before it is sold. If yours holds charge, it stays in.

  • Wiper blades

    Fitted at the counter while you wait.

  • Bulbs

    The right one, fitted, not a box you fail with later.

  • Oil and filters

    What the book says for your engine, not what is nearest the till.

What the counter will not do: sell you a part that is not worn out, order something it cannot fit, or read a code off your car and turn it into a shopping list.
