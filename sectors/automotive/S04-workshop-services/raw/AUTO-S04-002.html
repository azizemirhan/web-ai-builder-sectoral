In the workshop

Four jobs, and what each one includes

The work the bay does most. Each one says what is in it, and what is not.

THE RAMP · 21:9

  1. OneA service

    Oil, filter, fluids, lights, tyres and brakes looked at. You get the sheet with what was checked and what was found.

  2. TwoBrakes

    Pads and discs measured rather than guessed. The parts that came off go back in the boot.

  3. ThreeTyres

    Tread measured across the width, pressures set, wheels balanced. If one has life left in it, it stays on.

  4. FourDiagnostics

    The reader tells us where to look. It does not tell us what is wrong; somebody still has to find it.

What is not on this page: a price, a time, or a promise that your car will be ready today. The bay quotes after it has looked, and says so before it starts.
