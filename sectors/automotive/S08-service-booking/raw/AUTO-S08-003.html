Booking the bay

Four ways in, and none of them is a form

Nothing here books anything. It says which route to use, and what comes back.

THE SERVICE RECEPTION · 4:3

The four routes

  1. OneAt the deskWalk in and say what the car is doing.
  2. TwoBy telephone[Approved number, and the hours it is answered]
  3. ThreeIn writing[Approved written route, and how long a reply takes]
  4. FourNone of theseIf it is not safe to drive, do not drive it here.

What we need from you

  • What it is doing, and when it started.
  • Whether it is safe to drive.
  • Whether you need it back the same day. We will say yes or no, not that we will try.

There is no form on this page and no booking system behind it. A slot is somebody writing your name in a diary, and they tell you the day before you hang up.
