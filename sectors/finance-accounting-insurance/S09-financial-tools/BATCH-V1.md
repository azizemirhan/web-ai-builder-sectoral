## Interaction and Responsive Decisions

One labelled section with H2 contains three articles named by unique H3 headings.
Every article has a native Purpose and limits disclosure. All can stay open together.
Each disclosure stays associated with its tool context. No data entry, upload,
submission, dead routes, raw scripts or external dependencies.

Desktop layouts vary in grouping, alignment and emphasis. Mobile restores one reading
order with each disclosure following its description. Scoped CSS includes wrapping,
border-box descendants, visible focus, reduced-motion handling and summaries at least
48px tall. Focus contrasts with the teal and apricot featured surfaces.
