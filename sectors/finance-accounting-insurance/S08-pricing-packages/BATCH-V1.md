Three proposed scope contexts cover a defined assignment, recurring support and a
tailored engagement. Package names, fees, currency, billing basis, taxes, included
work and exclusions remain explicit reserved fields. No actual packages, prices,
discounts, free trials, popularity labels or service availability are invented.

commitment and fixed versus indicative pricing. Its native disclosure reserves

fee and billing-basis fields remain visible without opening it. No simulated plan

package content in its reading order. Scoped CSS includes wrapping, border-box
descendants, visible focus, reduced-motion handling and summary targets at least
48px tall. Price text inherits the correct light colour on violet panels.
