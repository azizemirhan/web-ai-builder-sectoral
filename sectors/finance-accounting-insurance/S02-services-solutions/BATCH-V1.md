## Research Metadata and Scope

Sources: user continuation request, section README, preceding S01 studies and the
FINANCE-THEME-CONTRACT.md established for this sector. Research date: 2026-09-15.
External research: NONE. Document-metaphor justification: NONE.

Finance, Accounting and Insurance are proposed category labels matching the sector.
They do not assert a particular firm's actual offering. Introductory copy invites
visitors to identify questions; it does not give financial advice or promise outcomes.
Each scope is reserved for confirmed services, audience, eligibility, exclusions,
geographic context or professional status. No fees, guarantees, registrations,
coverage, authorisation or service availability are invented.

## Structure and Media

One labelled section with H2 contains three articles, each with a unique H3 and one
native details disclosure. Numbers are decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
The articles differ in placement, hierarchy, scale and grouping across the five layouts.

Media: NONE. Category distinction is communicated through labels, spacing and colour;
photographs would not add evidence to these service summaries. No fake charts, document
metaphors, icons suggesting accreditation or remote assets. S01 was B/B/B/C/B;
S02 is the first A section in every variant.

## Interaction and Responsive Decisions

Each native disclosure opens service scope and suitability information independently.
Multiple scopes may remain open together. No hidden overview copy, fake booking,
submission or dead route to an unauthored service detail. Future routing and actual
service content belong to ingestion after the firm's approved information is supplied.

At tablet and phone widths the three categories retain their reading order in normal
flow. Scoped CSS supplies border-box descendants, controlled text measures, visible
focus, reduced-motion treatment and summaries at least 48px tall. No frameworks,
CDNs, remote runtime dependencies or raw JavaScript.
