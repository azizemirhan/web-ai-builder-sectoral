## What changed from V1

V1's rounded case cards, tinted stage counters, boxed metadata panels and shaded involvement chips go. The account is a set of rows, each opened by its own mark — information for the situation, the document for what was agreed, the compass for the approach, the layers for the staged delivery, the person for who was involved and the shield for the publication note. The stages are an ordered list whose names sit in ink and whose bracketed detail sits in the muted tone, and V1's refusal to claim results is carried beneath them with the prohibition mark in the graphite refusal tone. The engagement record is bare labelled fields on hairlines, not a boxed table; the involvement links are underlined with the register's arrow. Every reserved photograph is an empty flat labelled field with V1's bracketed caption beneath, and in 003 V1's Context, Review, Materials and Handover labels sit above their fields. The darkened surfaces are the 003 closing band, the 004 ask panel and the 005 account band.
