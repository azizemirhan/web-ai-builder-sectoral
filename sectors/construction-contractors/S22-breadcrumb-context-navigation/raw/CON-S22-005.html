Why there is no trail

A row of chevrons can only draw a line, and this page belongs to four places at once. A sentence holds all four, says which one the site is arranged on, and can name what the next page is ordered by — which is more than the convention it replaces manages.

Every link above is a page that exists. The one phrase in the sentence that is not a link is the page you are already reading, and it is marked rather than left to be discovered by pressing it.

And the ordering, stated

By sector, because somebody reading about one kind of building is usually reading about that kind of building. Not by the order the pages were published, which is the ordering almost every arrow in this trade silently uses.

The same sentence would be written differently on a service page or an office page, because those belong to fewer places and the sentence gets shorter rather than the component getting emptier.
