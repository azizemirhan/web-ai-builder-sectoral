A note on the arrangement

The trail above runs by sector, which is one of three orderings this page could have been given and not obviously the best one. It sits equally under the service it belonged to and under the year it finished, and a reader looking for either of those would not learn from the trail that they exist.

So they are listed beside this note rather than folded into the path. A trail can only show one arrangement, and the honest thing a component like this can do is say which one it chose and where the others went.

Nothing in the trail is a level invented to make the path look deeper. Each step is a page somebody can visit, and the last step is not a link to the page you are already reading.
