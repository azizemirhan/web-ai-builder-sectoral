From the site journal

The largest thing on an article page is the article, and it has not been written.

The text, at the measure it will occupy An opening A long middle And a short close

Drawn rather than blanked, and drawn rather than filled. A blank says nothing about how long the piece is or what shape it has; filler says something false, because a reviewer reads it and forms a view of writing that nobody has done. Measures say how much — an opening, a long middle and a short close, which is the shape this kind of piece actually has.

Why it exists

Three different people asked it inside a month. The third time, the answer was written down instead of said again.

Not because it ranks. A topic chosen by a search engine is a topic chosen by somebody who is not the reader.

Who wrote it

A contracts manager who runs the kind of site it is about. If the writer does not run something, it does not go up.

What you can do with it without us

  • Hose the first bay before the second is built.
  • Ask who owns the parapet joint, in writing.
  • Have three junction details drawn before you tender.

All three on your own building, by somebody who is not us. A piece that cannot be used without hiring us is an advertisement with paragraphs in it.

What we get out of it

Some people who read it ring us. Not many and not soon. Others ring somebody else with better questions, which is also fine.

What it leaves out

One kind of junction, one kind of building. Not a specification, and not advice on yours — nobody could give that without standing on it. No guide to everything and no list of tips.
