What you can do with this without us

  • Put a hose on the first bay before the second is built. An afternoon, and it tests the detail rather than the finished building.
  • Ask whoever is building it who owns the parapet joint, and get the answer written down rather than said in a meeting.
  • Have three junction details drawn before you tender — the parapet, a window head, and the base with the finished ground level on it.

All three can be done on your building by somebody who is not us, which is the test of whether a piece like this was worth writing. A piece that cannot be used without hiring us is an advertisement with paragraphs in it.

And a note on what it leaves out

It is about one kind of junction on one kind of building. It is not a specification, and it is not advice about yours — nobody could give that without standing on it.

What we get out of publishing it is that some people who read it ring us. Not many and not soon, but often enough to pay for the afternoon. Others ring somebody else with better questions than they had before, which is also fine — and saying so is more honest than calling this a public service.

There is no guide to everything here and no list of tips. A piece with that title was written for a search engine, and a search engine does not have a building.
