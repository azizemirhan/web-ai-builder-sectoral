What we hold

Every contractor says in-house. Here is how deep it goes.

Four markers, applied to everything below. The ones that admit something are printed the same size as the ones that do not, because a capability list with no gaps in it describes a firm that does not exist.

Directly employed

On our books, on our insurance, under our supervision.

Owned outright

In our yard, maintained by us, and a breakdown is our cost.

Long-term hire

Not ours — but the same units and the same operators each time.

Retained specialist

Not ours at all, and the same firm every job rather than the cheapest that month.

What we do not hold

  • Specialist façade and curtain walling
  • Asbestos removal
  • Lift installation and commissioning
  • Live process environments

None of these are ours, and buying them in to call them ours would only put a margin between you and the firm doing the work. Where a job is mostly one of them, a specialist will be better and cheaper, and we will say so at the first meeting rather than at the third.

The list above is the reason the rest of this page is worth reading. A capabilities section with nothing missing from it has been written by the marketing department, not by the people who resource the sites.
