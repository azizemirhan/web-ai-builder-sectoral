What we hold

Is the job you have got one of ours?

Everything we hold is marked one of four ways, and the honest two are printed the same size as the other two. The useful question is not what we can do — it is whether we can resource yours, and with whose hands.

Site media — our own gangs working

  • Directly employed

    Our books, our insurance, our supervision.

  • Owned outright

    In our yard, and a breakdown is our cost.

  • Long-term hire

    Not ours, but the same units and people each time.

  • Retained specialist

    Not ours at all, and the same firm every job.

Three questions, and you will get a straight answer to them

Enough for us to tell you which marker your job mostly falls under, and whether the part that matters would be done by our people or somebody else’s. If it is mostly somebody else’s, we will say so and name the kind of firm to call.

This books nothing, and it will not tell you we are free. Availability is a conversation with a person who knows what the teams are already committed to, not a light that turns itself on on a web page.

What we do not hold at all: specialist façade and curtain walling, asbestos removal, lift installation, live process environments. Choosing one of those above will get you told so rather than sold to — buying them in to call them ours would only put a margin between you and the firm doing the work.
