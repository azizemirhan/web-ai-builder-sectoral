What we hold

In-house is a word. How deep is the question.

Every contractor writes the same sentence about its own people, and the sentence costs nothing to write. So each thing below says how it is held, and what happens when a job is deeper than we are. The second half is the part a firm cannot print unless it is true.

Plant

Owning machinery is not a virtue in itself; it is a decision about who waits. Owned means our fitter and our cost when it breaks. Hired means somebody else’s yard, which is the right answer for anything that would otherwise stand idle half the year in your preliminaries.

  • Excavation & earthmovingOwned outright

    A breakdown is our cost and our fitter, rather than a hire-yard queue sitting on your programme.
