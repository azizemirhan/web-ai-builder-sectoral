What we hold

In-house is a word. How deep is the question.

Every contractor writes the same sentence about its own people, and the sentence costs nothing to write. So each thing below says how it is held, and what happens when a job is deeper than we are. The second half is the part a firm cannot print unless it is true.

Site media — our own people working, wide

Trades

Who is standing on your site matters more than who signed the contract. Directly employed means our books, our insurance and our supervision — and where that is not true, we have written what is true instead rather than leaving the word to do the work.

Plant

Owning machinery is not a virtue in itself; it is a decision about who waits. Owned means our fitter and our cost when it breaks. Hired means somebody else’s yard, which is the right answer for anything that would otherwise stand idle half the year in your preliminaries.

Technical

The programme and the price are written by people, and the useful question is whether those people ever stand on the site. Ours do, because a programme written from a desk is a spreadsheet, and a job handed to a surveyor who did not price it is where variations come from.
