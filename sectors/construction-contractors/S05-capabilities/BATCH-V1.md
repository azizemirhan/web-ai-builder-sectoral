## The Governing Constraint — The Depth Is Reserved. What Happens Beyond It Is Real.

Every contractor's capabilities page says *in-house*, and the word is free. The two things that
would make it mean something are exactly the two this catalog cannot invent: how many people, and
how much plant. The sector's counter rule already settles the first half — people employed is a
**reserved field** — but it does not say what fills the space.

    Every contractor says "in-house". Almost none says how deep it goes,
    or what happens when the job is deeper than they are.

So every capability in the batch carries two things, and the second is where the section's value
sits:

| Element | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| How it is held | **Real** — the four-value marker below |
| What happens at the limit | **Real** — a stated operating policy |
| The depth itself | **Reserved field**, inline and demoted |
| What is not held at all | **Real, and kept** |
| Utilisation, capacity or availability percentage | **Omitted** |
| Plant make, model, age, hours, fleet value | **Omitted** |
| Certification, scheme, operator card | **Forbidden** — sector direction |
| Client or project names, safety record | **Forbidden** |

### The four markers — the resource-level sibling of the `S02` delivery marker

| Marker | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `Directly employed` | On our books, on our insurance, under our supervision |
| `Owned outright` | In our yard, maintained by us |
| `Long-term hire` | Not ours, but the same units and operators each time |
| `Retained specialist` | Not ours at all, and the same firm every job rather than the cheapest that month |

`S02` marked each **service** self-delivered, managed or joint. `S05` marks each **resource** one of
these four. The two are deliberately parallel: a visitor who read `S02` already knows how to read
this page, and the sector now has one honesty device applied at two different levels.

**Fourteen reserved depth fields in `001` and `003`, eleven in `002`, four in `005`.** No digit
appears in visible copy anywhere in the batch.

## The Depth Is A Field, Not A Counter

`S09 Company Stats` owns the counter. A capabilities section that sets its reserved numbers at
display scale has written a stats band and put a different heading on it, and the two sections then
collide.

So in every study the reserved depth is **inline and subordinate** — captioned at body scale or
smaller, sitting inside the capability it belongs to, never in a row of its own, and always the
smallest thing in its block. `005` carries only four, one per band, precisely because at that
study's type scale a reserved figure would become a counter instantly.

This is the `WELL-S11` field-versus-figure test carried a step further: **the field is reserved and
demoted, because its prominence is what would turn it into a claim.**

### `003`, the inversion — the batch's most useful result

`001` groups by family (Trades, Plant, Technical) and carries the marker as a chip. `003` does the
exact opposite: it groups by **marker** and carries the family as the chip. Same content, transposed
axis, and the consequence is that the page states its own answer before a word of it is read — the
*Directly employed* column runs the full page, *Long-term hire* stops a third of the way down.

    A capability list grouped by trade hides its own answer. Grouped by how each thing is
    held, it cannot.

This is also the defence against the direction's trap. The sector direction warns that
Structured/Visual Modular becomes a specification table here, and *a table of trades is not a
design*. The columns are unequal **by construction** — one is deliberately short — so the layout
states a proportion where a table would only rule a grid.

### `005`, height as the argument

The same idea expressed as art direction rather than as structure: four full-width bands, each as
deep as the marker has things under it, the marker word set at display scale in its own colour. The
proportion is read at a glance, which is the one thing a list can never do and a layout can.

The direction's trap here is the car advert — plant photographed like a product. Every reserved
area is captioned for **work happening**, and the display type carries a marker rather than a
machine, so the subject stays the work and the people rather than the fleet.

### `004`, the conversion device and its refusal

The visitor's real question at a capabilities section is not *what can you do* but *can you resource
mine*. So the study asks three things — what the job leads with, when people are needed, whether the
building is in use — and routes them to a person. Three native `select` controls, no free-text
field, no budget field.

The refusal is printed on the panel in the panel's own voice:

> **This books nothing, and it will not tell you we are free.** Availability is a conversation with a
> person who knows what the teams are already committed to, not a light that goes green on a web
> page.

The direction's trap is the quote calculator that invents a price. The same trap in *this* section
is an availability checker that invents a diary, and it is refused in copy rather than avoided in
silence. `S15 Bid RFQ` still owns the formal submission; this is three questions that decide whether
the conversation is worth either party's time.

## The Steer-Away, And Why It Costs More Here

Carried through from `S02` and `S04`. All five studies name what the firm does not hold at all —
specialist façade and curtain walling, asbestos removal, lift installation, live process
environments — and two studies make it **structural** rather than a footnote: `003` draws it as a
fifth dashed column belonging to the same system, and `005` as a dashed band in the same stack.

This is the section where the admission costs most, which is exactly why it proves most. A
capabilities page with no gaps in it describes a firm that does not exist, and every reader who has
run a project knows it.

## Corrections at Render

| Study | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `001` | Three 21:9 areas at full width — at 1440px each is ~545px of reserved grey, and the capability cards stopped being the subject | Fixed-height bands, `clamp(140px, 16vw, 240px)` |
| `002` | 21:9 lead plate at ~467px on a low-density editorial page | Letterboxed to `32 / 9` |
| `003` | Same 21:9 slab above the columns | Band, `clamp(150px, 17vw, 260px)` |
| `004` | Reserved plate whose dark gradient stop approached the paper colour — it stopped reading as a reserved area on a dark ground | `--slot-a`/`--slot-b` lifted off the ground |
| `005` | The flipped bands handed the **wider** column to the plate, so alternate bands grew a size | Template mirrored on `.flip` as well as the columns; plates cut to `3 / 2` |

Three of the five are one fault: **a reserved area sized by ratio rather than by role.** An
`aspect-ratio` on a full-width element is a promise about height that scales with the viewport, and
at section width a cinematic ratio becomes a slab. Where the area is a header strip, height is the
honest property to set.

The `005` fault is the batch's other reusable one: **alternating sides is not the same as mirroring
a layout.** Swapping `grid-column` without swapping the template silently swaps the proportions too.

## Notes

- Fifth authored batch in the `CON` sector. `S01`–`S05` are complete.
- **The reusable outcome is the pairing of a claim with its limit.** *In-house* is unfalsifiable;
  *in-house, and here is what happens when your job is bigger than that* is checkable, and the
  second clause costs a competitor who does not operate that way more than it costs the firm that
  does. Any sector whose capability page runs on unfalsifiable adjectives — agencies, consultancies,
  manufacturers, logistics — can convert it by the same move.
- The second outcome is `003`'s transposition. `001` and `003` carry **identical content on
  different axes**, and the difference in what the reader learns is large. Worth remembering as a
  cheap way to generate a genuinely distinct study rather than a restyled one: do not vary the
  decoration, transpose the grouping.
- Checker note: the hard tier initially failed `004` on the word *availability*, which appears there
  inside a refusal. Moved to the soft tier and replaced with patterns for an availability
  **assertion** (*available now*, *check availability*). Third time this catalog has caught a
  refusal with a rule written for a claim — the standing rule holds: **check the mechanism hard, the
  vocabulary soft.**
