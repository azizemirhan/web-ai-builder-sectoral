The firm, in figures

An unequal grid, because these are unequal quantities

Four identical boxes would assert that these are four of the same thing. They are not, so no two modules below are the same width.

  • A fixed point

    Sits in the past. Does not grow because we are any good.

  • A running total

    Accumulates. Meaningless without a start date and a definition.

  • A snapshot

    True on the day it was taken. Different next month.

  • A single extreme

    One job. Not an average, not a floor, not typical.

  • A fixed point

    Year founded

    Year the firm was founded—

    Drawn wide and shallow because that is what a date is — a mark on a line rather than a quantity that went up. A long history under a recently adopted name is a combination worth asking us about, so both are printed.

The numbers that are not here

  • Happy clients
  • Satisfaction rating
  • On-time completion
  • Awards won
  • Any safety figure

Happy clients is a client list with a compliment attached. A satisfaction or on-time percentage asserts a measurement system, and the definition of on time belongs to whoever reports it. An award is a body we chose making a judgement we paid to enter.

Safety figures are absent for a harder reason, set out on the safety page. If a figure is countable and not one of the four above, it is a claim rather than a count.
