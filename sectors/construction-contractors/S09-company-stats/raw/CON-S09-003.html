The numbers that are not here

  • Happy clients
  • Satisfaction rating
  • On-time completion
  • Awards won
  • Any safety figure

Happy clients is a client list with a compliment attached. A satisfaction or on-time percentage asserts a measurement system, and the definition of on time belongs to whoever reports it. An award is a body we chose making a judgement we paid to enter.

Safety figures are absent for a harder reason, set out on the safety page. If a figure is countable and not one of the four above, it is a claim rather than a count.
