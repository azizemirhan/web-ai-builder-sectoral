Tell us the size of yours and we will send the three most like it

Not the three largest. The most comparable — in scope, in sector, and in how much of it was live while we worked. If none of ours is close to yours, that is the useful answer and you will get it in the same reply.

You can ask the people who ran them. We will not choose which of the three you call.

Tell us the size of your job

Why we are giving that away

Because the largest-project figure exists to attract larger projects, and printing it beside three other numbers in an identical box invites you to read it as typical.

It is one job. If your own is ordinary, the honest thing we can offer is the ordinary ones — and those are the jobs a client can actually check on.

The numbers that are not here

  • Happy clients
  • Satisfaction rating
  • On-time completion
  • Awards won
  • Any safety figure

Happy clients is a client list with a compliment attached. A satisfaction or on-time percentage asserts a measurement system, and the definition of on time belongs to whoever reports it.

Safety figures are absent for a harder reason, set out on the safety page. If a figure is countable and not one of the four above, it is a claim rather than a count.
