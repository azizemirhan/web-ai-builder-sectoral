The firm, in figures

Four figures. Four shapes. Not one row of boxes.

A date, a running total, a snapshot and a single extreme. Same size on this page; drawn four different ways, because they are four different things.

A fixed point

Year founded

A date is not a score. It does not grow because we did good work; it grows because time passed. The second line is the one worth reading — firms change names, and a long history under a recent name is a question rather than a boast.

The numbers that are not here

  • Happy clients
  • Satisfaction rating
  • On-time completion
  • Awards won
  • Any safety figure

Happy clients is a client list with a compliment attached, and nobody ran that survey. A satisfaction or on-time percentage asserts a measurement system, and the definition of on time belongs to whoever reports it. An award is a body we chose, making a judgement we paid to enter.

Safety figures are absent for a harder reason, set out on the safety page. If a figure is countable and it is not one of the four above, it is a claim rather than a count.
