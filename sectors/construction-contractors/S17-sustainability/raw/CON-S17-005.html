What we change, and by when

Everything we can do about it has a moment it stops being possible.

So the moments are the largest words on this page and the practices sit underneath them. The spine shows an order, not a duration — nothing here claims one gap is longer than another, only that they run in this sequence and that each one shuts.

The stretch before we are appointed

Most of it is decided here, and we are not in the room.

This is the largest block on the page because it is the largest part of the answer. A contractor can propose against any of it and we do, but proposing is not deciding — and a page that leaves this block out is claiming a drawing it was handed.

The frame order

After it, a substitution is a variation, and a variation costs more than the substitution saves.

What shuts with itSubstitutions in the specification

A handful of build-ups where an alternative does the same job. The frame and the substructure are not among them, and that is where the weight sits.

Will not claimThat we designed the building. We proposed against a drawing and somebody accepted or refused.

Closes at

The demolition machine

The shortest window on the page, and the one most often missed — usually by about a fortnight.

What shuts with itReuse, as distinct from recycling

Whether brick, steel, slate or joinery stays on the site as the thing it was. Once a structure is down you have rubble, and rubble is recycling at best.

Will not claimThat crushing on site is reuse. It is recycling done nearer, and we call it that.

Closes at

The services application

Miss it and the site runs on a generator for the duration, and nothing said afterwards changes that.

What shuts with itWhat the site runs on

Whether the compound draws from a supply or from a generator, and what the plant burns while it is standing as well as while it is working.

Will not claimThat a plant list is a carbon figure. It is a list of machines; the fuel records against it are the checkable part.

Closes at

The logistics plan

Written with the programme, or not at all — consolidation has to be designed into the sequence.

What shuts with itDeliveries, dust and what leaves in the water

How many vehicles come through somebody else's street and when, and whether what runs off the site is treated before it goes.

Will not claimThat a delivery plan is an environmental policy. It is a traffic decision that happens also to be an environmental one.

Closes at

The compound layout

Afterwards another stream is a lorry movement rather than a bin, and a full skip is moved whether it was sorted or not.

What shuts with itSegregation on site

Timber, metal, plasterboard and inert each in their own container, so that what leaves the gate leaves as a material rather than as mixed rubbish.

Will not claimThat it makes the building anything. It keeps plasterboard out of a mixed load, which is the expensive problem.

Closes at

The first week

A record kept from the first week is evidence. A record assembled at the end is a reconstruction, and you can see the difference in it.

What shuts with itWhat is measured, and by whom

On its own it changes nothing, which is worth saying plainly — measurement is the part of this subject most often presented as though it were an achievement.

Will not claimThat our own figures are evidence. The transfer notes are, because somebody outside this firm signs them.

Handover — and after this, nothing on this page is available at all

The one record on a site that an outside party signs

Every other number on this subject is produced by the party it flatters. Waste transfer notes are not — a carrier signs them, a receiving site signs them, and both are registered with somebody who can be asked.

So the figures sit on the notes rather than on this page. Ask by job and the set comes over whole, including the loads that went to landfill, which is the part usually left out of a summary.

We are not going to call ourselves green, eco or carbon neutral, because an adjective is not a mechanism and none of those words survives being asked what it does. We do not buy certificates in order to describe a job as neutral — a figure arrived at by purchase is a purchase rather than a measurement, and it says nothing whatever about how the work was done. There is no percentage and no tonnage on this page; both live on the notes somebody else signed. And there is no target dated further out than the work we currently hold, because a pledge for a year past the order book is a press release, whereas every moment on the spine above closes inside a job somebody is already paying for.
