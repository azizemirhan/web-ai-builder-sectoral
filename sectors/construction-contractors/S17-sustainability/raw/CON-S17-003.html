Not ours to decide

The widest column here, and not one line of it is a practice of ours. These were drawn by other people, and between them they hold most of the answer.

  • The frame

We can propose against any of them and we do, but proposing is not deciding, and a page that blurs the two is claiming credit for a drawing it was handed. The window on all six closes before a contractor is in the room.

We propose, somebody else accepts

Ours to put forward

Two of them, and both have to survive a decision that is not ours. Which way each went is in the correspondence.

  • Reuse before recycling

    Whether brick, steel, slate or joinery stays on the site as the thing it was, rather than leaving on a lorry and coming back as aggregate.

    Window

    Before the machine arrives. Once a structure is down you have rubble, and rubble is recycling at best. The shortest window on this page.

    Will not claim

    That crushing on site is reuse. It is recycling done nearer, and we call it that.

  • Substitutions in the specification

    A handful of build-ups where an alternative does the same job. The frame and the substructure are not among them, and that is where the weight sits.

    Window

    Between appointment and the frame order. Afterwards it is a variation, and a variation costs more than the substitution saves.

    Will not claim

    That we designed the building.

Nobody to blame but us

Ours alone

The narrowest column, and the only one we can be held to entirely. Four practices, four deadlines.

  • Segregation on site

    Timber, metal, plasterboard and inert each in their own container, so what leaves the gate leaves as a material.

    Window

    Compound layout. Afterwards another stream is a lorry movement rather than a bin.

    Will not claim

    That it makes the building anything. It keeps plasterboard out of a mixed load.

  • What the site runs on

    A supply or a generator, and what the plant burns standing as well as working.

    Window

    The temporary services application, before mobilisation. Miss it and it is a generator for the duration.

    Will not claim

    That a plant list is a carbon figure. It is a list of machines.

  • Deliveries, dust and water

    Movements through somebody else’s street, when they happen, and what is treated before it runs off.

    Window

    The logistics plan, written with the programme. Consolidation has to be in the sequence.

    Will not claim

    That a delivery plan is an environmental policy. It is a traffic decision.

  • What is measured

    Kept from the first week rather than assembled at the end, because the difference is visible in the record.

    Window

    Continuous, which is the difficulty with it.

    Will not claim

    That our own figures are evidence. The transfer notes are.

The one record on a site that somebody outside the firm signs

Every other number on this subject is produced by the party it flatters. Waste transfer notes are not — a carrier signs them, a receiving site signs them, and both are registered with somebody who can be asked.

The figures are on the notes rather than on this page. Ask by job and the set comes over whole, including the loads that went to landfill.

Not on this page

  • Not green
  • Not eco
  • Not carbon neutral
  • No offsetting

An adjective is not a mechanism, so none is applied to this firm above. We do not buy certificates in order to call a job neutral: a figure arrived at by purchase is a purchase. And a target dated past the work we hold is a press release, whereas every window in the columns closes inside a job somebody is already paying for.
