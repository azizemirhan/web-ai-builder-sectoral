## The Rule Was Set In Advance

Anti-pattern twelve, from the sector direction:

> **The sustainability claim without a mechanism** — *green*, *eco*, *carbon neutral* as adjectives.
> `S17` states practices, never labels.

With the counter rule alongside it, both halves of the usual version of this page were unavailable:
the adjective and the percentage. **What was left turned out to be the only part that was ever
load-bearing.**

## The Governing Constraint — Most Of It Was Decided Before We Arrived

    Most of a building's carbon is decided before a contractor is appointed. The frame,
    the substructure, the orientation and the glazing were drawn by somebody else. What
    we control is what happens between the drawing and the handover, and that is a
    shorter list than these pages usually claim.

This is the most useful true thing a contractor can put on a sustainability page and the opposite of
what one usually says. It also generates the content: **name what can still be changed, and the
point after which it cannot.**

## The Device — Practice, Window, And The Claim We Will Not Make

| What it actually changes | The last point it can be decided | **What we will not claim from it** |

The first is the mechanism anti-pattern twelve demands. The second is `S06`'s gate, arriving where it
bites hardest — **an environmental practice with no deadline attached is an intention.** The third is
the antibody: every item inoculates itself against its own overclaim, in its own words.

| Practice | Window | Will not claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Segregation on site | Compound layout | That it makes the building anything. It keeps plasterboard out of a mixed load |
| **Reuse before recycling** | **Before the machine arrives** | **That crushing on site is reuse.** It is recycling done nearer |
| Substitutions in the specification | Between appointment and the frame order | That we designed the building |
| What the site runs on | The temporary services application | That a plant list is a carbon figure |
| Deliveries, dust and water | The logistics plan | That a delivery plan is an environmental policy |
| What is measured, and by whom | Continuous | **That our own figures are evidence** |

## The Verification Move

    Waste transfer notes are the only environmental record on a construction site that
    somebody outside the firm signs. Everything else is us marking our own work.

The same shape as `S08`'s registration you can check and `S16`'s document somebody else dated. The
references are reserved fields; the argument about who signs them is real. All five studies also
offer the loads that went to landfill — **the part of the set usually left out of a summary.**

## No Green Anywhere In The Batch

A deliberate art-direction rule, and the batch made it **checkable**: the compliance script converts
every hex in every stylesheet to HSL and fails any colour with a hue between seventy-five and a
hundred and sixty-five at meaningful saturation. Nothing in the batch trips it.

The reasoning is the section's own: a page about skips, diesel and delivery slots coloured like
foliage is **the banned adjective smuggled back in through the palette.** The device tones are a blue
for what changes, an amber for the window and a red for the refusal.

This is the second hue-based rule in the sector, after `S07`'s hi-vis check. Both exist because the
thing being policed is not a word.

### `002`, the marginalia

Not the letter of `S15-002` and not the colophon of `S16-002`. **Marginalia** is the form used where
a text needs a second voice qualifying it line by line, and anti-pattern twelve is precisely a claim
exceeding its mechanism. Setting each limit in the margin rather than in the body means the reader
meets the practice and its ceiling in the same glance.

### `003`, the widest column with nothing in it

Sorted by **who holds the decision**: not ours, ours to propose, ours alone. The first column is the
widest on the page and contains no practice at all — only six things already drawn. The second holds
two proposals somebody else accepts or refuses. The third is the narrowest and the only one this firm
can be held to entirely.

The sector direction names this variant's trap as *the specification table.* A table would assert
that three lists are comparable. **The widths here run the opposite way to the counts**, which says
before any copy does that the influence and the accountability are inversely distributed.

### `004`, the ask that can be late

> **The ask is a date, not a service.**

Every other page of this kind asks a reader to get in touch to discuss our environmental commitments
— an offer that cannot be late, which is another way of saying it does not do anything. This one
routes by where the project is standing, and each stage prints **what has already shut** beside what
is still open.

Stage three says plainly: **we cannot retrofit decisions that are already built.** It is the shortest
list on the page and is presented as the shortest. That is **the seventh section in this sector to
convert by handing over the less flattering answer**, after `S09`, `S12`, `S13`, `S14`, `S15` and
`S16`.

### `005`, the spine

**The moment is the display element, not the practice.** *The frame order. The demolition machine.
The services application.* Each set at display scale, with the practice that dies with it underneath.

The page opens on a hatched block — the stretch before appointment — and that block is the largest
single element on the page, because it holds the largest part of the answer. The art direction is
that proportion.

**No axis and no measured spacing.** The spine shows an order, not a duration; nothing claims one gap
is longer than another. The `S09` field-versus-figure test, applied to a timeline. Its reserved
fields sit at ordinary scale in the foot, not enlarged — the lesson from `S08-005`, `S09-005`,
`S15-005` and `S16-005`.

## What Is Not On Any Of The Five Pages

- **No adjective applied to this firm.** Not *green*, not *eco*, not *sustainable*, not *carbon
  neutral*, not *net zero* — anywhere outside the blocks that name them in order to refuse them.
- **No offsetting, no tree planting, no purchased neutrality.** A figure arrived at by purchase is a
  purchase rather than a measurement.
- **No percentage and no tonnage.** Both live on the transfer notes, signed by somebody else.
- **No target dated past the order book.** Every window on these pages closes inside a job somebody
  is already paying for.
- **No photograph of a tree, a turbine or a panel we did not install.** The subject is waste, fuel
  and lorries.

## Corrections Before Render

| Study | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `002` | The opening paragraph described the page it is not, and used one of the banned adjectives to do it — outside the declared refusal region | Reworded to name the anti-pattern abstractly: *a version of this page that applies an adjective to this firm.* Stronger, and it does not require an exemption |
| `005` | **The entire art direction was silently cancelled by a selector.** `.stop > div p` (two classes and an element) outranked `.moment` (two classes), so every display-scale moment rendered at body size | The right-hand column given its own class and the generic rule scoped to it |
| `003` | The already-drawn list was set at small size, so the widest column read as the emptiest rather than the weightiest | List raised to body scale with more padding |

The `005` correction is the important one, and it is **the second time in this sector that a
descendant selector on an element type has captured a later element of that type in the same
container** — after `S13-005`, where a `ul` rule silently made an identity list look like column
headings.

Both failures had the same signature: **the page still looked deliberate.** Nothing was broken,
nothing overflowed, no check caught it. The study simply argued the opposite of what its own note
claimed, and only a render at full size showed it. That is now the standing reason this catalog
renders every study rather than trusting the checker.

## Checker Note

One rule failed a study for a defect in the rule: the outside-signature check looked for *somebody
outside this firm signs*, which `004` states as *signed by the carrier and by the receiving site*
plus *us marking our own work* — the same claim in a router's register. Broadened to the argument.

The batch's own new rule went the other way. **The green-hue check is the first palette rule in this
catalog written to enforce a content anti-pattern rather than a rendering property.** It is the right
shape for anti-pattern twelve, because the adjective this section refuses can be applied without
using the word — and a vocabulary scan would never have seen it.

## Notes

- **The reusable outcome is that an environmental practice is only real if it has a deadline.** Every
  page of this kind lists intentions; naming the moment after which each becomes unavailable is what
  separates a practice from an aspiration, and it is checkable by a client rather than by the firm.
- The second outcome is the giveaway that made the section work: **saying out loud that most of the

  everything else, because a reader who believes that sentence will believe the shorter list that
