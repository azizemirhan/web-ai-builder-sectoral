# Section Role

Where this firm works, and — the part that matters — what you lose the further out you are.

# The Rule Was Set In Advance

Anti-pattern eleven: **the map of pins across a country, asserting coverage nobody verified.** The
sector direction sends this section to *named real regions or a reserved area*.

A pin map is forbidden for the same reason a logo wall is: **it asserts something at a glance that
nothing on the page supports.** Twelve pins say *we work here*; they do not say whether anybody from
the firm has been to eleven of those places this year.

# The Governing Constraint — A Service Area Is Not A Circle

    A service area is the distance a site manager can cover and still be on your site
    every morning. It is not a radius, and it is not a county.

That is the honest definition, and it produces the section's content: an area is described by **what
you lose the further out you are**, not by where a line is drawn.

| Band | What you get, and what you lose |
| --- | --- |
| **Around our own yard** | The manager is on site every morning and goes home. Plant off our own yard, our own fitter the same day. Travel is not priced because it is where we work |
| **The next ring out** | Still every morning. Plant ours where it travels, hired locally where it does not. **The fitter is a drive rather than a visit** |
| **Further than that** | **Most mornings, not every morning.** This is the whole of what you lose and it is the only thing worth knowing. Plant hired locally; a hire desk rather than our fitter |
| **Beyond that** | **We do not bid**, and we will name a firm nearer you if we know one |

# Why The Bands Cannot Be Miles Or Minutes

A distance is a figure and a drive time is a duration, and this sector omits both — the `S06` rule
and the counter rule between them.

That constraint produced the better answer rather than a compromise. **Miles do not describe a
service area anyway**: fifty of them on a motorway and fifty across a city are different jobs, and
neither tells a client whether somebody will be standing on their site at seven in the morning.
Attendance does.

    The figure this section cannot print is the one that would have told you least.

# The Admission

> **We will travel further than this for the right job, and we are worse at it.**

A firm claiming to cover a whole country is either very large or not telling the truth, and the ones
in the middle simply cost a client more in travel and less in attendance. Every study says so, and
`003` and `005` make the outermost band the loudest thing on the page.

# What Is Real, And What May Not Be Invented

| Element | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| The bands, and what is lost at each | **Real** — the section's content |
| Travel-pricing policy | **Real** — shown as a line, or in the overhead, and which |
| The refusal to bid beyond the last band | **Real, and kept** |
| Region, county, city and town names | **Reserved fields** — the catalog cannot know them |
| The yard location | **Reserved field** |
| A map | **Reserved area only** — never drawn, never pinned |
| Miles, radius, drive time | **Omitted** — a figure and a duration |
| *Nationwide*, *national coverage*, *anywhere in the country* | **Forbidden** |
| Number of offices, depots or regions covered | **Reserved field** if used at all |

**Not present in any study:** a pin, a drawn map, a radius, a mileage, a drive time, a *nationwide*
claim, or a digit in visible copy.

# The Map Is Reserved, And Argued With

Where a study carries a map it is **a reserved area and nothing else** — and `002` uses that
reservation as its argument: here is where the map would go, and here is why it would tell you
less than the paragraph beside it.

| Section | What it owns |
| --- | --- |
| `S04` Sectors Served | **Who we build for**, and the constraint each sector imposes |
| `S05` Capabilities | **What we hold**, and how deep |

The test against `S11`: this section may state **attendance by distance**; the moment it describes
what the site manager decides it has written `S11`.
