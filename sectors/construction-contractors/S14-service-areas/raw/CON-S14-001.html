Where we work

A service area is not a circle. It is how far attendance survives.

The useful question is not whether we will come, it is what you lose the further out you are. So the bands below are described by that, and each one says what changes about the mornings, the plant and the fitter.

Everything on this page is measured from one yard, because that is where the plant, the fitters and most of the people go home to. A firm with depots everywhere would draw this differently and should say so.

Reserved area — a map, if the finished page carries one. No pins, and no shaded country

Why there is no map of pins here

  • No pins
  • No radius
  • No mileage
  • No drive times
  • No nationwide claim

A row of pins says we work here at a glance and supports none of it. It does not say whether anybody from this firm has been to most of those places this year, which is the only thing the pins are being used to imply.

There are no distances either. A mile on a motorway and a mile across a city are different jobs, and neither tells you whether somebody will be standing on your site at seven in the morning. Attendance does, so the bands are drawn on that instead.
