The paperwork

A logo tells you somebody paid. A number tells you where to look.

So the numbers are printed at the size the badges usually are, and the badges are not here at all. Every one of them sits on a public register kept by whoever issued it.

Check every one of them

A certificate is a permission with an expiry date and somebody who can take it away. That makes the register, not this page, the place where any of it is either true or not.

If one has lapsed by the time you read this, the register will say so before we do — which is the argument for printing numbers instead of badges.

What none of it tells you

Whether the site is run well. All of the above tells you that somebody checked the paperwork on a date.

Who can stop the work, what happens to them if they were wrong, and whether that changes when a job is behind — nobody certifies those answers. They are on the safety page, and they are the ones worth asking standing on a site.
