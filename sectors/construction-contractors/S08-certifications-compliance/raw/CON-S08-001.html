Check every one of them

Each number above sits on a public register kept by the body that issued it, and we would rather you looked. A certificate is a permission with an expiry date and somebody who can take it away — which makes the register, not this page, the place where it is either true or not. If any of them has lapsed by the time you read this, the register will say so before we do.

What none of this tells you

None of it tells you whether the site is run well. It tells you that somebody checked the paperwork on a date, which is worth something and is not worth what a wall of badges implies.

Who can stop the work, what happens to them if they were wrong, and whether any of that changes when a job is behind — those are the questions, and nobody certifies the answers. They are on the safety page, and they are the ones worth asking on site.
