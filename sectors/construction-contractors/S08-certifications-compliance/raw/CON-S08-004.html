The paperwork

Send us the list before you fill it in.

If your tender has a prequalification list, you are about to spend an evening finding out whether we qualify. We can answer that first, and there are only three answers.

  • We hold it

    Here is the number, go and check it

    You get the registration and the body that issued it, and you verify it on their public register rather than taking our word.

    If it has lapsed, the register tells you before we do. That is the point of printing numbers instead of badges.

  • We could go and get it

    And we will say whether it is worth waiting for

    Some assessments we have simply never needed. If yours requires one, we will tell you what getting it involves and let you decide.

    We will not tell you it is imminent to keep ourselves on your list while you wait.

  • We cannot

    And this is the card that makes the other two worth reading

    Some requirements we will not meet, and the honest thing is to say so at the first email rather than after you have shortlisted us.

    A page that can only say yes is a page whose yes means nothing.

What we hold, with the numbers where the badges usually go

  • Quality management

    Quality management registration number—

    Audits the system, not the building.

  • Health and safety assessment

    Health and safety assessment registration number—

    Not how a site runs when we are behind.

  • Environmental management

    Environmental management registration number—

    Says nothing about a carbon footprint.

  • Trade body registration

    Trade body membership number—

    Confirms we exist and we pay. Not competence.

  • Public sector supplier registration

    Public sector supplier registration number—

    Eligible is not chosen.

  • Insurance

    Insurance policy number—

    Cover has limits. Yours should read them.

Send the list and we will mark it up

Every line answered with one of the three above, the registration where we have it, and a straight sentence where we do not. You will know before you start writing whether the submission is worth either of our evenings.

Send us your prequalification list

This is not a bid and it does not put us on your list. It is the cheapest possible way for both of us to find out that we are not eligible — and where we are, you will have the numbers to verify before you have committed anything at all.

None of this tells you whether the site is run well. It tells you that somebody checked the paperwork on a date. Who can stop the work, and what happens to them if they were wrong, is on the safety page — nobody certifies those answers, which is why they are the ones worth asking.
