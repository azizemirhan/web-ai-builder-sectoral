# Section Role

The people. Which of them you will actually deal with, what each can decide without asking, and what
changes about that when the job starts.

# The Rules Were Set In Advance

Anti-pattern thirteen: **the team page as a wall of stock portraits.** And the sector direction sends
this section to the `WELL-S04` rule — **role leads, identity reserved.**

| Element | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Role titles | **Real** — generic vocabulary |
| Name | **Reserved field** |
| Portrait | **Reserved area** |
| Qualifications, memberships, years in the trade | **Omitted** — the `WELL-S26` rule |
| Biography, career history, previous employers | **Omitted** |
| Personal detail, interests, quotes | **Omitted** |

A portrait and a name are reserved **inside a single `<figure>`**, carried from `WELL-S26`, so no
reflow at any width can separate a face from the name under it.

# The Governing Constraint — A Team Page Shows You The People Who Win The Work

    You want the people who run it.

The person who comes to a tender interview is very often not the person who runs the job, and on most
sites in this industry that is discovered afterwards. It is the most common complaint about
contractors and almost no team page addresses it, because addressing it means admitting it.

So every person in this batch carries **when you will actually deal with them**:

| Marker | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `Every day, on your site` | The site manager and the foreman |
| `Every week` | The contracts manager |
| `At fixed points` | The planner, the commercial manager |
| `At tender and at handover` | The director — and nowhere in between |

**Every study prints the last row.** A leadership page whose directors appear to be on every site is
describing a firm that does not exist, and a client who believes it will be disappointed in month
two.

# The Second Half — What Each One Can Decide Without Asking

The `S07` authority device, applied to people instead of to a stop:

| Role | Decides without asking |
| --- | --- |
| Foreman | Runs the gang. Stops the work. Calls the site manager |
| Site manager | Stops the work. Orders what the job needs that day. Moves the sequence within the week |
| Contracts manager | Agrees a variation. Moves resource between sites. Is the escalation |
| Commercial manager | Agrees the valuation. Prices a change |
| Planner | Rewrites the programme. Nobody else does |
| Managing director | Changes the contract — **and nothing on your site day to day** |

That last line is the one that costs something to print:

> **The managing director decides nothing about your site day to day, and a firm where they do is a
> firm with a problem.**

A title tells a client nothing. An authority is checkable by asking the person next to it.

# No Wall Of Portraits

Anti-pattern thirteen is a **shape** as much as a content rule, so:

    No study lays the team out as a uniform grid of equal portraits.

The organising principle in every study is contact or authority, never seniority, and the groupings
that produces are naturally unequal. `005` takes it furthest: **the size of each person's block is
proportional to how often you will see them**, which makes the managing director the smallest block
on the page and every other leadership page in the sector the wrong way round.

# What Is Real, And What May Not Be Invented

**Not present in any study:** a name, a portrait, a qualification, a membership, a years-in-the-trade
figure, a biography, a previous employer, a personal quote, an award, or a headcount presented as a
credential. No digit in visible copy.

Reserved: names and portraits, and the count of site managers held — the `S05`/`S09` treatment, since
how many site managers a firm employs is a countable fact it knows and the empty field asserts
nothing.

# Boundary With S05, S06, S07 And S26

| Section | What it owns |
| --- | --- |
| `S05` Capabilities | **What we hold**, including how many site managers |
| `S06` Project Delivery Process | **What happens**, stage by stage |
| `S07` Safety Program | **Who can stop the work**, and what it costs when they do |
| `S11` Leadership Team | **Who you deal with**, and what each can decide |
| `S26` Leader / Project Expert Profile | **One of them in full**, on their own page |

    S06 says what happens at pre-start. S11 says who is in the room, and which of them
    was in the room at tender.

The test against `S07`: the stop authority may be **listed** as something the site manager holds; the
moment a study explains what happens after a near miss it has written `S07`.
