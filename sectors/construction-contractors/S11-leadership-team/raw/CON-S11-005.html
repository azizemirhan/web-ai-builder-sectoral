The people

Sized by how often you will see them.

Every leadership page in this industry is sized the other way round, so the biggest face on it is the person you will meet least. This one is scaled to contact, and the ordering is the argument.

  • Every day, on your site

    Site manager

    Decides without askingStops the work. Orders what the job needs that day. Moves the sequence within the week. They will make more decisions about your building in a week than anybody else on this page will make all year, and they run one site rather than three.

  • Every day, on your site

    Foreman

    Decides without askingRuns the gang. Stops the work. Calls the site manager rather than working round a problem.

  • Every week

    Contracts manager

    Decides without askingAgrees a variation. Moves resource between sites. The escalation, and the number you are given at pre-start.

  • At each valuation

    Commercial manager

    Decides without askingAgrees the valuation. Prices a change. The same person who priced the job originally.

  • When the programme changes

    Planner

    Decides without askingRewrites the programme. Nobody else does, which is why it stays a programme.

  • Tender, handover

    Managing director

    Decides without askingChanges the contract. Turns work down. Nothing on your site.

  • That last block is the correct size, and the space around it is the point. The managing director decides nothing about your site day to day — and a firm where they do is a firm where either the site manager has no authority or the director cannot leave it alone.

    Neither is good news for the job you are paying for. So they are the smallest thing on this page, drawn against the room they are not in.

What is not on this page

No qualifications, memberships, years in the trade or career histories. They are true of everybody here and they distinguish nobody.

What is here is who you will see, how often, and what each can decide without asking — and all of it can be checked by asking the person it is written under.
