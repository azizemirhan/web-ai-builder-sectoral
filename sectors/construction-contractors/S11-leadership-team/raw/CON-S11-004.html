The people

Ask to meet the one who will run it.

Before you sign anything, and of every firm on your list including this one. It costs you nothing and it tests every other claim on a page like this — because the person at a tender meeting is very often not the person on the site.

Every day, on your site

Site manager

This is the person to ask for, by role, at the tender meeting.

Not the director sitting opposite you, and not whoever is presenting. The site manager will make more decisions about your building in a week than anybody else in the room will make all year — they stop the work, they order what the day needs, and they move the sequence.

Half an hour with them tells you more than an hour of anybody else, and you will find out whether they have read your job or been handed a folder that morning.

Ask to meet the person who will run it

A straight answer sounds like

“It will be this person, they are finishing on another job, and you can meet them next week on site.”

Or: “we do not know yet, and here is when we will.” Both are answers. Either lets you plan, and either can be held to.

And a poor one sounds like

“You’ll be well looked after — we have a great team.”

That is a sentence every contractor alive can say, and it means the allocation has not been made. Which is fine, and is worth knowing before you sign rather than after.

What we can and cannot promise

Site managers on our own books

Number of site managers on our own books—

If your job starts far enough ahead that we do not yet know who will run it, we will tell you that rather than name somebody to win the tender and change them at pre-start.

Naming a person and swapping them later is the exact thing this page argues against, and doing it to win work would be the same failure in a better suit. What we will commit to is the role, the authority, and that you meet them before the first morning.

What is not on this page

No qualifications, no memberships, no years in the trade, no career histories, and no gallery. Every competitor’s team page carries the same list, and none of it tells you who will be standing on your site in month three.

One face here, because there is one person this page is about. The rest of the people, and what each of them can decide without asking, are set out in full elsewhere — but this is the only one you need to ask for by name.
