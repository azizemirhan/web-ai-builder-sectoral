While it runs

On site — and this is where you will spend the job

These are the only two people on this page with a face on it, because they are the only two you will stand next to every day. A gallery of six equal portraits would tell you less.

Site manager

Every day. Stops the work, orders what the day needs, moves the sequence within the week. One site, not three.

Foreman

Every day. Runs the gang, stops the work, calls the site manager rather than working round a problem.

What is not on this page

No qualifications, no memberships, no years in the trade, no career histories. Every competitor’s team page carries the same list and none of it tells you who will be on your site.

What does tell you that is above: who is in the room, when, and who has left. All of it is checkable at the pre-start meeting, by asking who in the room will be there in month three.
