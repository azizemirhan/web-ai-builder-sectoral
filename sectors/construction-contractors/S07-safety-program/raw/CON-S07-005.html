How the site is run

Anybody on this site can stop the work.

And if they were wrong, nothing happens to them.

That second sentence is the one that makes the first sentence true. A stop that costs the person something is a stop that will not happen the second time — and the second time is the one that matters. Everything below is what it takes to mean it.

    Who restarts it

    The supervisor and the person who raised it, together — never the person who stopped it alone, and never whoever is under the most pressure that morning.

    What it costs usSometimes the rest of the day, recorded against the programme rather than the person.

Site media — a brief before work starts

No figure appears on this page

  • No incident rate
  • No days-without board
  • No zero harm, and no perfect record
  • No wall of scheme logos

An incident rate rewards under-reporting. The cheapest way to improve one is to write less down. A days-without board punishes whoever breaks the streak, and the person who breaks it is the person who reported something.

Both make a page look better and a site more dangerous, so a firm that prints them has told you what it optimises. We would rather print the one sentence at the top, because you can check it by asking anybody standing on the site.

The certificates and registrations are real, and they are deliberately not here. They belong where a number can be checked against a register rather than admired on a wall.
