# Section Role

How this firm runs a site safely — as a set of standing decisions, not as a record.

# The Governing Constraint — A Record Is An Outcome. A Programme Is A Decision.

The sector direction forbids, outright and in every section including this one: an incident rate, an
accident-frequency or lost-time figure, a *days without an incident* board, *zero accidents*, *zero
harm*, *100% safe*, an invented certification or registration number, a logo wall of standards
bodies, and any named regulation asserted as complied with.

That is not a limitation to work around. **It is the section's argument**, and every study says so on
the page:

    A safety record is an outcome, and outcomes can be under-reported.
    A safety programme is a set of standing decisions, and decisions can be checked on site.

The reason is the one that matters to a client rather than to a catalog:

- **An incident rate rewards under-reporting.** The cheapest way to improve it is to record less.
- **A days-without board punishes the person who breaks the streak** — which is the person who
  reported something.

Both make a page look better and a site more dangerous. A firm that prints them is telling you what
it optimises. So the studies do not print them, and they explain why rather than leaving a silence
that reads as modesty.

# The Device — Who Holds It, And What It Costs Us

Everything on this page is a standing decision, and each one carries two things:

| On every decision | Why it is the honest half |
| --- | --- |
| **Who holds the authority** | A policy nobody can use is decoration. The question is whose hands it is in |
| **What it costs us when they use it** | Anyone can grant an authority that is free to exercise. The cost is the proof |

That second line is this section's version of the `S05` limit and the `S06` gate: the sentence a
firm cannot print unless it is true.

## The Sentence This Section Is Built Around

> **Anybody on this site can stop the work — including somebody who started this morning — and they
> do not need to find a manager first.**
>
> **If they stop it and they were wrong, nothing happens to them.** A stop that costs the person
> something is a stop that will not happen the second time.

It appears in every study. It is checkable by asking any single person on the site, and a firm where
only the manager can stop work cannot print it.

# What Is Real, And What May Not Be Invented

| Element | Treatment |

| What is briefed before work starts, and by whom | **Real** |

# This Section Reserves Nothing

`S06` reserved nothing because an empty duration asserts a system. This section reserves nothing for
a different reason, and it is worth stating separately:

    Every countable thing in a safety section is an incident figure or a proxy for one.

A labelled empty box on a safety page draws the shape of the board the sector direction forbids,
and a reviewer reading it learns that the finished page will carry a number where a decision should
be. So no study in this batch contains a reserved field of any kind.

Two consecutive sections that reserve nothing, for two different reasons, is the `WELL-S11` test
being applied rather than quoted.

# The Programme-Pressure Admission

The item that costs most to print, and the one that makes the rest credible:

> Every serious incident in this industry happens on a job that is behind. **The stop authority does
> not change when we are behind** — which is the only time it is worth anything.

Carried in every study, because a safety section that describes an unpressured site is describing
somebody else's job.

# No Hi-Vis As Decoration

Anti-pattern six. Orange is this sector's default way of signalling *safety* without saying
anything, and on a safety page it is at its most tempting and least honest.

**No study in this batch uses a saturated orange or amber anywhere in its palette.** The batch check
tests the stylesheets for it by hue rather than by trusting the author's eye. Where a colour carries
meaning it carries exactly one: the authority marker, distinguishing a decision **anybody** holds
from one a **named role** holds.

# Boundary With S05, S06, S08 And S11

| Section | What it owns |
| --- | --- |
| `S05` Capabilities | **What we hold**, and what happens at the edge of it |
| `S06` Project Delivery Process | **What happens to you**, and where each door closes |
| `S07` Safety Program | **How the site is run** — authority, briefing, near misses, the chain |
| `S08` Certifications & Compliance | **The paperwork**, every number and registration a reserved field |
| `S11` Leadership Team | **Who the people are** — role leads, identity reserved |

    S06 says where your decisions stop being cheap. S07 says whose hand is on the stop.

The test against `S08`: this section may describe **practice**; it may not display a **credential**.
The moment a study shows a scheme, a certificate or a registration it has written `S08`, and has
written it with invented content, which `S08` itself is not allowed to do.
