The register of change

Four things on this job were not what the drawings said.

Every project page shows what was built. The record worth having is the list of what changed between the drawing and the building, and who decided each one — it exists on every job, it is written down as it happens, and it is published on almost none of them.

  • Found on opening up

    The building was not what the drawings said

    It rarely is. A survey shows what somebody could see, and the parts nobody could see are the parts that change a programme. This one was found in the first fortnight, which is the best week it could have been found in.

  • The client changed their mind

    Which is entirely normal and needs no defending

    A building is designed over a couple of years and lived in for forty. Somebody deciding late that a room should be used differently is not a failure of anything, and a contractor treating it as one is a contractor who has forgotten what the building is for.
