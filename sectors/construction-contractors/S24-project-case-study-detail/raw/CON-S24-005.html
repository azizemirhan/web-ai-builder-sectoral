Why this comparison and not the other one

The comparison a project page usually reaches for is two photographs, and the two photographs are never of the same conditions — different light, different weather, different lens, and one of them chosen.

Two columns of text are of the same conditions. That is the whole reason this comparison can be published and that one cannot, and it is why there is no pair of images anywhere on this page.

And the phrase that is missing

Delivered on time and on budget. It is not a claim about a project; it is a claim about a page. A job with no differences is a job nobody has looked at.
