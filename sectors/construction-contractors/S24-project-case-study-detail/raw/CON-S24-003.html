Why the columns are not the same width

Because they are not four comparable quantities. One of them is an admission, and a layout that gave all four equal cells would be inviting a reader to count them instead of read them.

Nothing here is a proportion. The widths follow how much there is to say. The phrase this page does without is delivered on time and on budget. It is not a claim about a project; it is a claim about a page — and a job with no variations is a job nobody has looked at. The only question is whether the firm will show you the list.
