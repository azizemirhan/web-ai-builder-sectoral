Who we build for

The same three questions, asked of every sector

Read one if you are in one. Read across if you are deciding between two.

  • Healthcare

    What makes it hard

    The building stays open. Infection control, live entrances, agreed noisy hours.

    What we do about it

    Phasing is priced first, and our site manager sits in the estates meeting.

    Usually needs

    • Refurbishment
    • Fit-out
    • Enabling

  • Education

    What makes it hard

    A date nobody can move, and safeguarding around every gate until it arrives.

    What we do about it

    The programme runs backwards from the first day of teaching; gates are sequenced with the school.

    Usually needs

    • Frame
    • Envelope
    • Fit-out

  • Industrial

    What makes it hard

    Ground and loads decide the programme before anybody draws an elevation.

    What we do about it

    We self-deliver the groundworks rather than manage somebody else's risk on our programme.

    Usually needs

    • Groundworks
    • Civils
    • Frame

  • Retail

    What makes it hard

    A trading floor, weekend possessions, and a landlord as well as a client.

    What we do about it

    Work is written in shifts, and both parties get the same programme rather than two versions.

    Usually needs

    • Fit-out
    • Refurbishment

  • Civic

    What makes it hard

    The public alongside the works, and permissions in somebody else's gift.

    What we do about it

    Hoardings, routes and closures are designed at tender, and the sequence assumes one permission arrives late.

    Usually needs

    • Demolition
    • Enabling
    • Civils

  • Residential

    What makes it hard

    Neighbours who did not choose to be next to a site, and will be there after we have gone.

    What we do about it

    Somebody on site is named and reachable, and complaints get an answer rather than a form.

    Usually needs

    • Groundworks
    • Frame
    • Fit-out
