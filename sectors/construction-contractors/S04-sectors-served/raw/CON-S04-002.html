Who we build for

The sector is not a category. It is what makes the job hard.

Healthcare

The building does not close. There are patients on the floor below, a delivery route that is also somebody's front door, and a list of hours in which you are allowed to make noise.

What that changes: phasing is priced before anything else, and our site manager sits in the estates meeting rather than hearing about it afterwards.

Education

Term starts whether you are finished or not. It is the one date in construction that genuinely cannot move, and there is safeguarding around every gate until it arrives.

What that changes: the programme is written backwards from the first day of teaching, and gate sequencing is agreed with the school rather than worked around it.

Industrial

The ground decides. Loads, slabs and drainage set the programme before anybody has drawn an elevation, and the site investigation is a hypothesis rather than a fact.

What that changes: we self-deliver the groundworks, because we would rather be the ones standing in the hole when the ground disagrees with the survey.

Civic

The public is alongside the works the whole time, and half the permissions you need belong to somebody who is not in the room.

What that changes: hoardings, routes and closures are designed at tender rather than discovered on site, and the sequence assumes at least one of them arrives late.
