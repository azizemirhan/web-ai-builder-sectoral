Who we build for

Every contractor lists these. Here is what each one does to a programme.

Naming a sector is not a claim. The line under each name is the part that is either true of us or not, and you can check it on site.

Healthcare

The constraint

The building stays open. Infection control, live entrances, and every noisy hour agreed in advance with somebody who has patients in the room below.

SoPhasing is priced before anything else, and our site manager sits in the estates meeting.

Education

The constraint

A date nobody can move. Term starts whether the building is finished or not, and there is safeguarding around every gate while it is not.

SoThe programme runs backwards from the first day of teaching, and gates are sequenced with the school, not around it.

Industrial

The constraint

Ground and loads decide the programme before anybody has drawn anything, and the survey is a hypothesis rather than a fact.

SoWe self-deliver the groundworks and would rather be the ones standing in the hole when it disagrees.

Retail

The constraint

A trading floor, weekend possessions, and a landlord with an opinion as well as a client with one.

SoThe work is written in shifts, and both parties get the same programme rather than two versions.

Civic

The constraint

The public alongside the works, and half the permissions in somebody else’s gift.

SoHoardings, routes and closures are designed at tender rather than discovered on site.

Residential

The constraint

Neighbours who did not choose to live next to a construction site, and will be there long after we have gone.

SoSomebody on site is named and reachable, and complaints get an answer rather than a form.

Where we are the wrong firm

Anything with a live process running through it — food production, data halls, pharmaceutical clean areas. The constraint there is contamination and continuity of process, and specialists who work in it every week will beat us. We would rather say that here than find it out together on your site.
