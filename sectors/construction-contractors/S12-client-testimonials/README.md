# Section Role

What clients said, and everything that decides whether it is worth reading.

# This Section's Entire Content Is Unwritable

Harder than any section in this sector so far, and the reason is structural rather than
restrictive. A testimonial is **a quotation from a named person**, and this catalog may invent
neither half:

- **The words** are somebody's actual sentence. Writing one is fabricating a person's speech, which
  is the most serious invention available on a website and the one a reader is least able to detect.
- **The name** is a client's, and client names are forbidden sector-wide.
- **A rating** is anti-pattern eight — the satisfaction badge, the five stars, the *rated excellent*
  seal — and it asserts a survey nobody ran.

So there is no writable content in the section at all. This is the `WELL-S25` position, and it takes
the same answer:

    Reserve the quotation. Author the apparatus.

# The Governing Constraint — A Testimonial Is Worth What You Can Check

    A quotation you cannot verify is a sentence the firm wrote about itself.

So the quote is a reserved block and everything around it — who said it, whether you may ring them,
what was edited, what they were given, and who is missing — is the content. **The apparatus is the
only part a firm can be honest or dishonest about, which makes it the only part worth designing.**

Every quotation in this batch carries five things:

| Field | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| The quotation itself | **Reserved block**, set at prose cadence — lines, not a dash |
| Name, role, organisation | **Reserved fields** |
| **Will they take your call** | **Real** — and the answer varies, which is the point |
| **Was it edited** | **Real** — for length only, or not at all |
| **Were they given anything** | **Real** — nothing. No discount, no fee, no retention released early |

# The Three Groups, And Why They Are Not All The Same

`003` is built on this and every study carries it somewhere. Clients differ in what they can agree
to, and flattening that difference is the first small dishonesty of a testimonials page:

| Group | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Will take your call** | Named, and the number is yours on request. We do not choose which you ring |
| **Quoted, not contactable** | Happy to be named, not to field calls. A busy person, not a reluctant one |
| **Anonymous at their request** | An organisation type only. Public bodies mid-procurement and clients under an NDA |

# The Admission — A Testimonials Page Is A Selected Sample

The sentence the section turns on, and the one no competitor prints:

> **These are the clients who agreed to be quoted. The ones who did not are not here, and we are not
> going to pretend the difference is random.**

Everything above it is apparatus; this is the fact that makes the apparatus worth having.

# And The Offer That Follows From It

`004` converts on it, and it is the most exposed sentence in this sector's catalog:

> **Ask for the ones who are not on this page.** We will give you a reference from a job that went
> badly and the number of the client it went badly for.

A firm that cannot do that has a testimonials page and nothing else. It is checkable in one phone
call, it costs nothing to print if it is true, and it is unprintable if it is not.

# What May Not Appear

| Element | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Quotation text | **Reserved block** |
| Client name, role, organisation | **Reserved fields** |
| Contact policy, editing policy, inducement policy | **Real** |
| Project context — sector, scope | **Real** — generic vocabulary |
| Stars, scores, ratings, *rated excellent* | **Forbidden** — anti-pattern eight |
| An average rating, a review count, a satisfaction percentage | **Forbidden** |
| A logo wall of clients, *trusted by* | **Forbidden** |
| A third-party review platform or its branding | **Forbidden** |
| A client photograph or headshot | **Reserved area** where used, and used sparingly |
| Awards, *five-star*, *excellent*, *outstanding* as the firm's own words | **Omitted** |

**Not present in any study:** a star glyph of any kind, a numeric rating, a review count, an
aggregate score, a client name, an invented quotation, or a digit in visible copy.

# The Reserved Quotation Is Drawn As A Quotation

Carried from `WELL-S25`: a reserved block of running text is drawn **at prose cadence** — three or
four lines of varying length on a declared measure — rather than as a single dash. A dash says *a
value goes here*; lines say *a paragraph of somebody's speech goes here*, which is the honest shape
and tells a reviewer how much of it the finished page will carry.

# Boundary With S03, S09 And S13

| Section | What it owns |
| --- | --- |
| `S03` Projects | The work itself, names reserved |
| `S09` Company Stats | The counter, and why satisfaction figures are absent |
| `S12` Client Testimonials | **What clients said, and whether you can check it** |
| `S13` Case Studies | One job in full, as a method rather than a result |

The test against `S09`: this section may describe **how a quotation was obtained**; it may not
aggregate quotations into anything. The moment a study counts them or scores them it has written a
satisfaction figure, which `S09` omits and the direction forbids.
