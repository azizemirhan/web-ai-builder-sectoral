A quotation you cannot check is a sentence the firm wrote about itself. That is the whole problem with this kind of page, and it is not solved by having more quotations on it.

Why there is no rating anywhere

A score asserts a survey, and no contractor of this size has run one. An average of a handful of quotations is not a measurement of anything — it is a handful of quotations with arithmetic on top, and the arithmetic is there to make a small number of opinions look like evidence.

Stars are worse, because they are borrowed from a context where a stranger buys a thing once and rates it, and a building is not that. So there are none here, and there is nothing to average.

What we can actually offer instead

The apparatus above. Who said it, whether you may ring them, what was cut, and what they were given for it — which is nothing, every time, because a quotation obtained in exchange for something is an advertisement the client wrote for us.

The one that matters is the phone number. A firm willing to hand over a client’s number is making a bet that the conversation will go well, and that bet is the only part of a testimonials page that costs anything to make.

How we ask

At handover rather than during, because a client mid-job has a reason to be generous and everybody knows it. In writing, so nobody is put on the spot in a room. And the cut goes back to them before it goes up, which is why every quotation here says whether it was edited.

Some say no. Some say yes and then their organisation says no, which happens most often with public bodies and with anybody mid-procurement. Neither is a bad sign and neither is our decision to make.

And the part every page like this leaves out

This is a selected sample. These are the clients who agreed to be quoted; the ones who did not are not here, and we are not going to pretend the difference is random. Every testimonials page in this industry works this way, ours included, and one that does not say so is relying on you not to think about it.

So ask for the ones who are not on this page. We will give you a reference from a job that went badly, and the number of the client it went badly for.

That sentence is worth more than every quotation above it. It is checkable in one phone call, it costs nothing to print if it is true, and it is unprintable if it is not — which is the only test of a testimonials page worth applying.
