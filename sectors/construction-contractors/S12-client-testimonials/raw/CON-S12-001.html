Who is not on this page

  • No stars
  • No score
  • No review count
  • No client logos
  • No average anything

These are the clients who agreed to be quoted. The ones who did not are not here, and we are not going to pretend the difference is random. Every testimonials page in this industry is a selected sample, ours included, and a page that does not say so is relying on you forgetting.

There is no rating on it either. A score asserts a survey nobody ran, and an average of four quotations is not a measurement of anything — it is four quotations with arithmetic on top.

So ask for the ones who are not here. We will give you a reference from a job that went badly and the number of the client it went badly for. That is worth more than everything above it, and it is the one thing on this page a competitor cannot copy without meaning it.
