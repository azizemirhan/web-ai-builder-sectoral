These are the clients who agreed. The ones who did not are not on this page.

And we are not going to pretend the difference is random. Every testimonials page in this industry is a selected sample, ours included, and one that does not say so is relying on you not to think about it.

It is set larger than the quotations because it is the only sentence here we can be held to. Praise is cheap to display and impossible to check; a caveat is the reverse.

  • No stars
  • No score
  • No review count
  • No client logos
  • No average anything

A score asserts a survey nobody ran, and an average of three quotations is three quotations with arithmetic on top.

So ask for the ones who are missing. We will give you a reference from a job that went badly and the number of the client it went badly for.
