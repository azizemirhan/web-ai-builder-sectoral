What the widths are telling you

  • No stars
  • No score
  • No review count
  • No client logos
  • No average anything

A page where the narrow column is the full one is telling you something, and this layout would show it rather than hide it. That is the reason for sizing the columns rather than lining them up.

There is no rating anywhere. A score asserts a survey nobody ran, and an average of a handful of quotations is a handful of quotations with arithmetic on top.

These are also the clients who agreed to be quoted, and the ones who did not are not here — and we are not going to pretend the difference is random. Ask for those instead: we will give you a reference from a job that went badly and the number of the client it went badly for.
