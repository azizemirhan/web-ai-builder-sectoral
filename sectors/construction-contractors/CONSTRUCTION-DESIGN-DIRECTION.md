# Construction & Contractors — Sector Design Direction

Prefix `CON`. This document governs the visual language, the anti-patterns, the claims limits and
the copy direction for every `CON` study, in the same role `WELLNESS-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md` plays for
`WELL`. Each section README carries its own reading of it.

# The Register

    Beauty and wellness sells atmosphere. Construction sells the removal of risk.

A visitor to a contractor's site is not being seduced; they are **de-risking a decision that will
cost them a great deal of money and cannot be undone**. Behind every page are four questions:

1. **Can you actually do this one?** — not construction in general, this building, this scope.
2. **Will you finish?** — programme, sequence, what happens when it slips.
3. **Will anybody get hurt?** — and what happens if they do.
4. **Will it cost what you said?** — variations, and who carries them.

Everything in this sector's catalog exists to answer one of those four. A study that answers none of
them is decoration, however good it looks.

**The visual consequence:** the register is weight, evidence and plainness — heavy grotesques, tight
tracking, large photography of real work, concrete and asphalt neutrals with one high-visibility
accent. It is not the luxury-serif register of architecture and real estate, and it is not the soft
atmospheric register of `WELL`. **Where `WELL` avoided looking like a clinic, `CON` avoids looking
like a property developer's brochure.**

# The Governing Constraint — The Counter Is The Signature, And Every Figure In It Is Reserved

Five reference heroes were supplied for `S01`. **Every one of them is built around a number:**

> 10K happy clients · 5K properties sold · 15+ years · 40 years of experience · 50+ projects
> completed · 100+ expert works · $3.5M · 10M+ happy customers · 12k+ properties · 8+ years of
> service · from $8 per sq. m.

That is not a coincidence and it is not a fashion. **The counter is this sector's signature device**,
because a number is the fastest available proxy for *can you actually do this*. It is also the
single largest source of invented content in the sector, so the rule has to be exact.

The `S11` field-versus-figure test from `WELL` decides each one:

    Reserve the field when the empty field asserts nothing.
    Omit it when the empty field asserts a system.

| Figure | Treatment | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Year founded | **Reserved field** | Every firm has one |
| Projects completed | **Reserved field** | A countable fact the firm already knows |
| People employed | **Reserved field** | Same |
| Largest project by value | **Reserved field** | A fact, not a claim |
| Sectors or trades covered | **Real** — generic vocabulary | Not a figure |
| *Happy clients* | **Omitted** | Asserts a satisfaction survey nobody ran |
| *Satisfaction %, on-time %, on-budget %* | **Omitted** | Asserts a measurement system |
| *Safety record, incident rate, days without incident* | **Forbidden** — see below |
| *Awards won* | **Omitted** | Invented bodies, invented judgements |
| Price per square metre, day rate, *from £X* | **Omitted** | `S15` and the estimate own money, and no price survives a real scope |

**A reserved counter still works.** A labelled empty field at counter scale reads as *this is the
number that goes here*, which tells a reviewer what the finished hero asserts — and it makes it
impossible for the catalog to ship a fabricated proof.

# Safety Is Regulated. Treat It That Way.

Safety is the one subject in this sector where a placeholder can do real harm.

**Forbidden outright, in every section, including `S07 Safety Program`:**

- An incident rate, accident-frequency figure, lost-time figure or *days without an incident* board
- *Zero accidents*, *zero harm*, *100% safe*, *perfect safety record*
- An invented certification, accreditation, scheme membership or registration number
- A logo wall of standards bodies
- A named regulation asserted as complied with

**What is authorable instead** — because it describes the firm's own practice rather than its
record: who stops the job and on what authority, what happens after a near miss, what is briefed
before work starts, and who a subcontractor answers to on site. `S07` and `S08` are built from that,
and every certification and registration number in `S08` is a **reserved field**.

# Anti-Patterns

Thirteen, and the first five are the ones that make construction sites look identical:

1. **The handshake in hard hats.** Two people in clean PPE shaking hands over a drawing. Nobody has
   ever done this.
2. **The certification wall** — a row of standards logos, invented.
3. **The counter of unearned numbers** — see above.
4. **Stock plans, stock blueprints, stock CAD wireframe overlays.** A drawing you did not make is a
   drawing of nothing.
5. **The clipboard-and-tablet montage.** Technology as a mood rather than as a capability.
6. **Hi-vis as decoration** — orange used everywhere until it means nothing. One accent, used where
   attention must go.
7. **The countdown or the *limited slots* device.** Nobody buys a building on urgency.
8. **The satisfaction badge** — five stars, a review score, a *rated excellent* seal.
9. **The generic capability list** — *quality, integrity, excellence* — which every competitor also
   claims and none can be checked.
10. **The fake before-and-after of a site.** `WELL` forbade it on regulatory grounds; here it is
    forbidden because the two photographs are never of the same conditions.
11. **The map of pins across a country** asserting coverage nobody verified. `S14` uses named real
    regions or a reserved area.
12. **The sustainability claim without a mechanism** — *green*, *eco*, *carbon neutral* as adjectives.
    `S17` states practices, never labels.
13. **The team page as a wall of stock portraits.** `S11` follows the `WELL-S04` rule: role leads,
    identity reserved.

# The Five Authoring Directions, Read For This Sector

| Direction | This sector's reading | Its trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 001 Universal / Safe | The dependable evidence layout: what we build, proof, one clear ask | Becoming a template with nothing specific in it |
| 002 Premium / Editorial | The firm speaking plainly about how it works, at low density | Drifting into the developer-brochure serif register |
| 003 Structured / Visual Modular | Scope, sectors and capability as legible modules | **Becoming a specification table.** A table of trades is not a design |
| 004 Conversion-led | Starting an enquiry with a real scope, not a contact form | Becoming a quote calculator that invents a price |
| 005 Art-directed / Distinctive | Weight, scale and the machinery of the work itself | Becoming a car advert; the subject is the work, not the plant |

# Media Direction

**Project photography is this sector's only genuine asset**, and every image of it is a reserved
area. Reserved media in `CON` is larger and more central than in `WELL`, because a contractor with
no photographs of finished work has nothing to show.

| Slot | Expected content |
| --- | --- |
| Project media | Completed or in-progress work, wide |
| Site media | Work happening, people in real PPE, not posed |
| Plant media | Machinery in use — `005`'s register |
| Detail media | A junction, a pour, a fixing — the evidence of care |

**No slot may hold:** a stock handshake, a posed group in clean PPE, a certification logo, a
rendered CGI presented as a photograph of a built thing, or a before-and-after pair.

# Copy Direction

Plain, specific, and about work rather than values. The test for any sentence:

    Could a competitor print this sentence unchanged?
    If yes, it is not saying anything.

*"We deliver quality on time and on budget"* passes for every contractor alive and is therefore
worthless. *"We will not price a job we have not walked"* is a position, is checkable, and cannot be
copied by a firm that does not operate that way.

# Section Shell

Every study is a section only. No global header, logo, primary navigation, announcement bar or
footer — with the same `S22` exception the extended architecture set carries: a section whose role
*is* navigation uses navigation markup.

# The V2 Visual Language — Applied To All One Hundred And Thirty-Five Studies

The first authoring run produced correct content in a **document** style: hairline rules, small-caps
micro-labels at eight and nine pixels, body copy under fifteen, near-monochrome grounds and twenty
different frame widths. Read side by side it looked technical rather than designed, and that is not
what this catalog is for.

    The argument stays. The document look goes.

Every study in this sector now carries the V2 language. It is applied by `review/v2-modern-pass.js`,
which rewrites the scoped stylesheet only — **no content, markup, reserved field or isolation rule was
touched**, and all eighteen section checkers still pass.

## The Rules

| Element | V2 |
| --- | --- |
| **Frame** | **One width for the whole sector: `1320px`**, with `clamp(40px, 5vw, 92px)` of vertical air. Nothing is narrower than anything else any more |
| **The opening band** | **A colour-blocked hero panel** — accent gradient, thirty-four pixel radius, white display type, soft shadow. Where a study has no opening container, the headline itself carries the panel |
| **Display type** | Headlines scale up by roughly a quarter and set at weight 800. Nothing on a page is smaller than `0.74rem` any more, and body copy sits at `1rem`–`1.1rem` |
| **Tracking** | Wide micro-label tracking (`0.13em`+) comes down to `0.085em`. Letter-spaced small caps are the strongest single tell of a technical page |
| **Accent** | **One accent family per section**, from a nine-colour set, brightened for dark grounds. It carries the hero, the eyebrow pill, the reserved chips and the media edges |
| **Eyebrows** | Pills, not tracked captions |
| **Cards** | Twenty-six pixel radius, soft long shadow, a hairline edge |
| **Reserved media** | Larger — `min-height: clamp(200px, 21vw, 320px)` — with a dashed accent edge and a tinted gradient, so a placeholder reads as a designed area rather than a hole |
| **Reserved values** | Accent-tinted pills |
| **Geometry** | Every radius under eighteen pixels is rounded up; hard `2px`–`8px` document frames become soft panels |
| **Rhythm** | `clamp(26px, 3.2vw, 50px)` between bands, so nothing crowds a hairline |

## What Did Not Change

The grounds stay one per study and distinct across the sector, the reserved-field conventions stand,
there is still no script, iframe, image, inline style or remote dependency anywhere, and every content
rule each section invented is still enforced by its own checker.

`review/check-sector-structure.js` runs the constants across all one hundred and thirty-five studies:
scoping, frame width, clamp arity, hex validity, ASCII stylesheets, nesting and reduced motion.

# Black And White Only

    From here on this catalog is produced in black and white. No hue, in any sector,
    in any study.

Applied across all one hundred and thirty-five studies by `review/monochrome-pass.js`, and enforced
by `review/check-black-and-white.js`, which fails any study carrying a hex whose channels are not
equal, an `rgb()` that is not neutral, or a hue word in a variable name.

## Why It Is A Re-Grading, Not A Desaturation

Desaturating a colour palette produces mud: an emerald, an amber and a slate of the same lightness
become three identical greys, and a page that told you three things now tells you one.

    Hue was doing work. Value has to do that work instead.

So every study's palette is re-laid on a ten-step ramp from black to white in its original order, and
the tones that used to be told apart by hue are pushed onto a five-step block ladder that spans the
scale rather than clustering in the middle.

## The Rules

| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Ground** | **White paper or black paper.** Four light steps per section, far enough apart to tell studies apart on a contact sheet, and one near-black. Nothing muddy in the middle |
| **The hero** | **Solid black** with white type, on every study in the sector. It is the one black object on a light page |
| **Panels that used to differ by hue** | A five-step ladder: `#0f0f0f`, `#2e2e2e`, `#4d4d4d`, `#6c6c6c`, `#8b8b8b` — all still safe under white type |
| **Cards** | White, with a hairline edge and a soft shadow |
| **Reserved areas** | A dashed edge and a light gradient; the label sits in black |
| **Text** | Black, then two greys. Nothing else |
| **Contrast guard** | Any text-and-background pair that lands closer than ninety levels apart is pushed apart automatically |

## Where Value Now Carries Meaning

The sections that used colour to carry an argument now carry it in value, and in two of them the
result is better than the colour was:

- **`S02-005`** sorts the services by whose hands do the work. Ours is a **solid black** column,
  joint is **mid grey**, and bought in is **an outlined white card** — the outlier drawn as an
  outline, which says more than a third hue did.
- **`S07`** keeps one meaning for its darkest value: the moment something is wrong. There is nothing
  hi-vis anywhere, which was already the rule and is now unavoidable.

## For Every Future Study

Author in black and white from the start. A study that arrives with hue in it is not finished, and
the pass exists to correct the catalog rather than to license colour in new work.

# Detailing Pass — the reference register applied to CON

Status: ACTIVE from 17 September 2026. This pass **supersedes the V2 visual language and the
black-and-white rule above for the design layer of every `CON` study.** The argument, the reserved
fields, the counter rule, the safety limits, the thirteen anti-patterns and the text budget all
stand; the gradient hero panel, the pills, the soft shadows, the dashed slot edges and the
ten-step grey ramp go. The register is `../../standards/08-VISUAL-REFERENCE-STYLE.md`, read the way
`WELL` read it — but this sector is the register's *heavy* reading, and it is deliberately fuller.

## The CON reading in one sentence

    Concrete paper, one heavy tracked-uppercase display voice, hairlines and 3px site-tape bars,
    bare project fields at large scale, a drawing layer behind the composition and a stroke-icon
    set in front of it — everything drawn, nothing photographed, nothing invented.

## What CON translates

| Register element | CON reading |
| --- | --- |
| Display voice | The register's second option only: tracked uppercase grotesque, weight 800, `line-height: 0.9`, `letter-spacing: -0.03em`, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 5.6rem)`. **No serif anywhere** — the serif is the developer-brochure register this sector avoids. One word may carry the accent. |
| Eyebrows and labels | `0.6–0.7rem`, 700, tracking `0.16–0.2em`, uppercase. Index numerals as `01 / 02` in the accent. |
| Rules | 1px hairlines for structure; **one 3px accent bar** per composition — the "site tape" — on the head, the active cell or the promoted row. Never two. |
| Paper | Concrete, bone, steel, white, asphalt — see the theme table. Tinted bands are the paper darkened 4–6%. **No dark panels, no black hero.** The ink is the only black on the page. |
| Radius | `0–2px` on everything. Fields 2px, slots 2px, actions 0. |
| Media fields | Bare, flat, **large** — project photography is the sector's only asset, so fields run 21:9, 3:1, 16:10 and full-column 4:5. Slate labels bottom-left: `PROJECT AREA · 21:9`, `SITE AREA`, `PLANT AREA`, `DETAIL AREA`, `PORTRAIT AREA`. Never a dashed edge, never a gradient. |
| Reserved figures | The counter is the signature: reserved figures are drawn **at counter scale** — a bordered slot `min-width 8–12rem`, `height 3–5rem`, the em dash at `2.2–3.4rem` in the display weight, the label tracked beneath. Small facts (year, reference) use the 1.8rem slot. |
| Actions | 1px bordered rectangle, radius 0, uppercase tracked, **with a leading stroke icon** where it helps; the second route an underlined link with `↗`. Hover fills with the accent. |
| **The drawing layer** | Behind a composition, at `--line` tone or lighter, an inline SVG of construction-drawing geometry: a dimension line with end ticks, a grid, a section arrow, a hatched band, a levelling circle, a chevron run. `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events: none`, `position: absolute`, never over body text, never above 12% visual weight. Every study carries one; no two studies in a section carry the same figure. |
| **The site icons** | A 24×24 stroke set at `stroke-width: 1.6`, `currentColor`, round joins: hard hat, crane, spirit level, tape measure, clipboard, cone, brick course, I-beam, excavator bucket, drainage pipe, safety boot, hi-vis vest, handshake-crossed-out is **not** in the set. Inline SVG only, `aria-hidden`, sized 1.25–2rem, in the accent or the ink. Icons sit beside labels, at the head of cells and on actions — never as a decorative grid of their own. |
| Density | Fuller than `WELL`: a composition carries its head, a drawing figure, an icon-led cell row or ruled ledger, at least one large field and one action or foot line. Text budget unchanged (hero 40–90, standard 90–170, detail 150–230, ceiling 250). |

## Sector rules that remain in force

The counter rule (year founded, projects completed, people employed, largest project by value
reserved; satisfaction, on-time, safety figures, awards and prices omitted), the safety limits,
the media limits and the copy test are unchanged. Reserved fields stay empty. The checker for this
pass is `concheck.ps1`: it enforces the theme tokens, the namespace, the flat register (no pill,
dashed edge, shadow or gradient), inline-SVG-only graphics (`aria-hidden`, no `image`, `use` or
`script` inside), the shell rule, the safety and figure vocabulary, and the h1 rule.

## Completion record — 17 September 2026

The detailing pass is applied to all one hundred and thirty-five `CON` studies, S01 through S27.
Every section carries a `BATCH-V2.md` design record beside its `BATCH-V1.md`; every study passes
`concheck.ps1` (tokens, namespace, flat register, inline-SVG-only, reduced-motion rule, shell rule,
h1 rule, digit and claims vocabulary, field parity) and was rendered and read at 1440.

What the pass settled beyond the table above, and what the next sector should inherit:

- **The drawing layer is assigned by variant, not by section**: `001` setting-out grid, `002`
  levelling circle with crosshair, `003` dimension line with ticks, `004` chevron run behind the
  band, `005` cut-earth hatching down the left margin. Sections whose subject forbids an angle
  (S18 BIM) carry orthogonal datum lines only; sections whose figures *are* the drawing
  (S21 title bars, S26 week bars) carry the dimension line over them.
- **Word indices, never numerals.** The `01 / 02` index in the translation table was withdrawn:
  the checker refuses every digit in visible copy, so indices are `ONE … SIX` and `THE FIRST …`
  at 3rem in the line tone, and V1 copy that carried `01–06` was reworded to the same.
- **Reserved values read "Reserved"** in S21–S27 (the architecture run) and in every contact
  ledger; the em-dash slot is kept for figures at counter scale only.
- **A second colour token, `--no` (deep red, `#8a1f14`–`#8f1f14`)**, carries refusal across
  S17–S27: the will-not-claim edge, the *got it wrong* row, the client-is-not-on-that-list
  line, the away week, the not-ours plate. It is an edge, a top rule, an icon stroke or one
  plate — never a surface behind body text, never a second accent. S17 carries no green.
- **Contact before media** in S19 and S27 is a source-order rule the checker enforces, not a
  layout preference.
- **The 004 tape** is the `mark` underline in `#ffd400` on the display and a 3px top rule on
  the band; the accent `#c99e00` does the text and strokes.
