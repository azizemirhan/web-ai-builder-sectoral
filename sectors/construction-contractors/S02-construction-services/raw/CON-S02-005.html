What we are hired to do

Sorted by whose hands, not by what it is called.

Every contractor's list is the same list, so the list is not the useful part. What is useful is which of these we do with our own people, which we do alongside a specialist, and which we buy in entirely — so that is the order of this page rather than a marker printed beside a menu.

Ours

Our gangs, our supervision, our books. If it is wrong we are already standing there.

Joint

Our people alongside a specialist, with the seam between them named and drawn before either starts.

Bought in

Somebody else's gang, our programme, our liability. This is the column almost nobody prints.

Column two

Ours, with a specialist

Our gang and somebody else's on the same face of the work. The seam is drawn and signed by both before either orders anything.

  • Fit-out & internal finishes

    Partitions, ceilings and finishes with the specialist trades running through them, which is everybody in the same room at once.

    Joint

  • Demolition & enabling

    Taking down and making safe, with a specialist for the parts that need one and our people carrying the rest.

    Joint

  • Piling & substructure

    The rig and the piles are a specialist's; the caps, the beams and the level everything is set from are ours. The seam between those two is the one worth watching.

    Joint

  • Temporary works

    Propping, edge protection and access designed with the engineer, and erected by whoever is standing on it.

Column three

Somebody else's people, on our programme and our liability. You are told which firm before they start, not after something goes wrong.

    Managed

    Managed

  • Mechanical & electrical

    Managed

Reserved area — our own gangs working, wide. No stock handshake, no posed group in clean PPE

And what we will not take on

  • A single-trade package. A specialist will be better at it and cheaper, and we would only be adding a layer of management to somebody else's work.
  • Work we would buy in entirely. If every column-three answer applies and nothing of ours is on the job, that is a mark-up rather than a service, and you should be talking to the specialist.
  • A job we cannot resource in your window. We would rather say so at the first conversation than be the firm that started it and then thinned out.

The columns are checkable, which is the only reason printing them is worth anything. Ask any of our site managers which column a service is in and you will get the same answer, on site, without anybody looking at a page first.
