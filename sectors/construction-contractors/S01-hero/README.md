# Section Role

The opening proposition of the whole firm. It answers, in the time somebody takes to decide whether
to scroll: **what kind of work do you do, at what scale, and what do I do next?**

# The Governing Constraint — The Counter Is The Signature, And Every Figure In It Is Reserved

The sector direction sets the rule; this section is where it is felt hardest, because the hero is
where the counter lives.

| Element | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Year founded / years in operation | **Reserved field** |
| Projects completed | **Reserved field** |
| People employed | **Reserved field** |
| Largest project by value | **Reserved field** |
| Sectors and trades | **Real** — generic vocabulary |
| *Happy clients*, *satisfaction*, *on-time %* | **Omitted** — each asserts a survey nobody ran |
| Safety record, incident figures | **Forbidden** |
| Awards, ratings, review scores | **Forbidden** |
| Price, day rate, *from £X per m²* | **Omitted** |

A reserved counter still does its job: a labelled empty field at counter scale tells a reviewer
exactly what the finished hero will assert, and makes it impossible to ship a fabricated proof.

# What Is Real In A CON Hero

1. **What the firm builds** — real sector vocabulary: commercial fit-out, groundworks, structural
   frame, civils, refurbishment.
2. **At what scale** — described in words rather than in invented figures.
3. **How it works** — one position a competitor could not print unchanged. *"We will not price a job
   we have not walked"* is the batch's example, and it appears in more than one study.
4. **The next step** — one primary action, and at most one alternative.

**Not present in any study:** an invented figure of any kind; a client name, logo or project name; a
certification, accreditation or registration; a safety record or incident figure; an award, rating
or review score; a price or rate; an urgency device; a stock handshake or posed PPE group.

# Media Relationship

Project photography is the sector's only genuine asset, so reserved media here is large and central —
larger than anywhere in `WELL`. Every media area names what it expects and what it may not contain.

# Section Shell

Section only. No global header, logo, primary navigation, announcement bar or footer. **The
reference images all show a site header; it is deliberately absent from every study**, on the
standing section-shell rule and on the same instruction that removed headers from the
`architecture-interior-design` heroes.
