What we will send you

Do not download all six. Tell us what you are trying to find out.

  7. If you are trying to

    Find out what we would charge

    There is no document for this

    And there is not going to be one

    No rate card, no schedule of prices, no downloadable estimate. A price is a function of what you can tell us, so it is a conversation rather than a file — and what to send us in order to get one is set out where you put work to us.

    Put work to us

Two of the seven end in nothing to download, and both are the useful answer

Sending you a template so that the page has something to give you would have cost you a morning and cost us the job at the point somebody built to it. A document library exists to answer questions, not to have something for every question.

Nothing here is gated to collect an email address, nothing goes on this page without an issue date on its cover, and there are no download counts — what other people opened tells you nothing about which one you need. There is no brochure and there are no scanned certificates: a scan proves less than a registration you can check yourself.
