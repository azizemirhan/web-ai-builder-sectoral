Sorted by what makes each one stop being true.

Not by document type, which is how every library in this industry is ordered and which tells you nothing. Every figure worth having is inside one of these and none of them is on this page, because a number in a signed document has a date, a revision and a name against it.

Expires on a date somebody else set

An insurer's renewal, an assessor's revisit. We do not control either date, so both are printed on the document rather than described here — and when the date passes the document becomes a historical record, not a weaker version of itself.

Why two of them are on request

Both end up in somebody's tender file, and we have to know which version you are holding — an answer you quote back to us has to match the one we can evidence.

And not for this reason

Collecting an email address. A document gated for that is a document nobody wanted to write and nobody wants to read, and it is not a reason we use.

Not on this page

No brochure, and no scanned certificates — a scan proves less than a registration you can check yourself. Nothing undated, and no count of who else has downloaded what.
