What we will send you

Every figure you want is in one of these. None of them is on this page.

A number in a signed document has a date on it, a revision and somebody's name against it. A number on a web page has none of those, and can be edited on a Tuesday afternoon by whoever has the login. So the documents hold the figures, and this page tells you which one to open.

Open to anybody

Everything you need in order to decide whether to talk to us. No email address, no form. Somebody working out what we do should not have to trade for it.

Sent on request, and here is why

The two that end up in somebody's tender file. We have to know which version you are holding, because an answer you quote back to us has to match the one we can evidence.

Not a reason we use

Getting an email address. A document gated to capture a lead is a document nobody wanted to write and nobody wants to read.

And four things you will not find here

  • No brochure
  • No scanned certificates
  • No undated document
  • No download count

A brochure is a document made to be handed out rather than read, and a scan of a certificate proves less than a registration you can check yourself — which is why the registrations are listed rather than the certificates pictured. Nothing goes on this page without an issue date on its cover, because a document with no date on it is a claim about a firm that may no longer exist in that form. And how many people have downloaded something tells you what other people did, not whether it answers your question.
