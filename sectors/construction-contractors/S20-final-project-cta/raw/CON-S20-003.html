If you want to take this further

What a closing band usually offers, and what is on offer here.

The small dashed half of each row is the sentence this part of a website normally carries. None of them could be broken, which is why they are all still there — and the large half is what replaces it, written so that you would be able to see us fail to do it.

What is usually said What we undertake instead, and how you would check it
  • Usually Get your free, no-obligation quote today.

    We will come and look at it before we say anything about money

    Whatever you are holding — a sentence, a drawing set, a full package — the next thing that happens is somebody standing on your ground. A figure produced before that has nothing underneath it.

    If we broke itA quotation would arrive from somebody who has never seen the site, and it would move the first time anybody did.

    Ask usWho is coming, and have they seen it?
  • Usually Our expert team is ready to help.

    We will send the person who would run it, not the person who wins work

    In a firm of our size those are two different people, and the second is much easier to send. If the first is on a job and cannot come this week we will say so, rather than send somebody else with the same title on a card.

    If we broke itYou would meet somebody once, at the start, and never see them again after the contract was signed.

    Ask usWill you be on this job, and who else is?
  • Usually No job too large or too small.

    We will tell you at the walk if we are the wrong firm for it

    Too small for us to be sensible on, too far for our people to be there every morning, or a kind of work somebody else is honestly better at. And we will name that somebody, because a refusal without a redirection is just a refusal.

    If we broke itNobody would ever say no, and you would work it out from a price that read strangely without being told why.

    Ask usIs this the sort of job you turn down?
  • Usually We work to your timescales.

    We will say at the walk if your fixed start is not achievable

    A term, a lease, a season that cannot move. If we cannot meet it you will hear that on the day, not in week six — by which point it has become everybody's problem and nobody's fault.

    If we broke itThe programme would become impossible only after the contract was signed, which is when it stops being a conversation.

    Ask usWhat would have to be true for this start to hold?
  • Usually We will be in touch soon.

    We will not chase you

    One call after the visit, from the person who came to it. After that it is your move, and deciding not to go on needs no explanation and no reply to anybody.

    If we broke itYou would get a sequence of emails written by somebody who was not at the visit and does not know what was said at it.

    Ask usWhat happens if I do not reply?

What to have out on the table, so the morning is worth it

  1. Whatever drawings exist, even if they are out of date. Out of date is information; nothing is not.
  2. Who decides, and what that person can sign without asking somebody else.
  3. What fixes your start — a term, a lease, a season. If nothing does, that is useful too.
  4. Anything another firm has already told you, so we can disagree with it in front of you rather than behind them.

And the dashed half is not the only thing missing

Nobody buys a building on urgency, so there is no deadline on this page, no count of places remaining, and no line about our order book filling — the last is the polite form of the same device and it is still a pressure trick.

No free quote, because a number given before the walk is a guess that gets revised upward. No promise about how quickly we reply. And no sequence of reminders afterwards: one call, and then it is yours.
