And the struck half is not the only thing missing

Nobody buys a building on urgency, so there is no deadline on this page, no count of places remaining, and no line about our order book filling — the last is the polite form of the same device and it is still a pressure trick.

No free quote, because a number given before the walk is a guess that gets revised upward. No promise about how quickly we reply. And no sequence of reminders afterwards: one call, and then it is yours.
