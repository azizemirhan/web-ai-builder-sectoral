If you want to take this further

We undertake five things, and here is how you would know.

A closing section is usually a button and a sentence about building something together. This one is a declaration, because a promise nobody could check is decoration — so each undertaking below is written so that you would be able to see us break it, and each carries the question to put to whoever turns up.

  1. The first

    We will come and look at it before we say anything about money

    Whatever you are holding — a sentence, a set of drawings, a full package — the next thing that happens is somebody standing on your ground, rather than a figure arriving in an inbox from a person who has not been there.

    If we broke it

    A quotation would arrive from somebody who has never seen the site, and it would move the first time anybody did.

    Ask us

    Who is coming, and have they seen it?

  2. The second

    We will send the person who would run it, not the person who wins work

    Those are different people in most firms of our size, and the second one is easier to send. If the first is on a job and cannot come this week, we will tell you that rather than send somebody else and give them the same title on a card.

    If we broke it

    You would meet somebody once, at the start, and never see them again after the contract was signed.

    Ask us

    Will you be on this job, and who else is?

  3. The third

    We will tell you at the walk if we are the wrong firm for it

    Too small for us to be sensible on, too far for our people to be there every morning, or a kind of work we are honestly worse at than somebody else. And we will name that somebody, because a refusal without a redirection is just a refusal.

    If we broke it

    Nobody would ever say no, and you would work it out from a price that read strangely without ever being told why.

    Ask us

    Is this the sort of job you turn down?

  4. The fourth

    We will say at the walk if your fixed start is not achievable

    A term, a lease, a season that cannot move. If we cannot meet it, you will hear that on the day rather than in week six — by which point it has become everybody’s problem and nobody’s fault, and the only thing anybody can do is argue about it.

    If we broke it

    The programme would become impossible only after the contract was signed, which is the point at which it stops being a conversation.

    Ask us

    What would have to be true for this start to hold?

  5. And the fifth

    We will not chase you

    One call after the visit, from the person who came to it. After that it is your move, and deciding not to go on with it needs no explanation, no reply, and no awkward conversation with somebody whose target you have become.

    If we broke it

    You would get a sequence of emails written by somebody who was not at the visit and does not know what was said at it.

    Ask us

    What happens if I do not reply?

What to have out on the table, so that the morning is worth it

  • Whatever drawings exist, even if they are out of date. Out of date is information and nothing is not — and the version people have been arguing about is usually the most useful one in the room.
  • Who decides, and what that person can sign without asking somebody else. It is the question that most often turns out to have no answer, and finding that out on the first morning is worth the morning by itself.
  • What fixes your start. If nothing does, that is also useful, and it changes what we would propose.
  • Anything another firm has already told you, so that we can disagree with it in front of you rather than behind them.

And five things this page has not done to you

Nobody buys a building on urgency, so there is no deadline here, no count of places remaining, and no line about our order book filling — that last one is the polite version of the same device and it is still a pressure trick dressed as a courtesy.

There is no free quote, because a number given before anybody has walked the site is a guess that gets revised upward, and by then it has already done its work. There is no promise about how quickly we reply, because that would be a promise about a job nobody has looked at. And there is no sequence of reminders after the visit: one call, and then it is yours.
