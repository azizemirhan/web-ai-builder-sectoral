## The Governing Constraint — The Gates Are The Content, Not The Stages

Every contractor's process section is the same five arrows. *Consultation · Design · Build ·
Handover · Aftercare* costs nothing to write and tells a client nothing they had not already
assumed. It is anti-pattern nine drawn as a diagram.

The information is not in the stages. It is at the joins:

    A process section that only lists what we do is a diagram.
    What a client needs to know is where the doors close behind them.

So every study draws the **gate** between one stage and the next, and every gate carries two halves:

| At every gate | The question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| **What is fixed after this** | *When am I locked in?* |
| **What we need from you before it** | *What is going to be my fault?* |

The second half is the one no marketing process diagram contains. Every such diagram is written
entirely in the contractor's voice, while most delay on a live site is a decision waiting on
somebody on the client's side. Printing that is uncomfortable, checkable, and impossible for a firm
that does not work that way to copy — which is the sector's copy test exactly.

## The Schedule Rule — This Section Omits Where `S05` Reserved

The sector inherits `WELL-S08`: **a sequence is content, a schedule is a claim.** This is the
section where it decides the design, so the batch states the limit precisely:

**No duration, date, programme length or lead time appears anywhere — not as copy, and not as a
reserved field.**

That is the opposite treatment to the one used in the section immediately before it, and the
contrast is the `WELL-S11` test doing real work rather than being quoted:

| Section | Figure | Treatment | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `S05` | People on our books | **Reserved field** | The empty field asserts nothing — every firm has the number |
| `S06` | How long a stage takes | **Omitted entirely** | The empty field asserts a system: that a standard duration exists before anybody has seen the scope |

A labelled empty box reading *Pre-start: — weeks* tells a reviewer that the finished page promises a
fixed programme per stage. The catalog would be shipping the *shape* of a claim with only the number
removed. So the box is not drawn, and **all five studies say so on the page** rather than leaving
the absence silent:

> *A duration printed before anybody has walked your site is a guess wearing a uniform, and a blank
> marked "weeks" makes the same promise more quietly.*

**Two adjacent sections, opposite treatments, one rule.** That pairing is the most portable thing in
this batch.

## No Stage Numbers

The numbered arrow is this section's visual cliché and the counter is this sector's signature
failure, and they are the same mistake: a digit where an argument should be. No study numbers its
stages — not in copy, and not as a CSS counter, which would put the digit on screen while keeping
the markup clean. The checker tests for both.

The stages are ordered by **position and by name** — *to begin with · before any price · then ·
before we start · while it runs · at the end · afterwards* — and the names carry meaning that a
badge reading `03` does not.

### `003`, two lanes — the batch's most useful structural result

Every process diagram in this industry is written entirely in the contractor's lane. Giving the
client's obligations **a lane of the same width, in a colour of its own**, states structurally what
copy could only assert: this is a job with two parties in it, and the delay usually starts on the
other side.

It also required a legitimate re-division of the device. `001`, `002` and `005` carry both halves of
the gate inside the bar; here the *what you owe* half becomes a lane and the bar carries only *what
is fixed*. **A lane can hold an obligation per stage where a bar can only hold one per join** — so
the gate's two halves are not required to live in the same element, only on the same page.

The direction's trap is the specification table. The defence is that the lanes are coloured fields
rather than ruled columns, the cells are rounded cards with their own padding, and the gate bars
break across the entire grid at every join. A table has no rows that cut across it.

### `004`, where the conversion argument came from

The gate system handed this study its own call to action. Every stage after the site walk closes
something; the walk is the last point at which changing your mind costs nothing. So the page
converts on the walk and says exactly that:

> **The walk is the last one where changing your mind costs nothing at all** — which is the only
> honest reason to make it the next thing you do.

That is why the study carries **no urgency device of any kind**. *Nobody buys a building on
urgency* is the sector direction's line, and a page that can point to a real structural deadline
does not need a manufactured one. The CTA note refuses in the same voice: *"This commits you to
nothing and it is not a quotation. If what we see says a specialist would serve you better, we will
tell you that instead, and you will have lost a morning rather than a tender."*

A single plain link, no form — `S05-004` carries this sector's only form and `S15` owns the formal
submission.

### `005`, and a deliberate break with this sector's house habit

The three art-directed studies before this one in `CON` all reached for a dark ground. A fourth
would have been a house style rather than a direction, so `005` is **sand and black with one
accent** — the accent used only for what the client owes, and appearing nowhere else on the page so
it means one thing. The stages step one indent further right at each stage and the black slabs close
across the full width behind them: the layout says what the copy says.

## The Sentence This Section Earns

The sector's copy direction offers one line as its example of a sentence a competitor cannot copy:

> *"We will not price a job we have not walked."*

It appears at the site-walk stage in **all five studies**, and the checker requires it. It is a
position rather than a value, it is checkable on the first phone call, and a firm that prices from
drawings cannot print it.

## Corrections at Render

| Study | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `004` | The rail was labelled *and then, in order* but its last card was **Before all of it** — a backwards reference contradicting its own label | Card removed; rail cut to five, grid retuned |
| `005` | The measure was set on the whole stage block, so the reserved areas inherited reading width and the right third of the page stayed empty for its full length | Measure moved onto the prose; plates break wider, to `min(840px, 66vw)` |

`005`'s is the same family of fault the last two batches recorded — **a measure applied to a
container rather than to the text it is for.** `004`'s is a different and more interesting kind: not
a layout error but a **content error the layout exposed**, caught only because the rail carried an
explicit ordering promise that one of its own cards broke.

Also caught pre-render: an authoring slip left a non-standard attribute on one element in `005`, and
the staircase needed per-stage indent classes rather than an inline custom property, since a raw
study may carry no `style` attribute. Both are now checked for.

## Notes

- Sixth authored batch in the `CON` sector. `S01`–`S06` are complete.
- **The reusable outcome is that a process is described by its irreversibilities.** Any sector whose
  process section is a row of arrows — agencies, architects, software, legal, manufacturing,
  recruitment — has the same latent content: at which step does the client stop being able to change
  their mind cheaply, and what did they owe to get there. Both halves are real, checkable, and
  neither can be copied by a firm that does not work that way.
- The second outcome is the `S05`/`S06` pairing on the field-versus-figure test: **reserve, then
  omit, and say which and why.** Two adjacent sections applying one rule in opposite directions
  demonstrate the rule far better than either does alone.
- The third is `003`'s: the two halves of a device do not have to live in the same element. Splitting
  one half into a lane and leaving the other in a bar produced the most structurally distinct study
  in the batch from exactly the same content.
- `S07 Safety Program` is next in this sector, and it is the one governed hardest by the sector
  direction: every incident figure, *days without* board, invented certification and logo wall is
  forbidden outright, and what is authorable instead is who stops the job and on whose authority.
