# Section Role

Who to ring, about what, and what that person can actually do when they answer.

# The Governing Constraint — The Traffic Is Not The Enquiry

    The people who most need to reach a contractor are not buying anything. They are
    standing on a site, or living next to one.

Every contact page in this industry is built for the enquiry that becomes a job. The traffic is a
neighbour whose wall is shaking at half past six, a client's facilities manager with a defect in a
building that was signed off, a subcontractor chasing a payment, a delivery driver who cannot find
the gate.

So **this section is ordered by whose problem is most urgent, not by whose is most valuable** — which
puts the commercial enquiry last on every study in the batch. The ordering is the argument.

# The Six Routes, In That Order

| Route | Who answers | What they can actually do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Something is happening on a site now** | The site manager, and out of hours the duty phone | Stop it, make it safe, and write it down |
| You live or work next to one of our sites | The same number, which is also on the hoarding | Tell you what is happening and when it stops |
| A defect in a building we finished | Aftercare, against the job reference | Book somebody to look, and say who pays |
| You are a subcontractor or supplier | The person who owns payment, named | Tell you where the invoice is |
| You want to work here | Where applications actually land | Read it. **We do not keep a talent pool** |
| **You have work for us** | Last on the page, and it points at `S15`| Take the scope, and say which of three answers you get |

# The Out-Of-Hours Answer, Which Is The Whole Test

    Out of hours a real number rings a real phone. Whoever picks it up can stop
    something, make it safe, and write it down. **They cannot authorise work, and
    anybody who says they can at that hour is guessing.**

No response-time promise anywhere — the `S06` rule. What is promised instead is **what the person who
answers is able to decide**, which is more use at two in the morning than a service level.

# The Site-Visit Refusal

> **Do not come to a site to talk to us.** You would need an induction to get past the gate, and the
> person you want is probably in a different county. The office is where a conversation happens; the
> site is where work happens.

Not a brush-off — a safety rule, and the same one `S07` states from the other side.

# The Design Problem This Section Sets

A contact section is almost entirely reserved fields: every number, address, mailbox and name. Left
alone it becomes a grid of dashes.

    Every number on this page is reserved. Every sentence saying which one to ring is
    real — and there is more of the second than the first.

That is the batch's governing design rule, and it is checkable: **each study must carry more authored
sentences per route than reserved fields.** The routing is the content; the contacts are the fields.

# What This Section Will Not Do

- **No general enquiry form.** `S15-004` holds this sector's only form, and a form here would be the
  slowest of the six routes wearing the largest button.
- **No *we will get back to you shortly*.** The `S06` schedule rule. What is stated is who answers
  and what they can settle, not when.
- **No map.** Anti-pattern eleven, and `S27` owns offices.
- **No single inbox as the only route.** A page with one address on it is a page that has decided
  every problem is the same size.
- **No opening-hours graphic implying the site keeps them.** A site starts before an office does.

# What Is Real, And What May Not Be Invented

| Element | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Which route belongs to which problem | **Real** |
| What the person who answers can decide | **Real** |
| What they cannot decide | **Real, and kept** |
| The out-of-hours position | **Real** |
| The site-visit refusal | **Real** |
| Every number, address, mailbox and name | **Reserved fields** |
| Job reference, duty phone, aftercare reference | **Reserved fields** |
| A response time, a service level, an answer-within | **Forbidden** |
| A map, a pin, a branch list | **Omitted** — `S27` owns offices |
