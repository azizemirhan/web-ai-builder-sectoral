The plant

Sorted by what fits through the gap.

Ask a groundworker what they want to know about a job and they ask about access before they ask about volume. So the fleet is sorted by what can physically get in.

What is not on this page

  • Makes and models

  • Hours run

The gates above are drawn rather than dimensioned, deliberately. A measurement printed here would be a specification, and a specification tells you what a machine can do somewhere rather than whether it fits your opening.

Send us a photograph of your access and we will tell you which tier you are in. Every picture on this page is of a machine working with somebody in it, because a clean machine on a forecourt is an advertisement rather than evidence.
