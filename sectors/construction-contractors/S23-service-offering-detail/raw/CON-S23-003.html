Every hole that goes through it

Vents, flues, brackets, cables — each one a hole in a thing whose whole job is to have no holes in it, arriving from a different package on a different week.

On most jobs this cell is empty. Nobody owns it, nobody notices, and the second winter is when it gets discussed.

Who owns the joint here

We take it, and we say so at the start. Whoever makes a hole tells us before they make it, and the sealing is ours.
