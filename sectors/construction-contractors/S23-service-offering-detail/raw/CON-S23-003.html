The building envelope

The joint is drawn as a thing, not as the gap between two boxes.

A scope list gives every package a box and leaves the space between the boxes empty, which is exactly the mistake the buildings make. Here the middle cell is the widest of the three, and it is the only one with a name attached to it.

Standard details cover the typical bay. Every job has three junctions the drawings do not show, and they always have two trades in them.

What we doDraw them and get them signed before we start, not when somebody wants the scaffold down.

The sequence
