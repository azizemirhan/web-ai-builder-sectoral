A week in the post

Your job is two mornings of this week, and the diagram says so before the sentence does.

The honest answer to will this person be on my job is a shape rather than a promise. The largest band below is not your site, and a profile page that hid that would be describing somebody with one client.

The darkest band is the honest part. A half day somewhere in the week goes to something that has gone wrong, and it is not always on the job that caused it. A week drawn without it would be a week nobody in this trade has ever had.

The two fortnights that suspend the pattern

The fortnight either side of a date that cannot move

A pour, a lift, watertight, handover. Your site becomes every morning, and another client on this page is reading that their pattern thins out in the same weeks.

The hour after the work stops

Not a response-time promise. A stopped site costs more than the drive, so the drive happens, and whatever was on the diagram that afternoon does not.

Settled standing on your site

  • The sequence inside the week. Which trade goes where, and in what order.
  • What is ordered for tomorrow, so a gang is not stood waiting on it.
  • Stopping the work, without needing the reason accepted first.

These are the three worth a visit. Everything else on a walk is looking and listening.

Has to ring somebody about

  • Anything with money on it. Priced by the commercial manager, not agreed on a walk.
  • Anything that moves the completion date. A change to the contract, and it goes to a director.
  • Taking somebody off site for good. A day is theirs to call; permanently is not.

A page listing only what somebody can decide is describing an owner, not an employee. This side tells you how long an answer takes.

The weeks they are not here at all

Holiday, booked

Told in advance, with the cover named rather than implied, and walked round the site before the week starts.

Illness, not booked

Covered the same morning. You will notice, because the walk is slower — somebody reading the job rather than remembering it.

Moved to another job

The reason nobody prints. It happens when another site is in trouble, and you are told at the time rather than left to notice.

Absence costs anything at all because a person carries a job in their head. Three things are written down on every job, and they are what makes a week of cover survivable:

  1. The outstanding items, with who owes each one and the day it was asked for.
  2. The drawing revision the work is actually being built to, which is not always the latest one issued.
  3. The things the client has asked for that are not in the contract yet — the line that saves an argument in month four.

What is not in any module above

No qualifications, memberships or training cards, and not as empty fields either. An empty field says we keep such a list and that this person's is pending.

No count of anything and no sentence in quotation marks. A quote attributed to a name that has not been filled in is a statement by a person who does not exist.

And no dates in the week above. A diagram with a date on it is a booking, and nobody has booked anything by reading a page.
