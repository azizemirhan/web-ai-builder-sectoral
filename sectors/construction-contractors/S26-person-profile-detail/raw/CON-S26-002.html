The post

What I am, and what I am not

I run the job above the site. I hold the programme, the labour across more than one job, and the conversation that decides whether a change is a change or an argument.

I am not on any site every day. The person who is there every day is the site manager, and a page that blurs the two is selling a visit nobody is going to make.

The pattern

When I am on your site

  • Two mornings in a normal week, walked before anything else in the day.
  • Every morning in the fortnight either side of a date that cannot move. A pour, a lift, watertight, handover.
  • Inside the hour if the work has stopped, which is not a promise about response time but simply what a stopped site is worth.

Authority

What I settle, and what I have to ring about

I settle the sequence inside the week, what gets ordered for tomorrow, and stopping the work. Those happen in front of the client, and they are most of what the visit is for.

I ring somebody about anything with money on it, anything that moves the completion date, and taking a person off site for good. A note that lists only what I can decide would be describing an owner rather than an employee, and it would leave you guessing how long an answer takes.

On paper

What is in my head that has to be on paper

This is the part that makes a week of cover survivable, and it is the honest reason we write anything down at all.

  • The outstanding items, with who owes each one and the day it was asked for.
  • The drawing revision the work is actually being built to, which is not always the latest one issued.
  • The things the client has asked for that are not in the contract yet — the line that saves an argument in month four.

Absence

Why I might not be here

Holiday, which you knew about. Illness, which you did not. And the one nobody prints: I get moved to a job that is in trouble.

When that happens the client is told at the time. The alternative is letting the visits quietly thin out, and a client who works that out on their own is right to draw the worst conclusion from it.

On the missing photograph

There is no portrait on this page, and nothing above needed one. A profile that stops making sense without a face was never about the work.

A note survives the person. That is the whole point of writing one.

What is not written here

No qualifications, memberships or training cards, and not as empty fields either. An empty field says we keep such a list and that this one is pending.

No count of anything. No sentence in quotation marks: a quote over a name that has not been filled in was written by nobody.

If you need somebody today

Ask for this person by name and you will get them or the cover. At seven in the morning you want the site, not this person — they are driving, and the answer is standing on the slab.
