CONTACT DETAILS AWAITING CONFIRMATION is a graphite chip — the sector's `--no` — above V1's line in semibold ink; no form, enquiry control or map is drawn; the field is a bare 8px area labelled THE ENTRANCE. The darkened surfaces are the 001 plate foot, the 003 contact cell and the 004 band. Copy is V1's throughout.
