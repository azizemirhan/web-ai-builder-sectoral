Learning notes / Choosing a course

Before you choose your next course.

A course can begin with curiosity, a practical need or a change of direction. Before comparing options, make a little space to understand what you want from the experience.

One

Start with a question

What would you like to explore, practise or understand more clearly? Write a short answer in your own words. You do not need a complete career plan; a useful starting point might simply be a subject you keep returning to.

Two

Picture an ordinary week

Think about when and where learning could fit alongside your existing commitments. Compare the published study format, expected workload and attendance requirements. Note anything that needs clarification instead of assuming every course works the same way.

Three

Read beyond the title

Look at the topics, activities and assessment approach. Keep a short list of questions about the content and entry requirements. Return to those questions when you speak with the institution, and use the answers to make your own comparison.
