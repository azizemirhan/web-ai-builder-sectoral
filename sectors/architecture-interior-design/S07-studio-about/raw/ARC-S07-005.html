The studio is an architecture and interior design practice working across houses, workplaces and small civic buildings. We read a site before we draw, keep the number of construction systems in a project low so each junction can be detailed properly, and stay on a commission until the last thing is fixed in place. The work is made by the same small team from the first survey to the final handover set.
