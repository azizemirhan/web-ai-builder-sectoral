The studio

Why not make the room you live in
the reason you came home?

The studio works on spaces that people occupy every day. That means planning for quiet, for storage, for how light moves across a floor between morning and evening, and for what a surface looks like once it has been lived on for a while.

We work across architecture and interiors, and we keep the same team on a project from the first survey through to the final handover set.
