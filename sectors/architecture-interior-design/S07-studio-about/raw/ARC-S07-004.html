The studio works across residential and small civic projects, and across the whole arc of one: reading the site, testing the plan, drawing the details, and staying on the project while it is built.

Every commission arrives with its own constraints, so the scope is written around them rather than assembled from a standard list. If that is how you want to work, the first conversation is short.
