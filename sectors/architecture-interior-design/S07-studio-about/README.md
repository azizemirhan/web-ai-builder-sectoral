# Section Name

Studio About

# Sector

Architecture & Interior Design

# Prefix

ARC

# Purpose

Introduce the practice itself: who it is, how it is structured, and how it works.

# Visitor Intent

The visitor wants to know who they would be working with — the size and shape of the practice, its
disciplines, and where it is based.

# Content Responsibility

Studio identity: disciplines, collaboration and practice structure, team presence, locations, and
the relationship between the architecture and interiors sides of the work.

# In Scope

- Studio identity and a concise account of the practice
- Disciplines covered and how they work together
- Practice structure and collaboration
- Team presence and locations, at summary level
- Studio, workplace or project imagery

# Out Of Scope

- A global site header, primary navigation or footer
- **Design positions and principles**, which belong to S05 — About must not become a second
  manifesto
- The full team roster, which belongs to S08
- Full location detail, which belongs to S17 and S27
- A colophon, register or imprint page used as the section's identity
- Fabricated founding dates, staff numbers, registrations or memberships

These are authoring and research directions, not production enums, and the study IDs do not change
with them. The five studies must differ structurally. They must not become five colour schemes,
five font themes, five cosmetic variants, or five copies of one grid with the content swapped.

In this section:

- **003** organises several facets of the practice — disciplines, structure, locations — into
  distinct visual modules rather than into a longer account.
- **005** is an art-directed studio portrait. It should remain art-directed rather than defaulting
  to a colophon, imprint or practice register.
