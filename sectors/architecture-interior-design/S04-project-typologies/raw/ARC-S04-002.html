Who are we building for?

Every typology begins with the person who will use the space and the conditions the site already sets. The four below are how the studio groups its work.

Typology set

  • Families

    Houses and apartments planned around daily routine, storage, and shared rooms that take wear.

    Image area

  • Single dwellings

    One-off houses where the site, the orientation, and the brief set the plan rather than a template.

    Image area

  • Workplaces

    Studios and offices arranged for focus and for the moments people actually work together.

    Image area

  • Shared buildings

    Small civic and cultural programmes where circulation, light, and acoustics carry the design.

    Image area

Each typology carries its own constraints, and its own way of being drawn.
