Is this the right service for you?

Choose a feasibility study if

  • FitsYou have a site or a building and an idea, and want to know whether they meet.
  • FitsA decision to buy, sell or keep depends on what could be built.
  • FitsYou want the constraints written down before spending on design.
  • FitsTwo or three options need comparing on the same terms.

Choose something else if

  • OtherThe scheme is already agreed and needs drawing up.
  • OtherA submission deadline is fixed and close.
  • OtherOnly the inside is changing and the shell is settled.

In those cases the right route is technical design, planning and permissions or interior architecture. The studio will say so rather than sell this one.

What the study includes

  • Site visit and photographic record
  • Review of the constraints on the record
  • Capacity testing in plan and section
  • Two or three options, drawn to one standard
  • Comparison of the options against the brief
  • Written report with a recommendation

Not included: statutory applications, structural or services design, cost planning, and any valuation. The report sets out what is possible and what would have to be resolved — it is not a permission and it is not a price.
