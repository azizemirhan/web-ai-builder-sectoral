1.0

Description of the service

Placeholder description. Technical design takes a scheme that has already been agreed and resolves it to the level at which it can be priced, built and inspected. Junctions are drawn, materials are specified by performance and by name, and the drawings are coordinated against the consultants' information rather than issued alongside it.

The service continues through construction. Queries from site are answered against the issued information, and the record is kept current so that what was built and what was drawn do not drift apart.

Scope: included and excluded

2.1  Included in the appointment

  • InclGeneral arrangement drawings, coordinated
  • InclAssembly and junction details
  • InclDoor, window and finishes schedules
  • InclWritten specification, performance and named
  • InclCoordination of consultant information
  • InclResponses to contractor queries
  • InclPeriodic site inspection against the drawings
  • InclRecord of changes issued during construction

2.2  Excluded from the appointment

  • ExclStructural and civil engineering design
  • ExclMechanical, electrical and public health design
  • ExclFire engineering and specialist certification
  • ExclCost planning and contract administration
  • ExclTemporary works and construction methodology
  • ExclStatutory applications and their fees

Excluded items are not gaps. Each is normally covered by a separate appointment, and the studio coordinates with whoever holds it.

Assumptions and conditions

4.1  The service assumes

  • AA developed design signed off before T1 begins
  • AConsultants appointed and issuing to a shared programme
  • AA measured survey of adequate accuracy
  • AOne route for client decisions and comments

4.2  Conditions that change the scope

  • CDesign changed after an issue for construction
  • CStatutory conditions requiring redesign
  • CGround or fabric found to differ from survey
  • CProcurement route changed mid-appointment

Where a condition applies, the change is agreed in writing before the affected work is done.

6.0

Questions raised most often

Can the studio take on technical design for a scheme it did not design?
Yes. The scheme is reviewed first, and anything unresolved is listed before the appointment is agreed.
Who coordinates the consultants?
The studio coordinates its own information against theirs. Appointing and managing the consultants remains with the client unless a separate appointment says otherwise.
What happens if the contractor proposes an alternative product?
It is reviewed against the specified performance and either accepted in writing or refused with the reason stated.
Does the service include being on site?
It includes periodic inspection against the issued drawings. Continuous supervision is a different appointment and is not included.
How are changes recorded?
Every issue is revisioned, and superseded sheets stay in the record rather than being replaced silently.
