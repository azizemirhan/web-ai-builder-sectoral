Service detail

Interior architecture

Designing the inside of a building as one set of decisions — layout, light, joinery and surface — rather than as a finish applied to a plan someone else settled.

What this service covers

Placeholder description. The service is defined by what it decides: where the walls go, how daylight reaches the rooms that need it, what is built in and what is loose, and how the surfaces meet each other.

It runs from a measured survey through to information a joiner and a contractor can build from. It can follow the studio's own architectural work or begin on a building somebody else designed.

How it runs

  1. 01

    Survey and brief

    The interior is measured and recorded, and the brief is written against what is actually there.

  2. 02

    Layout studies

    Arrangements are tested in plan and section until one answers the brief, with the reasoning written down.

  3. 03

    Detail and specification

    Joinery, lighting and surfaces are drawn and scheduled, and the specification follows the drawings.

  4. 04

    Installation

    The studio reviews the work as it is installed and answers the queries that come back.

Who it is for

Placeholder description. This service suits a project where the shell is settled and the decisions that remain are about how the inside works: a house being reorganised, a workplace being fitted out, or a building whose use is changing.

It is less suited to a project that still needs its plan or its structure resolved. That work sits in architectural design, which is listed below.
