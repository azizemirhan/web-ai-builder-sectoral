Is a visit worth arranging?

Come here if

  • FitsYou want to see materials and samples in daylight rather than on a screen.
  • FitsA drawing set is easier to read across a table than in a call.
  • FitsSeveral people need to be in the same room to decide something.
  • FitsYou are already nearby and would rather talk in person.

Do something else if

  • OtherThe question can be answered in writing.
  • OtherThe work is on your site and should be seen there.
  • OtherYou are only beginning to think about a project.

In those cases write to the studio, ask for a site visit instead, or start with a feasibility study.

How a visit is arranged

  1. Step 01

    You ask

    Say what the visit is for and roughly when suits you. Nothing is committed by asking.

  2. Step 02

    The studio proposes a time

    Held against who needs to be in the room. No time is promised on this page.

  3. Step 03

    You are told where to come

    The entrance, the floor and who to ask for are confirmed in writing beforehand.

Before you come

Worth bringing

  • Any drawings or surveys you already hold
  • Photographs of the space as it is now
  • A note of what has to be decided
  • Anyone else whose agreement is needed
