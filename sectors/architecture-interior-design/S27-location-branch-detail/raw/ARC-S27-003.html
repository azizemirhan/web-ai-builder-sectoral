Placeholder location page · structure demonstration only. The densest register in this section: an identification band and three tables — contact and address, hours by day, access and arrival — with everything open on the page and nothing behind a control. No travel duration or distance is stated as a number anywhere; each is a reserved field, because this is the layout where "twelve minutes from the station" gets invented. No review, rating or claim is attributed to the location, and the map is a reserved slot with no embedded service. The breadcrumb and identification band overlap ARC-S21 and ARC-S22.
