Capabilities

Four things the studio is built to do

Not a list of everything possible, but the four capacities the practice is actually organised around — the ones that decide which commissions it takes.

  1. 01

    Reading a site

    Understanding what is already there before proposing anything, so later decisions can be traced to a condition rather than a preference.

    • Measured and condition survey
    • Constraint and orientation review
    • Existing-fabric assessment
  2. 02

    Resolving a plan

    Testing arrangements in plan and section until one answers the brief, and being able to say why the others were set aside.

    • Option studies and massing
    • Interior layout and light
    • Model and drawing review
  3. 03

    Detailing a junction

    Drawing the places where two systems meet, early enough that the specification follows the drawing rather than the other way round.

    • Construction detailing
    • Specification writing
    • Joinery and fixed furniture
  4. 04

    Holding a project together

    Coordinating consultants and answering site queries so the building that gets made is the one that was agreed.

    • Consultant coordination
    • Tender and procurement support
    • Site review and handover
