Placeholder person profile page · structure demonstration only. The register is the one-page curriculum sheet a practice issues with a bid: a ruled sheet with an issue line, a portrait paired with the post, a coverage matrix of project type against stage, an experience register, and a verification block. The coverage matrix is the organising device, which is what separates this from the drawing-sheet title block used in ARC-S01-005 and ARC-S03-005. Every cell of the matrix states involvement in a word rather than a mark. The verification block is present and empty by design — on a real sheet the practice fills it and the client checks it, and no study may invent a qualification, registration, membership or award. The return line overlaps ARC-S22.
