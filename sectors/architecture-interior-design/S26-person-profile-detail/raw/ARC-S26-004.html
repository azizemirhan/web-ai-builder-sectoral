How an introduction works

  1. Step 01

    You write to the studio

    Name the post and say what the work is. Nothing is committed by asking.

  2. Step 02

    The studio confirms the fit

    If another post suits the work better, that is said before a meeting is arranged.

  3. Step 03

    A conversation is arranged

    Held with the person named, or with the post that actually carries the work.

What to include when you write

  • Where the site or building is — a town or a postcode is enough
  • What you are hoping to do with it
  • Anything already known about what stands in the way
  • Whether anyone else is already appointed
