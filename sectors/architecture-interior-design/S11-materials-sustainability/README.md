# Section Name

Materials & Sustainability

# Sector

Architecture & Interior Design

# Prefix

ARC

# Purpose

Show how the practice works with materials and what it holds itself to environmentally.

# Visitor Intent

The visitor wants to see the material sensibility of the practice, and to understand its
sustainability position without wading through policy.

# Content Responsibility

Material presence and environmental position: samples and textures, how materials are chosen and
paired with projects, and a small set of sustainability principles or criteria.

# In Scope

- Material samples and texture fields
- Close-up detail imagery
- Material and project pairings
- Concise material principles
- Sustainability criteria stated briefly
- Visual comparison between materials or approaches

# Out Of Scope

- A global site header, primary navigation or footer
- **Material schedules, specification sheets, performance tables or compliance registers** as the
  section's primary device
- Fabricated certifications, ratings, standards compliance or performance figures
- Extended environmental policy text

These are authoring and research directions, not production enums, and the study IDs do not change
with them. The five studies must differ structurally. They must not become five colour schemes,
five font themes, five cosmetic variants, or five copies of one grid with the content swapped.

In this section:

- **003** organises a larger material set through sample fields, grouped panels and comparison
  blocks — not through a specification table.
- **005** is an art-directed material composition: texture, close-up, light and scale. Not a
  material schedule.

**Recorded correction.** `ARC-S11-003` and `ARC-S11-005` were reviewed as too technical and too
text-heavy, and contain no material imagery at all — the exact failure this section cannot afford.

# Architecture Direction

Sector direction: `../ARCHITECTURE-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`.

This section's Architecture strength is **visual materiality**. Samples, textures and close-ups are
the content; principles are the caption. A materials section with no material imagery has avoided
its role.

# Section Shell

Section only. No global header, navigation or footer.

# Media Relationship

Central. Sample and texture areas are the primary content, and must be reserved at a size that
shows the material relationship.

# Interaction Notes

Optional. Sample switching or comparison may use interaction; the full set must remain reachable
without script.

# Responsive Considerations

Sample fields must stay large enough to read as material at small widths rather than collapsing to
thin bands, and a comparison must remain legible when its columns stack.

# Authoring Questions

- **Primary visual element:** material samples, textures and close-up detail imagery.
- **Immediate understanding:** how this practice treats material, and what it commits to.

Full authoring question list: `../ARCHITECTURE-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`.
