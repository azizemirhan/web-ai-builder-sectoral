Materials & sustainability

We choose few materials and use them well

A short palette is easier to detail, easier to source and easier to repair. It also means every material in a project has had to argue for its place against the others.

These are the questions a material has to answer before it is specified. None of them is about how a surface photographs.

  1. 01Where does it come from, and can that be documented?
  2. 02How does it behave after a decade of ordinary use?
  3. 03Can a damaged part be reached and replaced on its own?
  4. 04Can it be separated from what it is fixed to at the end?

Placeholder board · structure demonstration only. No certification, rating scheme, standard, carbon figure, energy figure or percentage appears in this study, and none is implied. The four questions describe how the studio selects, not what the selection achieves.
