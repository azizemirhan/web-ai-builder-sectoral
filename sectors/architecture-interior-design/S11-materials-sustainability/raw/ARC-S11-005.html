What a wall is made of, layer by layer

Sheet ARC / S11 · placeholder

Materials are easiest to talk about in the drawing where they actually matter: the build-up. Read outside to inside. Each layer is annotated with what it does and what has to be true of it — not with a product name.

  1. Layer 01
    01

    Outer weathering surface

    Takes the weather and is expected to change colour doing it. Fixed in units that can be lifted off one at a time.

  2. Layer 02
    02

    Ventilated cavity

    Keeps the outer layer drying and separates it from everything behind, so one can be replaced without the other.

  3. Layer 03
    03

    Insulation zone

    The thickest layer in the build-up. Held mechanically rather than bonded, so it can be taken out whole at the end of the building's life.

  4. Layer 04
    04

    Air and moisture control layer

    Continuous, and detailed at every junction rather than only in the middle of the panel — where it is lapped is where it fails.

  5. Layer 05
    05

    Structure and internal lining

    Carries the load and receives the finish. Services run in a separate zone so the control layer is not punctured to reach a socket.
