Materials

The palette the studio keeps returning to

A short list rather than a long one. Each entry says where the material is used and what the studio asks of it before specifying it.

  • Sample area

    Material 01

    Used for
    Structure and exposed frame
    What we ask of it
    That it can be sourced with a documented origin and repaired in place.

  • Sample area

    Material 02

    Used for
    External wall and weathering surface
    What we ask of it
    That it weathers visibly rather than depending on staying new to look right.

  • Sample area

    Material 03

    Used for
    Internal lining and ceiling
    What we ask of it
    That it can be taken off and put back without destroying what sits behind it.

  • Sample area

    Material 04

    Used for
    Floors in occupied rooms
    What we ask of it
    That it takes daily wear and can be refinished rather than replaced.

  • Sample area

    Material 05

    Used for
    Joinery and fixed furniture
    What we ask of it
    That its fixings stay reachable, so a damaged part is a repair and not a rebuild.

  • Sample area

    Material 06

    Used for
    Insulation and build-up
    What we ask of it
    That it is separable from the layers around it at the end of the building's life.
