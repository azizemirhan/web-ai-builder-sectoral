Design philosophy

We design buildings that answer the place they stand in.

The studio works from the conditions a project already has — orientation, structure, budget, and the way a space will actually be used — rather than from a preferred style applied to every site.

What we believe

Architecture built on restraint, useful detail, and materials that are allowed to age.

We treat proportion and daylight as the first decisions, not the finishing ones. A plan that works in section, in use, and in ordinary weather needs less decoration to hold together.

Detail is where a building either lasts or does not. We draw junctions early, specify materials we can stand behind, and keep the number of different systems in a project as low as the brief allows.
