The studio works to a written position rather than a house style. The clauses below are the ones we return to when a decision is contested, and they are the ones we expect to be held to.

  1. 01.1

    Context precedes form

    The site is surveyed and understood before a form is proposed. Ground, orientation, boundary, and what already stands nearby are treated as given conditions, not as obstacles to a preferred outcome.

  2. 01.2

    The plan carries the building

    Movement, daylight, storage, and sequence are resolved in plan and section first. Elevation follows from that resolution rather than being composed independently of it.

  3. 01.3

    Materials are declared, not disguised

    Surfaces are what they appear to be, and are selected for how they weather. Where two materials meet, the junction is drawn rather than covered.

  4. 01.4

    Buildings are designed to be maintained

    Assemblies allow a component to be reached, replaced, or renewed without dismantling what surrounds it. Longevity is treated as a design requirement, not as an outcome hoped for after handover.
