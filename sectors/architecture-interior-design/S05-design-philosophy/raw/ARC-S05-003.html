How the studio thinks

Six positions the practice keeps returning to, whatever the scale of the project or the stage it arrives at.

  • Read the site first

    Orientation, ground, boundary and neighbours are surveyed before anything is drawn, because they decide more than taste does.

  • Plan before image

    If the plan is right the elevation usually follows. We resolve movement, storage and daylight before we consider how a building photographs.

  • Daylight as a material

    Openings are sized and placed for the light they bring in across a day and a year, not only for the view out or the pattern on a facade.

  • Fewer systems, better detailed

    We keep the number of construction systems in a project low, so each junction can be drawn properly and built the way it was drawn.

  • Honest materials

    Surfaces are what they appear to be. We prefer materials that weather visibly to finishes that depend on staying new to look right.

  • Built to be repaired

    Assemblies are designed so that a component can be reached, replaced or renewed without taking the rest of the building apart.
