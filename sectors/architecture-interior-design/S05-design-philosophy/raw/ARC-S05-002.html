We design for the long middle of a building's life — the ordinary years after the photographs, when material, light and plan have to keep working. Our aim is not to build more, but to build what will still make sense when the brief that produced it is forgotten.

Material

We specify a small number of materials and let them meet each other plainly, so repairs stay possible and surfaces are allowed to weather.

Restraint

Every added element has to earn its place against the plan. What is left out is as deliberate as what is drawn.
