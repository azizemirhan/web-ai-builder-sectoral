# Section Name

Design Philosophy

# Sector

Architecture & Interior Design

# Prefix

ARC

# Purpose

State how the studio thinks about design: the positions and principles behind the work.

# Visitor Intent

The visitor has seen the work and wants to know what drives it — whether this practice thinks
about space the way they do.

# Content Responsibility

A small set of design positions, expressed concisely and supported visually.

# In Scope

- A few concise principles or positions
- Manifesto-register typography
- Project, spatial or material imagery supporting the positions
- Short editorial statements, visually separated from one another

# Out Of Scope

- A global site header, primary navigation or footer
- **Who the practice is** — identity, structure, team and history belong to S07
- Long theoretical essays
- Clause numbering, register or contract-document framing
- Process stages, which belong to S06

These are authoring and research directions, not production enums, and the study IDs do not change
with them. The five studies must differ structurally. They must not become five colour schemes,
five font themes, five cosmetic variants, or five copies of one grid with the content swapped.

In this section:

- **003** separates several positions into distinct visual modules with their own imagery — it does
  not become the text-dominant variant of a text-prone section.
- **005** is an art-directed statement: scale, typography, material and light. Not a clause set, a
  numbered register or a revision-stamped document.

# Architecture Direction

Sector direction: `../ARCHITECTURE-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`.

This is the section most likely to drift into prose. Keep each position short enough to be read in
one pass, give it visual separation, and let imagery carry as much of the idea as the words do.

# Section Shell

Section only. No global header, navigation or footer.

# Media Relationship

Expected. Spatial and material imagery should support the positions rather than decorate them.

# Interaction Notes

Normally none.

# Responsive Considerations

Large statement typography must remain readable and unclipped at 320px, and positions must not
merge into an undifferentiated column when their separators reflow.
