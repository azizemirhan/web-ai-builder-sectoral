01 / Project brief

A low structure aligned with the land

The project begins with orientation, level and the relationship between sheltered rooms and an exposed setting.

The page records a design proposition without asserting a real site, client or completed performance. The form of the case study follows an architect’s drawing issue so narrative and technical material can sit together.
