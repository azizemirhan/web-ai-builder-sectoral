House Between Walls

A long-form project story about making room within a narrow existing structure.

The editorial format moves from inherited condition to spatial idea and material resolution. All names, places and project facts are illustrative placeholders.

01 Inherit ↓02 Open ↓03 Resolve ↓
Chapter 01 / Inherit

Start with what the place already knows

The first design act is to read rather than remove.

Existing walls, changes in level and traces of earlier use become evidence. The project brief is framed as a careful reorganization, not a claim of wholesale transformation.

A narrow plan asks for precise decisions: where light can travel, where movement can pause and where old and new work should meet without imitation.

The project keeps the weight of the walls while making the route through them feel open.

Chapter 02 / Open

A sequence of rooms, not a single open plan

Connection is created through calibrated openings and shared sightlines.

Each room retains a distinct atmosphere while the full depth of the site becomes legible. Daylight is treated as part of circulation, marking the path from street to garden.
