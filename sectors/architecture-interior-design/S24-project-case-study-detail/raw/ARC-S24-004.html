01 / Project context

Make the outside part of the working day

The brief begins with use: how could a compact existing structure support different ways of working without feeling divided?

The proposal treats the route through the building as shared space. Openings, thresholds and built-in elements connect team activity to a planted edge.

No performance claim is invented; the page records the design logic and scope as illustrative case-study content.
