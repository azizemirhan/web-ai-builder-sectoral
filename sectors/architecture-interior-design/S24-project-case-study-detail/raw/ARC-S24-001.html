Courtyard House

A complete case-study structure for a home reorganized around daylight, shared space and an existing garden.

LocationSample City

01 / Context

A house turned toward its centre

The brief asked how an existing home could support a more connected daily life without losing the character already present.

The case study begins with the situation rather than an outcome claim: a sequence of disconnected rooms, limited daylight and a garden that felt separate from the interior.

The proposal uses a new courtyard relationship as the organizing idea, allowing circulation, shared rooms and quieter spaces to read as one coherent whole.

02 / Approach

Three connected decisions

The project narrative is structured around decisions that can be understood even when imagery is absent.

A

Open the threshold

Rework the point between existing rooms and the garden.

B

Gather the plan

Place shared uses around a clear central route.

C

Keep the traces

Retain useful fabric and make new work visibly precise.
