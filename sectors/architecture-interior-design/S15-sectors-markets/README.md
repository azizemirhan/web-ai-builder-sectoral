# Section ID

ARC-S15

# Section Name

Sectors Markets

# Sector

Architecture & Interior Design

# Prefix

ARC

# Purpose

Show the contexts and markets the practice works in, so a visitor from one of them recognises
themselves.

# Visitor Intent

The visitor is checking whether the practice understands their world — their sector, their kind of
client, their constraints.

# Content Responsibility

The market set: each market named, illustrated with real project imagery, and given a short
descriptor and a route onward.

# In Scope

- Visual market categories
- Project imagery per market
- Short descriptors
- Image-led or interactive navigation into the relevant work

# Out Of Scope

- A global site header, primary navigation or footer
- **Maps, matrices and atlases used primarily as a styling gimmick**
- Building typologies, which are a different axis and belong to S04
- Fabricated client names, market share, coverage claims or regional presence

These are authoring and research directions, not production enums, and the study IDs do not change
with them. The five studies must differ structurally. They must not become five colour schemes,
five font themes, five cosmetic variants, or five copies of one grid with the content swapped.

In this section:

- **003** organises a broader market set through image-led modules and grouping, not through a
  capability matrix.
- **005** is an art-directed market composition rather than an atlas or coverage map.

**Recorded correction.** `ARC-S15-003` and `ARC-S15-005` are matrices with no media and are later
reworks. This section currently carries the least media in the sector.

# Architecture Direction

Sector direction: `../ARCHITECTURE-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`.

A market is recognisable through the work done in it. Carry the section with project imagery and
short descriptors; a matrix is not the sector-native answer here.

# Section Shell

Section only. No global header, navigation or footer.

# Media Relationship

Expected. Project imagery per market. A market presented as a label alone has not been shown.

# Interaction Notes

Optional. Image-led switching or filtering is justified; all markets must remain reachable without
script.

# Responsive Considerations

Market imagery and label must stay paired; a matrix must not survive into small widths as a
horizontally scrolling table.

# Authoring Questions

- **Primary visual element:** project imagery representing each market.
- **Immediate understanding:** whether the practice works in the visitor's world.

Full authoring question list: `../ARCHITECTURE-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`.
