Design process

Four stages, drawn in order

Each stage closes on something drawn, agreed and dated before the next one opens. Nothing is carried forward on the assumption that it will be resolved later.

  1. 01

    Brief and site

    The ground, the boundary, the orientation and the brief are recorded together, so later decisions can be traced back to a condition rather than a preference.

  2. 02

    Concept design

    Options are tested in plan and section rather than in elevation. One is chosen because it answers the brief, not because it renders well.

  3. 03

    Developed design

    Structure, services and material are coordinated onto one set of drawings, and the junctions that will matter on site are drawn before they are specified.

  4. 04

    Technical design and site

    Construction information is issued and the studio stays on the project while it is built, so what was drawn is what gets made.
