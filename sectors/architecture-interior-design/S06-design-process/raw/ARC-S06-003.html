How a project takes shape

Five phases, from the first site visit to handover.

  1. Brief and site

    Survey, constraints and the brief, agreed before anything is drawn.

  2. Concept design

    Options tested in plan and section until one is worth developing.

  3. Developed design

    The chosen option resolved in detail, with structure and material coordinated.

  4. Technical design

    Construction information: junctions drawn, specification written to match.

  5. Site and handover

    The studio stays with the project while it is built, and reviews what is made.
