Design process

You always know which stage you are in

Each stage is agreed before the next begins, and each one ends in drawings you can hold on to. The last step on this list is the one that starts it.

  1. 01

    First conversation

    A short discussion of the space, the intent and what the project has to resolve. No drawings yet, and nothing to prepare.

  2. 02

    Site and brief review

    We look at the site, the existing documents and the constraints, then set out in writing what the scope covers.

  3. 03

    Proposal and appointment

    A written outline of stages, deliverables and terms. You decide how far to take it before anything is committed.

  4. 04

    Design stages begin

    Concept, developed and technical design follow in order, each closing on a set of drawings that is signed off.

  5. 05

    Start at stage one

    Everything above begins with a conversation. Tell us about the space and we will take it from there.
