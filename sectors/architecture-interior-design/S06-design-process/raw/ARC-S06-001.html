Design process

How a project moves from brief to building

Four stages, each ending in something you can look at and sign off. Work does not start on the next stage until the last one has been agreed.

  1. Stage 01

    Brief and site

    We survey what is there, agree what the project has to do, and set out the constraints in writing.

    Ends with
    Agreed brief and site notes
  2. Stage 02

    Concept design

    Plan, section and massing are tested against the brief until one option is worth developing.

    Ends with
    Concept drawings and a model
  3. Stage 03

    Developed design

    The chosen option is resolved in detail, with materials, structure and services coordinated.

    Ends with
    Developed drawing set
  4. Stage 04

    Technical design and site

    Construction information is issued and we stay involved while the work is built.

    Ends with
    Handover set
