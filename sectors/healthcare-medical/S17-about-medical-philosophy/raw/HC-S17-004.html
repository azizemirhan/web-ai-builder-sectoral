Five rules this clinic works by, what each one means on the day, and what each one costs us.

Each one is checkable on the day. If one is not true, say so at the desk.

THE TWO CHAIRS · 4:3
  • You are told who, before you book.

    Not a member of the team. A role, and the same role on the day.

    Costs us: We cannot quietly move you to whoever is free.

  • Nothing happens without your yes.

    Including the examination. You can stop it at any point, and nobody argues.

    Costs us: Some visits end without the thing we expected to do.

  • A test only if it changes what we do.

    We own machines we do not use on you, and say why.

    Costs us: We are sometimes asked why we did not scan.

  • What was found goes to you in writing.

    In plain words, in your hand before you leave, not posted later.

    Costs us: Every visit takes longer than it would without it.

  • If this is not the place, you are told.

    On the day, with the reason and a referral.

    Costs us: We turn work away most weeks.

Words you will not find here: compassionate, patient-centred, holistic, state-of-the-art. Not one of them can be checked on the day, and a rule you cannot check is not a rule.
