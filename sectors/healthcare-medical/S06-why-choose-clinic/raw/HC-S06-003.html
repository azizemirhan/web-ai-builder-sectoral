Why here

Reasons you can check on the first visit, not reasons we would like you to believe.

Five things we do that you can see for yourself on the day, each with how to check it.

THE EXAMINATION ROOM · 4:3
  1. We doYou are told who you will see before you book.

    By role, and if they are away, you are told.

    You check

    Ask when you book.

  2. We doThe first visit is an examination and a conversation.

    Nothing is started on the day unless you ask.

    You check

    You leave with a plan, not a bill for treatment.

  3. We doWhat was found goes to you in writing.

    In plain words, not a copy of the notes.

    You check

    It is in your hand before you leave.

  4. We doA scan or a test only if it changes what we do.

    You are told why, either way.

    You check

    Ask what the result would change.

  5. We doIf this is not the place, you are told, and referred.

    With the reason and the letter.

    You check

    Ask on the day if this is the right place for it.

What is not on this page: a figure, a rating, an award, or the word best. You could not check any of them.
