Why here

Reasons you can check on the first visit, not reasons we would like you to believe.

Five things we do that you can see for yourself on the day, each with how to check it.

  • THE DESK · 4:3
    You are told who you will see before you book.

    By role, and if they are away, you are told. Check: ask when you book.

  • THE EXAMINATION ROOM · 4:3
    The first visit is an examination and a conversation.

    Nothing is started on the day unless you ask. Check: you leave with a plan, not a bill for treatment.

  • THE DOCTOR, WRITING · 4:3
    What was found goes to you in writing.

    In plain words, not a copy of the notes. Check: it is in your hand before you leave.

  • THE SCANNER ROOM · 4:3
    A scan or a test only if it changes what we do.

    You are told why, either way. Check: ask what the result would change.

  • THE DOCTOR, ON THE PHONE · 4:3
    If this is not the place, you are told, and referred.

    With the reason and the letter. Check: ask on the day if this is the right place for it.

What is not on this page: a figure, a rating, an award, or the word best. You could not check any of them.
