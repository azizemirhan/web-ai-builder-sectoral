To take away

Nothing here replaces being seen. These are the things you leave with.

Five of them, what each one is for, and who writes it.

  1. One

    The letter

    What was found and what happens next, in plain words.

    Written by the person who saw you, before you leave. Usually a page.

  2. Two

    The plan

    What to do at home, and for how long.

    Written with you in the room. Say if it will not fit your week.

  3. Three

    A note for someone else

    A short version you can show whoever you will have to explain this to.

    Written only if you ask for it, and only about what was found.

  4. Four

    Where to read about the area

    What is usual for skin, joints or the heart. Not about you.

    Chosen by the clinic and listed by name, so you can see who wrote it.

    Reserved — the reading the clinic points to
  5. Five

    Who to ring, and when

    One line, so it is never a search.

    On every letter, with the hours it applies to.

Not published here: a diagnosis, a dose, a symptom checker, or anything that could be read as advice about you. If it could be mistaken for that, it does not go on the page.
