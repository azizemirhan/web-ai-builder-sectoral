In their words

Nothing here is written by us. Until a visitor says yes, the space stays empty.

Three places for what people said after a visit, and the three things we ask before any of it is printed.

  1. It is from a real visit here.

    Not a form, not a survey, not a review site.

  2. It is in the person’s own words.

    Cut only for length, never for tone.

  3. They said yes, and can say no later.

    Then it comes down, the same day.
