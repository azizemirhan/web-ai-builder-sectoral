Questions

The questions people ask at the desk, and the three we cannot answer on a page.

Six answered here, in the words they are asked in. Three that have to be about you, and are not answered anywhere but the room.

  • How soon can I be seen?

    Usually within a week. You are told the date when you book, not promised one before.

  • Who will I see?

    You are told by role before you book. If they are away, you are told that too.

  • What happens at the first visit?

    A conversation, then an examination if you agree. You leave with what was found, in writing.

  • Do I need a referral?

    No. Bring one if you have it, so nothing is asked twice.

  • Can somebody come with me?

    Yes, to any part of it. There is a seat for them in the room.

  • What does it cost?

    The first-visit fee is on the letter you get when you book.

THE DESK · 4:3

Not answered here

  • What is wrong with me?

    Not from a page. That is what the visit is.

  • Is this serious?

    Nobody can say without seeing you. If it cannot wait, use the emergency service.

  • What should I take?

    A dose depends on you and on what was found. It is not written here.
