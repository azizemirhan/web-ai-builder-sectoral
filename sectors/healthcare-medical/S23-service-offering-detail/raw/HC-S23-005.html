Skin: rashes, moles, and wounds that will not close

One area, written out in full, so you can decide whether it is the one you need.

What this covers

  • A rash that will not settle.
  • A mole or a spot that has changed.
  • A wound that will not close.
  • Skin that itches or burns without a reason you can name.

How the visit runs

  1. OneYou say what changed, and when.
  2. TwoSomebody looks, in daylight, and asks.
  3. ThreeYou are told what it looks like, what it is not, and what happens next, in writing.

What it does not cover

Cosmetic work, and any diagnosis made before somebody has looked at the skin.

Bleeding that will not stop, or a spreading rash with a fever, is not seen here. Use the emergency service. If it cannot wait

Who it is for

Anyone whose skin has changed and who has no answer yet. If the skin belongs to a child, read the children's area first. Children
