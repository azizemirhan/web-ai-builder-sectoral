Booking

Booking is a short conversation about what is wrong, not a form about who you are.

Four questions, then you are told who you will see, when, and what the first visit involves.

  1. What is wrong, in your own words.
  2. How long it has been going on, and whether it is getting worse.
  3. Whether you would like to see a particular person.
  4. Any dates that do not work for you.
  • Ring

    You speak to reception. They ask the four questions and offer a time while you are on the line.

  • Online

    The same four questions, and you are rung back, usually the same working day.

    Answer the four questions
  • At the desk

    Walk in and ask. If a first appointment can be made there and then, it is.

What you get

A time, who you will see by role, and what the first visit involves, in writing. First appointments are usually within a week.

Emergencies are not booked.
