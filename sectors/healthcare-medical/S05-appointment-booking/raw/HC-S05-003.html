Booking

Booking is a short conversation about what is wrong, not a form about who you are.

Four questions, then you are told who you will see, when, and what the first visit involves. The questions are the same whichever way you come.

  • Ring

    How
    You speak to reception.
    What happens
    They ask the four questions and offer a time while you are on the line.
    When you hear
    On the call.

    The quickest of the three.

  • Online

    How
    You answer the same four questions in writing.
    What happens
    Reception reads them and rings you back with a time.
    When you hear
    Usually the same working day.
    Answer the four questions

  • At the desk

    How
    Walk in and ask.
    What happens
    If a first appointment can be made there and then, it is.
    When you hear
    Before you leave.

    Useful if you are already nearby.

The four questions

  1. What is wrong, in your own words.
  2. How long it has been going on, and whether it is getting worse.
  3. Whether you would like to see a particular person.
  4. Any dates that do not work for you.

What you get

A time, who you will see by role, and what the first visit involves, in writing. First appointments are usually within a week.
