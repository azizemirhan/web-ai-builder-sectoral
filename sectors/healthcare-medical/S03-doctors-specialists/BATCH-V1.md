## The Governing Idea

> **You are not choosing a CV. You are choosing who will be in the room.**

The sector's people page is a wall of headshots with qualifications under each. None of it answers
the question a worried person actually has, which is what the person will be like when the door
closes. So every entry says who they see and how they work — *asks first, examines second; talks
to the child, not over them; often the first person you see* — and the batch refuses the
credential list outright: **no list of qualifications here — they are real and checkable, and they
do not tell you what someone is like in the room.** The registrations link goes to `#` because
`S11` owns them and is not yet authored.

Five roles in the `S02` vocabulary: the doctor (adults — skin, joints, heart), the children's
doctor, the nurse, the physiotherapist, the women's health doctor. **No name, qualification,
registration, membership, award or years of experience anywhere in the batch**; every entry would
be invented and none of it answers the question.

**The one booking promise:** *ask for them by role when you book — if they are away, you are told,
not quietly given somebody else.* A checkable practice, in all five.

## Media

Twelve reserved areas: five portraits at work in `001` and `005`, the handover in `002`, the doctor
and the nurse in `003`, the doctor in `004`. The contract's second honest subject — **a person at
work, by role** — and never a line-up, folded arms or a stock model. `002` refuses the headshot wall
by refusing headshots: one frame of the team working together, from the doorway. `005` turns the
forbidden device into its opposite — tall, dark, working portraits the visitor walks along like the
clinic's corridor, nobody looking at the camera.
