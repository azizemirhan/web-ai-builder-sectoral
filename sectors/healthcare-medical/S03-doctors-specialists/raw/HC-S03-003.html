Who you will see

You are not choosing a CV. You are choosing who will be in the room.

Five people, by role: who they see, the first visit, and what they are like.

THE DOCTOR · 3:4
The doctor
Sees
Adults, for skin, joints and heart.
First visit
A conversation, then an examination.
In the room
Asks first, examines second, writes down what they found.
The children’s doctor
Sees
Children, with a parent in the room.
First visit
Mostly talking, with the child.
In the room
Slow, and talks to the child rather than over them.
The nurse
Sees
Checks, screening, samples, dressings.
First visit
Often the first person you meet.
In the room
Tells you what comes next before it happens.
The physiotherapist
Sees
Joints, bones and muscles.
First visit
An examination, hands-on.
In the room
You leave with a plan you can do at home.
The women’s health doctor
Sees
Cycles, contraception, pregnancy, the menopause.
First visit
A conversation first.
In the room
Nothing else happens unless you agree.
THE NURSE · 21:9
Ask for them by role when you book.

If they are away, you are told, not quietly given somebody else.
