Who you will see

You are not choosing a CV. You are choosing who will be in the room.

Five people, by role, and what each is like to see. Slide along; ask for any of them when you book.

  • THE DOCTOR · 4:5
    The doctor

    Adults, for skin, joints and heart.

    Asks first, examines second, and writes down what they found.

  • THE CHILDREN’S DOCTOR · 4:5
    The children’s doctor

    Children, with a parent in the room.

    Slow, and talks to the child rather than over them.

  • THE NURSE · 4:5
    The nurse

    Checks, screening, samples, dressings.

    Often the first person you see, and the one who tells you what comes next.

  • THE PHYSIOTHERAPIST · 4:5
    The physiotherapist

    Joints, bones and muscles.

    Hands-on, and you leave with a plan you can do at home.

  • THE WOMEN’S HEALTH DOCTOR · 4:5
    The women’s health doctor

    Cycles, contraception, pregnancy, the menopause.

    A conversation first, and nothing else unless you agree.

Ask for them by role when you book.

If they are away, you are told, not quietly given somebody else.

No list of qualifications here.

They are real and checkable, and they do not tell you what someone is like in the room. Registrations and accreditations
