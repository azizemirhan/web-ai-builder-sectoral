Who you will see

You are not choosing a CV. You are choosing who will be in the room.

Five people, by role, and what each is like to see. Ask for any of them when you book.

THE HANDOVER · 4:5
  • The doctor

    Adults, for skin, joints and heart. Asks first, examines second.

  • The children’s doctor

    Children, with a parent in the room. Talks to the child, not over them.

  • The nurse

    Checks, screening, samples, dressings. Often the first person you see.

  • The physiotherapist

    Joints, bones and muscles. Hands-on, and a plan to take home.

  • The women’s health doctor

    Cycles, contraception, pregnancy, the menopause. A conversation first; nothing else unless you agree.

Ask for them by role when you book.

If they are away, you are told, not quietly given somebody else.
