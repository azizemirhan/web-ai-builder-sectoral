If it cannot wait

If it cannot wait, this is not the page. Here is where to go instead.

Three routes, in the order to try them, and the one thing to have ready before you speak to anybody.

RECEPTION, ON THE PHONE · 4:3
  1. The route
    What it looks like
    Where to go
  2. One

    Now

    Chest pain, severe bleeding, difficulty breathing, someone who will not wake.

    The emergency service. Do not ring us first: ringing us costs you minutes.

  3. Two

    Today, but not the emergency service

    A wound, a temperature that will not settle, pain that has changed quickly.

    The out-of-hours or urgent-care service for where you are.

    Reserved — the out-of-hours service for this area
  4. Three

    It can wait until we are open

    The same symptom for days or weeks, a repeat, a result you are waiting for.

    Ring the desk. If there is a gap today, you are told on the call.

Have this ready: where you are, what happened and when it started, and anything already taken. Say it in that order; it is the order you will be asked.

We do not triage on this page. Nothing here tells you which of the three you are in. That judgement is yours, or the emergency service’s, and it is not one a website should make for you.
