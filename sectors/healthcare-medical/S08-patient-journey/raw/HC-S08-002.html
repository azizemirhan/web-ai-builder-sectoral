The first visit

What happens, in order, what you are asked at each step, and what you can do at each step.

Six things, from the call to the letter. None of them is a surprise.

  1. RECEPTION, ON THE PHONE · 4:3
    The callFour questions about what is wrong.

    You are told who and when.

    You can ask for a particular person.

  2. THE DESK · 4:3
    The deskOnly now do we ask who you are.

    Name and date of birth.

    You can bring someone with you.

  3. THE TWO CHAIRS · 4:3
    The conversationWhat is wrong, in your words.

    To the person you were told about. Usually half an hour.

    You can ask for it said again.

  4. THE COUCH · 4:3
    The examinationTold before it starts.

    Nothing without your yes.

    You can ask to stop, at any point.

  5. THE SAMPLE ROOM · 4:3
    Tests, if anyOnly if the result would change what we do.

    Results usually within a few days.

    You can ask what the result would change.

  6. THE LETTER · 4:3
    The letterWhat was found and what happens next.

    In plain words, in your hand before you leave.

    You can ask for a copy for someone else.

At any point: if this is not the place for it, you are told, and referred. Emergencies go to the emergency service.
