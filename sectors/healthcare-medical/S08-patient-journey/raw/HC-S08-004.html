The first visit

What happens, in order, what you are asked at each step, and what you can do at each step.

Six things, from the call to the letter. None of them is a surprise.

TWO SEATS · 4:3
Nothing to prepare

Bring what is wrong and, if you like, someone with you.

No forms before you arrive, no records to find. The six steps are the whole day.

Book a first appointment
  1. The call

    Four questions about what is wrong. You are told who and when.

    You can: ask for a particular person.

  2. The desk

    Only now do we ask who you are: name and date of birth.

    You can: bring someone with you.

  3. The conversation

    What is wrong, in your words, to the person you were told about. Usually half an hour.

    You can: ask for it said again.

  4. The examination

    Told before it starts. Nothing without your yes.

    You can: ask to stop, at any point.

  5. Tests, if any

    Only if the result would change what we do. Results usually within a few days.

    You can: ask what the result would change.

  6. The letter

    What was found and what happens next, in plain words, in your hand before you leave.

    You can: ask for a copy for someone else.

At any point: if this is not the place for it, you are told, and referred. Emergencies go to the emergency service.
