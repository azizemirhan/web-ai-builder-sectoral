What we see

Start from what is wrong, not from a list of departments.

Six areas, each written from your side of the desk. If yours is not here, you are told where to go instead.

  • THE COUCH · 4:3
    Skin

    Rashes, moles, spots that have changed, wounds that will not close.

    A look, a conversation, sometimes a sample.

  • THE PHYSIOTHERAPY ROOM · 4:3
    Joints, bones and muscles

    Pain that stays, a joint that gives, something that moves less than it did.

    An examination and, if needed, a scan.

  • THE MONITOR · 4:3
    Heart and circulation

    Breathlessness, a racing pulse, swelling, blood pressure you were told about.

    A listen, a trace, and a plan.

  • THE ROOM · 4:3
    Women’s health

    Cycles, contraception, pregnancy questions, the menopause.

    A conversation first, then what you agree to.

  • THE CHILDREN’S CORNER · 4:3
    Children

    A child who is not themselves, a rash, a temperature that will not settle.

    A doctor who sees children, with you there.

  • THE NURSE’S ROOM · 4:3
    Checks and screening

    Nothing wrong yet, and you want to know.

    Which checks make sense for you.

Emergencies are not seen here. Use the emergency service. Anything else we do not see, you are told where to go instead.
