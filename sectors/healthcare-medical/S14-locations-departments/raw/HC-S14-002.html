The building

One building, described so you can find your way in it: the door, the desk, the rooms, by what happens in them.

Six places, in the order you meet them, with the one practical thing to know at each.

THE DOOR · 3:4
  1. The door

    One door from the street, marked. It opens into the desk, not a corridor.

    Step-free from the pavement; a buzzer if you need it opened.

  2. The desk

    Where you say your name and are asked who you are: the only place that happens.

    Say if you cannot stand long; you can sit and be called.

  3. The waiting room

    Chairs, water, a window. You are called by name, by the person who will see you.

    Bring someone if you like; there is a seat for them.

  4. The consulting rooms

    Six of them, off one corridor. Two chairs, a couch, a desk. The conversation and the examination happen here.

    The door is closed while you are in it, and nobody comes in without knocking.

  5. The treatment room

    Where samples are taken and the trace is done. The nurse’s room.

    You are walked here; you do not have to find it.

  6. The way out

    Back past the desk, with your letter. The desk is where anything is paid and the next visit is booked.

    Ask for a taxi to be called if you need one.

Where it is: the street, how to get here and the plan of the ground floor are on the contact page, with the door photographed from the pavement.
