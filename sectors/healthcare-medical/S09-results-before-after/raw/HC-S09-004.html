The only before-and-after that is honest is your own.

What you have before the first visit, what you have after it, and the two records that are compared at the follow-up.

Before the first visit

  • A symptom, in your own words.
  • A worry about what it might be.
  • Questions you have not asked yet.
  • No idea who you should see.

After it

  • A name for it, or an honest not yet.
  • What was found, in writing.
  • A plan, a referral, or nothing needed.
  • Who you saw, by role.
The distance

Everything under before is where you are now. Everything under after is one first visit away.
