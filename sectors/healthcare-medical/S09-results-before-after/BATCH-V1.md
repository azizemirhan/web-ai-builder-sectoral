## The Governing Idea

> **The only before-and-after that is honest is your own.**

The contract is explicit: *a results role is composed as a comparison structure with reserved
areas and no outcome text.* The sector's version is a grid of other people's photographs and
figures presented as proof; in this sector they are not evidence about the visitor and may not
be shown. This batch keeps the **before / after** structure and fills it with the only comparison
that can honestly be made in advance:

| Before the first visit | After it |
| --- | --- |
| A symptom, in your own words. | A name for it, or an honest *not yet*. |
| A worry about what it might be. | What was found, in writing. |
| Questions you have not asked yet. | A plan, a referral, or nothing needed. |
| No idea who you should see. | Who you saw, by role. |

**Structure authored; evidence reserved.** The two reserved areas in every anchored study are the
visitor's own two records — *filled in from your own visits, compared with you at the follow-up,
and shown to nobody else.* **Not shown here:** other people's photographs or figures. Every action
links to the same-variant booking study.

## Density

Standard band throughout, 133–160. The role is short by nature once outcome text is removed;

## Media

Eight reserved areas across four studies, none of them a photograph: each is a pair of empty
records belonging to the visitor. `002` is type — the paired lines are the comparison — and says
where the records live in its foot.
