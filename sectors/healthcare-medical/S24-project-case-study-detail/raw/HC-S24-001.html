Why this page has no case

Clinical case material identifies people. A date, a town, an age and a photograph are enough between them. So this page documents the route and leaves the person out.

The route

  1. OneThe first appointmentWhat changed, and when. Written down as you said it.
  2. TwoThe lookIn daylight, with a second opinion in the room if you want one.
  3. ThreeThe decisionWhat it looks like, what it is not, and what is still being ruled out.
  4. FourThe letterAll of the above, in writing, to you and to whoever asked.

What is recorded

The stage, the day it happened, the decision, and who made it, by role.

What is not

No name, no photograph, no age, no town. Nothing that would let this page be read backwards to a person.

What this cannot show you

An outcome, a figure, a before and after, or a quote from somebody who went through it. None of those can be shown without showing the person.
