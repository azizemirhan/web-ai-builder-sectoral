Registered and inspected

A register you can look up, not a wall of badges.

Three things about this clinic that are checked by somebody other than us, and how you check them yourself.

Ask to see it

The register entry, the inspection report, the service log. Ask at the desk. Nobody will ask why.

If we cannot show you one of the three on the day, that is worth knowing too.
