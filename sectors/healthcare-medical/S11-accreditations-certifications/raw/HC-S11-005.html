Registered and inspected

A register you can look up, not a wall of badges.

Three things about this clinic that are checked by somebody other than us, and how you check them yourself.

  • Registered.

    The people

    Every clinician here is on a public register, by number, and stays on it only while they meet its conditions.

    Look it up: the register and the number are on the letter you take home, and at the desk.

    Reserved — Register name and number

  • Inspected.

    The building

    By the regulator, on its schedule, not ours. The last report says what was found, and what was asked of us.

    Look it up: the report is at the desk, and on the regulator’s own site.

    Reserved — Date of last inspection

  • Serviced.

    The equipment

    On a schedule, and the log is kept. Anything overdue is not used until it is done.

    Look it up: ask to see the log for the machine used on you.

    Reserved — Service schedule

Not shown here: logos of bodies we are not members of. Awards. Any seal we could not explain to you at the desk.
