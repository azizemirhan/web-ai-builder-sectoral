Paying for it

The price of the first visit is told to you before you book, in writing, and nothing is added on the day without your yes.

Three ways it can be paid for, what is never charged for, and what to do if you cannot pay.

  • Yourself
    You need

    Nothing in advance. The fee is on the letter you get when you book.

    What happens

    You pay at the desk after the visit, for what was agreed, and get a receipt that says what for.

    ReservedFirst-visit fee
  • Through an insurer
    You need

    Your policy details and, if your insurer asks for one, an authorisation before the visit.

    What happens

    We tell you before you book whether we work with them, and what they usually do not cover.

    ReservedThe insurers we work with
  • Through a referral
    You need

    The referral letter from whoever sent you.

    What happens

    We tell you before you book who pays, and whether anything falls to you.

    ReservedReferral arrangements
Never charged for
  • Being told this is not the place for it.
  • A referral letter.
  • Asking a question, before or after.
If you cannot pay

Say so at the desk. There is a conversation to be had, not a form.
