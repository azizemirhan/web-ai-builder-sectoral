What we use

The machine is named, what it is for is said, and when it is not used is said too.

Six things in the building, each with the question it answers and the question it cannot. The second one first.

  • Not for bone.

    Ultrasound. Soft tissue and organs, without radiation, on the day.

  • Only if the picture would change what we do.

    X-ray. Bones and chests, read the same day.

  • Rhythm, not blockages.

    Heart trace. The heart’s rhythm, ten minutes on the couch.

  • Only the tests the visit calls for.

    Blood tests. The sample taken here; results back, usually in a few days.

  • Instead of one nervous reading in a room.

    Pressure monitor to take home. A day and a night of readings, worn under a sleeve.

  • A look, not a diagnosis.

    Skin scope. A close look at a mole, with a photograph kept for next time.

Nothing here is new for the sake of it. If a test would not change what we do, it is not done, whatever machine we own.
