What we use

The machine is named, what it is for is said, and when it is not used is said too.

Six things in the building, each with the question it answers and the question it cannot.

  • THE ULTRASOUND ROOM · 4:3

    Ultrasound

    Answers
    Soft tissue and organs, without radiation, on the day.
    Not for
    Bone.
  • THE X-RAY ROOM · 4:3

    X-ray

    Answers
    Bones and chests, read the same day.
    Not for
    A picture that would not change what we do.
  • THE TRACE MACHINE · 4:3

    Heart trace

    Answers
    The heart’s rhythm, ten minutes on the couch.
    Not for
    Blockages.
  • THE SAMPLE ROOM · 4:3

    Blood tests

    Answers
    The sample taken here; results back, usually in a few days.
    Not for
    A panel of everything.
  • THE TAKE-HOME MONITOR · 4:3

    Pressure monitor to take home

    Answers
    A day and a night of readings, worn under a sleeve.
    Not for
    Replacing the conversation about why.
  • THE SKIN SCOPE · 4:3

    Skin scope

    Answers
    A close look at a mole, with a photograph kept for next time.
    Not for
    A diagnosis.
The rule

Nothing here is new for the sake of it. If a test would not change what we do, it is not done, whatever machine we own.

Not held here

The scans a hospital does. Arranged for you, and you are told why.
