Not treated here

Anything that needs surgery, cancer treatment or a hospital. You are referred, and told why. Emergencies go to the emergency service.

  • Skin

    Eczema, acne, psoriasis, moles and skin checks, infections.

    A look and a conversation; a sample if needed.

  • Joints, bones and muscles

    Back and neck pain, arthritis, sports injuries, sprains.

    An examination, and a scan only if it changes what we do.

  • Heart and circulation

    High blood pressure, palpitations, cholesterol, swelling.

    A listen, a trace, a reading, and a plan you take home.

  • Women’s health

    Periods and cycles, contraception, the menopause, pregnancy questions.

    A conversation first; nothing else unless you agree.

  • Children

    Rashes, fevers, asthma, allergies, growth and feeding.

    A doctor who sees children, with you in the room.

  • Checks and screening

    Diabetes risk, cholesterol, blood pressure, well-person checks.

    Which checks make sense for you, and which do not.
