Finding us, reaching us

Where we are, how to get here, and which way of reaching us is for what.

Four routes, what each is for and what comes back. Everything that could be got wrong is left blank until it is checked.

  • The desk, in person

    Anything at all, including what you would rather not write down.

    Answered while you are standing there.

  • The telephone

    Booking, changing a time, a question about a letter.

    Answered by reception, not a menu.

    Reserved — the number, and the hours it is answered
  • In writing

    Anything that is not urgent and can be read later.

    Read the next working day, and answered the same way.

    Reserved — the enquiry address
  • None of these

    Anything that cannot wait.

    The emergency service. Do not use this page for it.
